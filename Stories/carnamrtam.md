### Word of appreciation

After getting encouraging response from the readers of 'Carnamrtam', rendering it into English was my next priority, as repeated requests were made by Margi brothers and sisters for its English version. My weak vision would not allow me to venture into it, so I thought of Shri Anil Sharma, Associate Professor at Government S.P.M.R College of Commerce Jammu, who is personally known to me since his childhood. I know that Ananda Marga and spirituality are in his blood and if he takes up the job, it would be the best choice. When I approached him, he readily agreed and started doing it alongside his official and family duties.

When I read the English version after its completion I found it to be the best translation, even beyond my expectations: It maintained lucidity and Spirit of the original work. Shri Anil's father is an Acarya in Ananda Marga, since about fifty years. Ac. Maharaj Krishan Sharma and his twin brother Shri Om Prakash Sharma are like my elder brothers. I respect them and they love me very much. All the members of both the families are like my own.

I highly appreciate Shri Anil's work and wish him a blissful life.

# Foreword

It is only by the Divine Grace of Baba that I have been able to pen down my experiences. My vision has been impaired to such an extent for the last fifty five years that I am barely able to read the newspaper and occasionally dare read the Marga literature. Despite agonizing pain in the eyes, my life boat is afloat by the sheer grace of Baba and hardly have I breathed a word about my intense pain to anyone. I have been trying my utmost to perform my work with dedication even in my old age (running 79 in 2013) and most of my time is consumed in travelling, thus leaving little time for writing.

About 30-35 years ago, I used to respond with silence at the repeated suggestion of Margiis to pen down my experiences: Perhaps, I thought it was not possible for me. I used to narrate the inspirational stories of Baba but was helpless to put these in black and white.

With declining age, my travelling has been reduced. But one thing I am blessed with is that the entire planning of my life is done by Baba, who guides all my movements.

On the strong insistence of some young Margiis of Delhi, I was compelled to dictate my experiences, (which I did not really enjoy), because while dictating, the small details are lost. I began this pleasant ordeal of inking my experiences, resting my eyes repeatedly and have taken several months to complete the task.

Getting the manuscript typed and edited was an equally arduous task which was accomplished with the help of my well wishers.

There is not an iota of exaggeration in my writings and it is an anthology of all what I have done, heard, perceived, experienced and practised. Remembering Him (Baba) all the time, I have been scripting my reminiscences of Him for His devotees.

I am not a literateur, hence apologize for the flaws in writing at the very outset as I have been a student of science.

I have spent plenty of time in the divine company of Baba—the object of my adoration. Many others like me were fortunate to enjoy His proximity, while some were more fortunate to have spent more time with Baba and they too have their own record of experiences but have never seriously thought of compiling them.

From 1985 to 1990, Baba, indifferent to His physical health plunged Himself into the gigantic task of accelerating the momentum of the Mission, because of the paucity of time and many like me were deprived of His physical proximity. He was more accessible to His Personal Assistant and Secretaries while I yearned for His physical proximity and would try to console myself with the thoughts that organizational matters were more important than my wishes.

I could only see Him from a distance and I was yearning for a closer proximity. One day, Baba was coming out of the room after reporting while I was eagerly awaiting His glimpse outside, He stopped abruptly near me and held my hands, looked at me and patted me on my cheeks which filled every pore of my being with inexplicable joy. At that moment, I almost lost my consciousness and sank deep into the ocean of Divine Bliss. His charming eyes, His loving touch, His sublime words, His loquacious silence had an indelible effect on me. He is all my Father, Friend and my Lord.

From His lips would flow words of limitless knowledge. All the time, He would be masterly dealing with History, Geography Literature, Philosophy and sundry other subjects in depth. He would say “Attain Knowledge but do more Action—the blending of Knowledge and Action would arouse devotion which will take you to salvation.”

He is not with us physically but who can say that He does not exist. His Omnipresence is always felt in every moment of my life and in the activities of the organization. I, alongwith my fellow workers, have experienced His direct grace all the time.

Many devotees have felt His physical presence, be it India, America, Manila or Africa. Never for a moment, have I felt Him away from me.

The truth is that Parampurusha is with us individually and collectively and there is no question of His not being with us.

I have not been able to express all my experiences but that does not make what I have written less valuable and in doing so, I am greatly indebted to Gautama and Gopesh and their wives for their invaluable help. I can't forget my most affectionate Ananda Verma who took pains in editing the entire work, so arduously.

— Ác Sujit Kumar

### CARNAMRTAM
**(The Nectar of thy Lotus Feet)**

The Nectar of Thy Holy Feet has
Entirely distinct taste, unlike
The wine, which is unsavoury.
A tiny drop of that Elixir
Fills one with divine inebriation
I cry and yearn for it!
I pine for it: but it eludes me
Your benevolent Grace alone
Can bless me with that!
The Sun of my life is about to set
Death awaits at the close of day
I writhe in the blissful agony
Of Thy indelible sweet memories
In the enveloping clouds
‘Hope’ lies shattered
Can Thou be so unkind
To deny the momentary flash
Of that Supreme Beatitude
Which You alone can shower
On my ‘Inner-eye’
I will be eternally blessed.
I solicit Thy Grace supernal
To put life into the withering petals
Of ‘Thousand-petaled Lotus, once white and tender
And restore their pristine splendour
I pine for that tiny drop of Elixir!

— **Ác Sujit Kumar**

# I MEET MY SPIRITUAL MASTER

From my early years, I evinced great interest in the science of spirituality; this innate urge was spontaneous and was independent of any external factor and would find expression in my behaviour, I would oppose my mother’s worship of numerous gods and goddesses, her fasts and worship of the Moon and the Sun. From my childhood I would tell her to pursue her beliefs, but not to ask me to follow her beliefs. Sometimes, she would scold me and try to make me understand but that would not shake my firm conviction. When I grew up, I developed a strong aversion to Casteism and Dowry etc. and clearly told my parents not to make any commitment regarding my marriage as they would regret it later. Afterwards, when I joined Ananda Marga and understood the spiritual and social philosophy of Ananda Marga, I could perceive that my convictions were rational and were in consonance with tenets of spirituality. I was following Dharma, while my parents were pursuing Mazhab (religion). They were blindly clinging to the social evils and dogmas without an iota of logic while I was unconsciously and unknowingly opposing them. It does appear that I was ignorantly and innocently acting as per the directions of Baba and not being able to understand the origin of these thoughts at that point of time.

Of course, I had been interested in Yoga and would practice Yogic Exercises and Prańáyáma from my study of books at the tender age of four or five. I would hold my breath for minutes and by the time I was in my teens, I would go to

some secluded place and practice concentration of mind and close my eyes for several hours and try to contemplate on the mysteries of creation without reaching any conclusion, yet I developed the ability for deep contemplation. I got a chance to narrate my experiences to Baba and He said, “Yes, I know all about it. Didn’t it happen that seeing you sitting in meditation posture, some passersby remarked that you were sitting like Buddha in meditation.” It had happened exactly like that.

From 1956-57, I started my search for Spiritual Master (Guru). Those days, I had hired a room and my studies were complete by July 1957, even then I preferred to live alone in the room. Whatever money I got from my guardian, I would save some amount and spend it on my search for Spiritual Master wandering in some mountainous terrains or holy places like Vrindavan. During the course of my search in my town, I came across some ‘gurus’ but could not accept them as my convictions were quite different from what they upheld. In 1958, I went as far as Kathmandu in March (7th -8th March) searching for the Guru. I had read in the newspaper about a holy man, 150 years of age, who lived on a hillock 2-3 kms from the temple of Pashupatinath in Kathmandu. His name was Shivpuri Baba and was an elevated soul. With great difficulty, one fine morning, I managed to reach the secluded hermitage on the hillock where he lived, assisted by a young man.

I found the gate closed, when I arrived there at 7.30 a.m on Wednesday. A young boy appeared: I told him that I had come to meet the holy man (Mahatma). He told me to come on Saturday at 5.00 p.m when meeting could be possible as he does not give audience on other days of the week. I enquired about his present engagement. The boy said that he had just come out of his secluded hut and was washing his face. When I insisted that he (the boy) should tell him that I could not stay in Kathmandu till Saturday and I had come from Muzaffarpur only to meet him. The message reached the holy man and I was called inside. I found him an old man with a face glowing with fulgency. I touched his feet and introduced myself. He asked me to wait for fifteen minutes when he would be free. I busied myself by looking at the photographs in the antechamber and from the captions I came to know that he hailed from Kerala and had joined the caravan of holy men at the age of four and attained spiritual knowledge from them. When he grew up, he visited the holy places and went up to Russia where he was greeted by the King Czar, as an Indian Yogi. From there, he came to Kathmandu via Tibet and started residing in the hermitage where his disciples made arrangements for his stay. He would come out of his hermitage for half an hour in the morning and evening and his daily food intake was just one litre of milk and occasionally some bananas.

On being called, I could meet him comfortably and expressed my desire to learn Yoga meditation from him and if need be, I would stay there to learn that unique science. He told me clearly “You will find the Spiritual Guide where you are. He will initiate you into the science of Spirituality”. I felt, being a great Sadhaka, he must have peeped into my future to tell me so. Then he blessed me with words of spiritual wisdom before retiring to his place.

Hoping to find my Spiritual Master (Guru) in my own town, I returned to Muzaffarpur and continued my search. I would test all those who professed to know the science of Meditation. Casually, I asked one such person if he had any literature on Spirituality. He replied, “I will tell you an address, if you are keen on reading spiritual literature.” He guided me to a small cycle shop in Kalyani Bazaar with the sign board “Ananda Marga Information Centre”. I reached there and made enquiries and learnt that Ananda Marga is a spiritual organization and from there I got the address of an Ananda Marga Ácarya Sakal Dev, an advocate by profession and District Secretary of Ananda Marga. I went there and met the Acarya, accompanied by one inquisitive friend. It was on 14th of April 1958 that I had detailed discussion with Ácarya Sakal Dev and was greatly impressed by his talk. My friend got initiated immediately, while I took initiation on 16th of April 1958. Atleast one thing was quite clear that his was the most logical and rational interpretation of the Science of Spirituality that I had ever come across during my search. I was satisfied but had yet to ascertain truth of the prophetic words of the holyman of Kathmandu known as Shivpuri Baba that I would meet my Spiritual Master (Guru) in the town. With these doubts still lingering in my mind, I remained engrossed in my meditation learnt from my Ácarya. I performed Sadhana regularly and visited him off and on. Even my Ácarya would visit me and inspire me. I was blessed with the darshan of Shri Shri Ananda Murtii Ji, Ananda Marga Guru, by the sincere efforts of my Ácarya, at Krishnagar in the Nadia district of W. Bengal where Dharma MahaCa'kra was arranged.

I had some tangible experience during Baba’s darshan at Kolkata that though living the life of a householder, with an ordinary job (Shri Shri Anandamurtiji), He was a realized soul. I thought my experience would be enriched with time. I got engrossed in Sadhana and started relishing it. It was only when I came in close contact with Baba that I understood that He is Purshohattam Krsna, He is Shiva and He is Param Purusha. Shivpuri Baba had rightly indicated that I would meet my Master in my own town.

### MY EARLY YEARS IN ANANDA MARGA

My Ácarya Sakal Dev, advocate by profession, was a very dynamic person and a great devotee. Now, when I am writing these lines, he is no more in this world. He would always try to be in touch with me so that I performed sadhana regularly and properly and was a great source of inspiration. Incase, I could not meet him, he would call at my place.

I was extremely regular in my sadhana after my initiation and followed the procedure in letter and spirit.

In 1958, a DMC (Dharma Maha Ca’kra) was arranged at Krishna Nagar in the Nadia district of West Bengal. Many Margiis were eager to attend the DMC and I too was encouraged by my Ácarya to go there and he narrated many stories of Baba, but, I was helpless as I had no money. When he came to know of this he even proposed to bear my travelling expenses, but I did not agree. Still, he persuaded me to go there at any cost.

The Margiis had fixed the day of their departure for Krishnanagar DMC and after they left, I also thought of going there even by borrowing money. When I reached Krishna Nagar, first day of the DMC was in session over, and I immediately went to search my Ácarya with whose help I managed to enter the DMC hall. It was a good gathering and devotional songs were being sung by sadhakas while some people were dancing in a state of trance.

After sometime, Baba entered and sat on the dais and people greeted him with triumphant chants. I kept on gazing at Him without any special feelings towards Him; the reason being that Baba’s appearance was contrary to the conventional religious heads, ie without any beard and was wearing spectacles. I was having a totally different picture of a religious Guru with ochre robes and matted locks which are not there at all. Even then, I listened quietly and I had very negative feeling as the discourse was in Bangla language which at that time I didn’t understand. I then left for the place meant for our stay that too was not upto to my expectations.

Some of the sadhakas were so ecstatic that they continued to dance the whole night and I could not sleep. I was repeatedly asked to participate in the dance much to my dislike. Thus two days passed and the DMC was over and we were returning along with our Ácarya. My Ácarya told me that Baba would be staying in Kolkata for two days, hence, he was staying back expecting me also to take advantage of the occasion so that I could have close personal contact with Baba. Initially, I did not agree but my Ácarya was able to persuade me to stay back and arranged meeting with Baba during field walk.

Three persons were present there and Baba continued speaking with them and then He addressed me by my name and asked me several questions. I was extremely surprised as to how He knew things no one knew. In my heart of hearts, I started pondering over His Omniscience. The meeting inspired me to some extent, but the realization that He was the Sadguru had not dawned as yet. I returned to Muzaffarpur and continued with my usual routine. It was in December 1958 when my friend, who was my classmate, persuaded me to accompany him to Jamalpur to have Baba’s Darshan. We reached Jamalpur on 29th of December 1958 and stayed in a lodge (Dharamshala) near the station and began our search for the Ananda Marga Ashram. Then we contacted the then General Secretary Ác Pranay Kumar Chatterji at his residence and requested for having Baba’s darshan and he readily obliged and asked me and my friend to reach the place where Baba sat in the field after the walk. Both of us reached there and Baba arrived after sometime. We prostrated before Him and sat beside Him. We were the only three persons in that desolate place and perfect peace reigned. Baba was silent and in a grave mood and was gazing at the sky for sometime then He put me a question whether Microcosm could become Macrocosm. The words Microcosm and Macrocosm were quite alien to me and I replied, “I don’t know the meaning of Microcosm and Macrocosm”. Baba explained the meaning i.e Unit Mind and Cosmic mind. I admitted that I could not

answer but I could prove with the help of a mathematical formula that I had read that One=Infinity=0. Baba asked me to prove that.

With a calculation of a page or so, that I had read in Higher Mathematics and remembered, I proved One=Infinity=0. Baba patted me on the back and asked instantly, “How can you explain it philosophically?” By then the answer had already struck my mind and I promptly replied that by doing sadhana one can attain Infinity i.e Parampurusha.

Baba was extremely happy and remarked that had He been conducting Tattvika examination, He would declare me a Tattvika then and there. Then we accompanied Baba on His way back and found two/three senior Sadhakas waiting at the door of Baba’s residence and we narrated to them what had transpired during the field walk and they, in turn, informed us that a DMC at Jamalpur was being held on the occasion of the New Year and we would definitely benefit from it, and hence we decided to stay for the DMC.

The lodging was arranged in a local school and, I too, stayed there and derived great spiritual pleasure from the DMC. During my stay in the school, one sadhaka was doing Sadhana, reclining against the wall, abruptly started producing the sound of Hum’kara (roaring sound) and then his body levitated and hit the opposite wall, at least 20 feet away. I thought he must have got seriously hurt by the huge impact, but I was wonder struck to see him totally unscathed and he left the room dancing in a state of spiritual trance. Later, our Ácarya explained to us that on the awakening of Coiled Serpentine (Kula Kundalini) such things do happen.

The DMC ended and I enjoyed it because Baba’s discourse was in Hindi and I could understand it. The next day, on 2nd January, I, alongwith my friend, reached the Ashram. Baba arrived at the Ashram and after sometime, I was told by the General Secretary that I had to appear for the Tattvika test. I was extremely surprised at this, as I was a new-comer to this Organization. I was all the more astonished as I had little knowledge of the Ananda Marga Philosophy and still I was being asked to sit for the test. I resisted, but was asked to sit before Baba who put me some questions. All that I answered was totally incorrect and finally Baba said that I could not answer rightly. I came out wildly upset. I strongly criticized the General Secretary and the system of examination in which one is declared as failed without providing one any material on Philosophy. I firmly and seriously expressed my opinion about such an unreasonable system, but the General Secretary, very politely made me understand about the unique methodology of Baba and assured me that there was nothing to worry about. I was asked to come the next day and I did accordingly. Baba arrived and I was to take the test again. Baba asked me a number of questions in English and I tried to answer to the best of my ability not knowing whether these were right or wrong.

At last Baba said, “You got half mark out of hundred,” and He smilingly asked me, “Would you like to work for the Mission of Ananda Marga?” I said, “Yes Baba, why not?” At this Baba said, “All right.” He wrote on a paper, “I am giving you 29 ½ marks as grace marks, so you get ½ + 29 ½ =30. You

have passed and from now onwards you are a Tattvika”. I was delighted that I was a Tattvika and started thinking that I had given a word to Guru, hence from that day the purpose of my life would be Ananda Marga and I would not take up any job to earn my livelihood. From that time till date I have been keeping my promise with sincerity, dedication and patience. From the time I became a Tattvika, my belief in the Marga became firmer and I plunged myself into the organizational work. I started visiting my Ácarya Sakal Dev everyday and would sit with him for hours discussing Baba and His Organization. My visits to Jamalpur increased and I started devoting my time for the work of the Mission. I sincerely performed the duties of the local unit and the directions of the Central Organization as well.

My Ácarya used to reside all alone in the heart of the city at a house in Kalyani Chowk, while his family lived in a village. Many Margiis used to frequent his place as he was the District Secretary. He was very dynamic, committed and above all, a devotee, with jovial disposition, fully dedicated to the Marga. It was a matter of great pleasure to be in his company and listen to his inspiring words; that is why I would invariably enjoy his company for hours.

### THE BIRTH OF PROUT PHILOSOPHY

In the April of 1959, a DMC was held in Motihari, and I too, attended it and got a chance to render service to Baba. One day, I asked Him (Baba), “Is Ananda Marga going to confine itself, like other religious organizations, to mere religious worship and meditation or do something different?” Baba replied, “Wait for sometime and you will find it is very special, quite novel and totally different from all such organizations.” I received a letter from Jamalpur, some days after my return from Motihari DMC. It was from the General Secretary Ác Pranay Kumar in which he desired my presence during Baba’s talk at a Seminar in the month of May.

I reached Jamalpur as per the schedule of the seminar. Baba delivered talks twice a day at around 10.00 a.m in the morning and 7.00 p.m in the evening. Each discourse would last for one and a quarter hours and in all there were fourteen sadhakas fortunate to be called for the purpose—some of them being doctors, advocates, and engineers, all learned and young. We were directed to make notes to be shown to Baba and we did accordingly. Baba would make necessary corrections and thus a manuscript was prepared. In the concluding discourse on the seventh day, Baba said, “This is your Socio-Economic Theory. It is Progressive Utilization Theory. In short it is PROUT; in Hindi ‘Pragatishila Upayogi Tattva’ or PROUT.” Thus a new Socio-Politico-Economic Theory came into existence. Later, Baba would expatiate on the topics whenever He liked which resulted in the publishing of books on PROUT, like *PROUT Economy*, *PROUT in a Nutshell*, *Human Society*, etc.

Baba gave all fourteen of us a test after the seventh day’s discourse in which only seven of us could qualify. Alas! now that I am scripting my memories, none of those who attended the Discourses are alive except myself.

## IDEA AND IDEOLOGY COMES INTO EXISTENCE

A month after the PROUT Seminar and a month thereafter, Baba gave classes for these Seminars. I was not able to attend the classes conducted in the first month, but I attended all the seven classes in the second phase of the Seminar. The book that came into being from the manuscript of the discourses lasting fourteen days, was christened as IDEA AND IDEOLOGY, the fundamental philosophy of Ananda Marga, while Ananda Sutram came later.

Idea and ideology is highly abstruse philosophical treatise, but as per Baba's directions that it should be thoroughly and seriously studied, I read it again and again, contemplated on it and made notes.

## MY SEMINAR AT KRISHNANAGAR

At the end of the year 1959, I received a letter asking me to come to Jamalpur. I reached there on 25th or 26th December and met Baba. Baba directed me to conduct a Seminar at Krishnanagar in the Nadia district of West Bengal, as the arrangements were being made for the New Year Dharma Maha Cakra to be held on 1st Jan. 1960. Baba said to me, "Look, the area is poverty stricken and it is not possible to hold DMC frequently, so you meet all the Margiis especially the youth and hold Seminar prior to DMC. The message has been sent by the General Secretary about your taking the seminar. The senior Margiis there will make necessary arrangements for the Seminar." I asked Baba, "What topic should I discuss in the class?" Baba said, "You take classes on PROUT and Idea and Ideology." People were extremely happy as I started taking classes as per Baba's directions.

There were about thirty participants in my class. All listened to me with rapt attention and made genuine queries; especially one youth who later became Ác Vijayananda Avadhuta who was initiated by Ác Sachinandan Mandal, Baba's classmate during school days.

At the end of the Seminar program i.e on 31st Dec. 59 and 1st Jan. 1960, the DMC was held. Baba was accorded a grand reception on His arrival by train. Baba was taken to His place of stay in a majestic procession. I was made to sit beside Baba in the front seat. The day after the DMC, Baba returned to Kolkata and on His back, one devoted Margii, engineer by profession, narrated his tale of woe. He was doing a one-year course from All India Institute of Health and Hygiene in Kolkata.

Perhaps, it was a unique institute, which attracted Govt. doctors and engineers, with an experience of 7 to 10 years, from all over India like Jammu, Kashmir, Tamilnadu, Kerala, Bihar, Punjab and Maharasthra etc. They were offered courses in Health and Hygiene. The engineer Margii told Baba that his friends in the hostel made fun of him while he was doing meditation and created disturbance. Baba suggested to him that he should take me along and arrange a meeting with his friends.

I was already on board the train, so the engineer Margii told me about Baba's directions to stay back at Kolkata: so I did. The meeting was fixed for the next day i.e Sunday at 11.00 a.m. I reached there in time and was made to sit in the reception hall, while he went to call his friends, but all of them refused to come. He returned dejected and apprised me of the situation. I thought when Baba had sent me here, they were bound to come.

I closed my eyes and remembered Baba and asked the Margii brother to do the same and take Guru Mantra and request his friends again. This time he was able to persuade about a dozen of his friends and on entering the room, two of them addressed me in an ill mannered way, "We will put questions to you." I said, "Very well! question-answer is essential for understanding any philosophy, but let me first express my ideas and then you can make queries." They readily agreed. I thought they were all egoists but had scientific temper, so why not address them in the language of Science.

I closed my eyes and meditated on Baba, mentally seeking His permission to speak on 'Chemistry of Mind'. I spoke for about forty five minutes, in English, explaining to them that Mind comes from Matter and the movement of Matter from crudity towards subtlety increases its intra-atomic space and consequently mind grows in magnitude with spiritual ideation.

Later, I dwelt at length on the subjects like: What is Meditation? What is Science of Spirituality? How to control Mind? How to attain Bliss etc? All of them listened attentively and on being invited to ask questions, none came forward, rather, they were all praise for me. They became friendly and some of them expressed their desire to learn Meditation, but I was only a Tattavika at that time, so I put them off saying, "You give a serious thought to it and make up your mind, then I will teach Meditation."

Later, I came to know that no one disturbed the engineer Margii brother any more. By that time, my conviction that Baba is a great Guru became firmer.

I was called by Baba several times through the General Secretary's letters regarding some organizational programs: In 1961, and 1962, I was called several times to take classes on Ananda Marga Philosophy for those who would come to meet Baba for 3-4 days. Sometimes, Baba would drop in when the class was in progress and be part of the audience and occasionally would interrupt me and explain the topic in a more lucid way but He displayed an exclusive style which was worth watching. He would say, "With due permission of the trainer, may I speak something on this subject? I would instantly say, "Yes Baba, please". My classes were on the basis of Idea and Ideology and PROUT and those who attended would turn into committed sadhakas.

### MY KAPALIKA INITIATION

This dates back to 1960 when Baba often referred to Kapalika Sadhana during the field walk that sadhana could help control the instincts of Fear, Hate, Shame and add

dynamism to life. He told that Sadhana is an all-round fight against one's reactive momenta, instincts and ego and one has to emerge victorious: only then, one can attain Liberation. This sadhana is known as Viiraćár in Tantra.

I evinced a great desire to learn this novel practice. When I went to Jamalpur in March, I came to know that some sadhakas got initiated into this practice in January and February and the first one to get the Sadhana was Ác Kedar Sharma, while Ác Sakal Dev and Ac Ganga Sharan followed soon after. The test Ác Kedar Nath had to undergo before his Kapalika initiation is very interesting. It was a wintry Sunday that he had come to Jamalpur for Baba's darshan and accompanied Baba on His afternoon walk along with two or three people. The subject alluded to was Kapalika Sadhana. Kedarji insisted on learning the practice. Baba asked him, "Do you fear?" He said, "Just a little." Baba further asked, "Do you feel shame?" "Never thought about it," said Kedarji. Baba was going towards the field via Jubli Well route. He asked Kedarji, "If you go running across the pedestrian track at the station naked and come back the same way, without feeling shame, I will think of initiating you into Kapalika Sadhana."

Kedarji immediately disrobed himself and started running across the indicated track and returned to Baba after 15–20 minutes. Baba patted him and initiated him into Kapalika Sadhana on reaching the field.

One thing Baba said is worth mentioning. He said, "Do you know that when Kedar was running naked, the passersby would have obviously considered him mad and he would have invited stones from street urchins. I had cast a spell on passersby so that none could see him in the nude."

Kedarji, father of a respected Margii sister Dr. Karuna of Ranchi, is no more today. I recollect that Dr Karuna was a small girl at that time. Kedarji was an LIC Field Officer in 1963–64 and had arranged many Tattva Sabhas (spiritual meetings) which were addressed by me. Many a time, he took me to his house. He was a dynamic person and his contribution in building the Ashram at Hehal Road, Ranchi and arranging many DMCs is noteworthy.

I requested Baba several times to initiate me into Kapalika Sadhana. Baba put me off by inducing fear in my mind, but I kept on insisting. At last Baba graced me by initiating me into this esoteric practice on 16th of April 1960 at the Jamalpur field in the midst of three palm trees at about 8.30 p.m. in the evening. Two days before that, i.e. 14th of April 1960, Baba made me Acarya.

### TOTAPURI ATTAINS LIBERATION

I had witnessed Sh. Totapuri's meeting with Baba at Jamalpur, which was very strange for me and I wanted to know from Baba more about him. One evening in the April of 1960, I accompanied Baba from His house on a walk. Baba used to cross the Railway over-bridge and we accompanied Him on His evening walk towards the field. I was to the left of Baba, who was in the middle followed by some Margiis. We were accosted by a man of sentient countenance appearing

about 60 years of age, with dishevelled hair saluting Baba. Baba reciprocated the gesture by casting a glance towards him and continued to climb the stairs. My curiosity was aroused but I did not ask any question as it was a crowded place. But at the same time, I was quite inquisitive to know the identity of the stranger who saluted Baba. At this, Baba said, "Didn't you recognize him? He is Totapuri and has come to meet me." I exclaimed in surprise, "Totapuri the revered guru of Sh. Ramkrishan Paramahansa?" Baba affirmed, "Yes, the same Totapuri, whose age is about 300 years now".

By then, we reached the Tiger's grave and sat there after prostrating before Him, as usual. After sometime Baba said, "You people move a little away, as Totapuri is coming." We shifted to another grave nearby but kept gazing towards Baba, out of sheer curiosity. We could only see some person coming to Baba, who called us back after 20-25 minutes. I, being the most eager to know more about Totapuriji and seizing the opportune moment asked Baba, "Are you the Guru of Totapuri too"? Baba said, "See! He is on the verge of attaining Moksha (Salvation), hence, he had come to seek my permission and I granted his wish and guided him to the place where he would shuffle off his mortal coil. Look at the hillock to the right of another hill, there is a desolate stretch of land where I have asked him to leave his body midnight tonight." Then Baba narrated in detail that Totapuri had come to meet Him about 15 days before at Jamalpur, seeking his Salvation. Baba further said that He had found him worthy of Moksha (Salvation) just by looking at him, from head to foot but had found him lacking a bit in devotion which He (Baba) did not relish and so He taught him special technique to develop more devotion and asked him to practise it, staying around the place. That was the purpose of his visit that day. Baba said that he assessed him once again and found substantial increase in the quantum of devotion and hence, He had granted him the permission. Baba further said, "He (Totapuri) had travelled on foot for about one month from the forests of Nagaland where he was residing: He was an accomplished and realized Sadhaka of Tantra and Hathayoga and with such aged body, he rightly deserved Salvation (Moksha), so I granted him the permission."

One most significant thing Baba revealed to me, saying, "Do you know that when Parampurusha (Mahapurusha) comes in human form and if some sadhaka attains the sublime stance of Salvation; he has to physically seek permission of Parampurusha as per the spiritual canons set by Lord Shiva, but I have slightly modified it, keeping in view that Totapuri being so old, had to travel a long distance on foot, which touched me and the change I have affected is that if some sadhaka willingly wants to come physically, it is his sweet will or else the permission can be sought mentally, too."

I made a request to Baba that we might be allowed to visit the place the next morning where he would leave his body and perform his last funeral rites, but Baba did not concede our request saying that he possessed great occult powers and would evaporate the remains of his body while learning it. I have heard from Ácarya Nagina about many such

realized souls, who like Totapuriji would visit Baba and feel blessed by His darshan.

### STRANGE BUT TRUE STORY OF THE POWER OF SAMSKARA

Sh. Shashi Ranjanji, a great devotee of Baba, hailing from Muzaffarpur was a staunch Margii alongwith his family; Dharma Cakra was also held at his place. He was a Congress MP from 1960-70. I was very much present when Baba persuaded him and motivated him to become a Member of Parliament. His impressive personality, simple mindedness and robust body clad in Khadi dress appeared all the more handsome. He was a great devotee of Baba with an unflinching faith in Him.

His son with nickname Guddu, was about 18 or 19 at that time. It was in 1960 that he felt pain in his thigh and was quite upset. Sh. Shashi Ranjan consulted some reputed physicians of Patna and other cities for his treatment, but the pain did not subside, which disturbed the family very much. On his arrival at Jamalpur, he was suggested by some senior Margiis to consult Baba about his son’s illness as Baba is the Supreme Physician and merciful and would definitely shower His grace on him. But Sh. Shashi Ranjanji did not agree, saying that Baba is Omniscient. His argument was that if he asked Baba, about it then Guddu would be alright, but Baba would take his suffering on Himself which no devotee would like. He was firm on not telling Baba about it. But Baba is Baba and nothing is hidden from Him.

When Shashi Ranjanji met Baba, He enquired about all the members of his family one by one—wife, brother, son etc. and in the end about Guddu: thus putting him in a dilemma, so he had to tell Baba about his severe pain in the thigh. Baba sternly asked, “Why is he not being properly treated”? Shashi Ranjanji told Baba about the futility of the treatment being given by several reputed physicians.

Baba became grave and said, “Shashi Ranjan, I will tell you of a doctor whose treatment would cure Guddu of his pain”. Then He told about a doctor practising in a lane of Bardhman city of W. Bengal. Being a staunch devotee, he thought of following Baba’s directions without the slightest doubt. He searched for the doctor, going to Bardhman from Jamalpur and fixed the appointment and came back. After informing everyone in the family about the matter, he took Guddu to Bhardhman by car, along with the prescriptions of other doctors. The doctor was wondering, as to why this famous personality and a political leader had come to him for treatment, after consulting reputed physicians, but following the ethics of medical profession, he decided not to say ‘no,’ but to treat the boy to the best of his ability. He wrote a prescription for the boy asking him to take the medicine and wished him speedy recovery by the grace of God.

When Shashi Ranjanji was about to leave, the doctor enquired, as to who had directed him to the place so far off. Shashi Ranjan revealed the name of Baba and said, “He is my Guru who lives at Jamalpur and has given me your name and address”. At this, the doctor further assured him of his

son’s, well-being, because whatever Guru or Realised Souls say, invariably comes to pass and he need not worry. Shashi Ranjanji returned and the treatment began and the pain and inflammation started to subside in 3-4 days, and miraculously the boy was perfectly cured within 15 days.

I came to know of this happening when I came to Muzaffarpur after some time. As mentioned earlier that there used to be spiritual meetings at the house of Ác Sakal Dev and sometimes, Baba stories continued the whole night creating a blissful environment and during the discussion, Guddu’s ailment and his subsequent treatment and later his complete cure by an ordinary doctor, who was not even an MBBS but only LMP (Licentiate Medical Practitioner), came up. (In those days LMP was a three year Diploma Course). Thus Guddu was cured.

All of us were wonderstruck, and at the same time could feel the inscrutable dispensation of Baba’s grace on His devotees. After a few days, I got a chance to refer to the incident during the field walk with Baba. I asked Baba, as to how it had transpired that an ordinary doctor cured Guddu while, reputed doctors failed. “Do you want to know?” said Baba. “Yes,” I said.

Then, Baba narrated that around nine hundred years ago, there lived a Landlord of great religious disposition near Siliguri in North Bengal. He was honest and philanthropist and had four sons: the elder son was a scoundrel and selfish. He would demand that management of the land be entrusted to him, because he had grown up and could look after the work, moreover, he strongly disapproved of the Philanthropic ways of his father. He demanded the entire property from his father who knew very well that his son was self centred and would exploit the poor and deprive his brothers of their share and ruin all. It was a sin to concede his demand as he would squander all the wealth. His suggestions to him that he should wait till his death, and then divide the property, cut no ice with him and he remained adamant. At this, the son thought of killing his father. During a summer afternoon, when his father was enjoying a siesta, he started hitting his father with a heavy cane. There were none around who could come to his rescue and he beat him to death and ran away. His other brothers got him treated, but to no use and he died in great agony. In the present life, the same father was born as the doctor from Bardhman and the boy who had beaten his father to death, was born as Guddu. Baba said that it was the result of the same sinful Samskara that Guddu experienced pain in his leg. Baba further said that He had created such a situation that the father and son of the past life met and the father being totally ignorant about what had happened in the previous birth, his natural instinct of affection would be aroused towards a young handsome boy from a noble family and would utter a sigh of sympathy which would cure him and not the prescription of the doctor.

I understood the play of Samskaras of the past life or else how could anyone cure him? When I narrated the story to Margiis even Shashi Ranjanji was quite unaware of. They understood the whole story.

Shashi Ranjanji did a lot of work for the Marga. I, alongwith other workers, used to stay at 93-North Avenue, which was his official residence which he got on being elected as an MP. He always remained the Chairperson of all organizations allied with PROUT and played a leading role in propagating the Ideology of PROUT. He shifted to 8-Ferozshah Road bungalow on being elected as an MP second time.

During those ten years, Baba also stayed at his residence on His visits to Delhi. He helped to arrange a DMC in Delhi. Ác Nagina, Ác Gangasharan and Ác Chandranath were his classmates during his school and college days. Sh. Shamsulhaq from Muzaffarpur was his other friend and a great devotee of Baba. I, alongwith Ác Nagina and Ác Gangasharan used to visit him quite often.

He would be transported to a state of ecstasy, as he listened to Baba stories. Ác Gangasharan and Shashi Ranjanji were room mates at the Banaras Hindu University hostel in 1941-42; hence, were cronies. Both were students of B.Sc and hailed from Muzaffarpur and later became Baba’s devotees.

### BABA’S UNEXPECTED VISIT TO MUZAFFARPUR

Baba always stayed with Ác Sakal Dev when he visited Muzaffarpur and went for field walk in Shashi Ranjan’s car. Sometimes, taking advantage of holidays or taking leave from office, Baba would visit Laheriasarai, Muzaffarpur or Bhagalpur and would infuse a wave of devotion by his personal contact and discourses in group meetings and would inspire all in building a Progressive Society. He would lay stress on Sadhana and adherence to high moral standards.

Here is an interesting story, which shows how Baba used to reform people. Once Baba arrived unexpectedly at Muzaffarpur at 11.00 a.m and went straight to Ác Sakal Dev’s residence. No prior arrangements were made, so Ác Sakal Dev started setting his personal room in proper order. I was wondering how in such a short span of time 25-30 Margiis arrived without any information sent to them. Meanwhile, we, alongwith other Margiis, reached Ác Sakaldev’s residence and Baba was asked to sit in a chair in the drawing room, while the room was being cleaned. Margiis were singing Bhajans (devotional songs) and beholding Him.

Amongst the gathering of Margiis, there was one respectable Margii from Laheriasarai namely Shyamananda Lal sitting in the front row near Baba. Shashi Ranjanji came a little late and was standing close to the wall due to shortage of space. All of a sudden Baba became furious and started scolding Shyama Babu, “Six months ago, you took a vow in front of me not to drink anymore. Have you done so?” Shyama Babu hesitantly said, “Yes Baba, I have given up drinking,” At this Baba said still more furiously. “Didn’t you drink with such and such people, on such and such day.” Shyam Babu was shocked and his blood congealed on being exposed in front of all Margiis. Baba loudly said that He is watching everything; He is present where the rays of the sun cannot reach: where the air and sound cannot enter. No one can do anything

secretly from Him. His eyes and ears are everywhere. You had made a promise to Guru and have broken it which is the greatest sin. At this Shyama Babu started crying loudly. I was curiously witnessing Baba's Liila, sitting in a corner. I also saw that Shashi Ranjan Ji, who was standing at the back, started chanting Hanuman Chalisa. On my asking him the reason of his shivering, he innocently replied, “I was scared that Baba might start telling my mistakes, too. Is there anyone who is totally pure and innocent?”

In the meanwhile, Ác Sakal Devji had set the room in order and called Baba inside. People were trying to pacify, Shyama Babu but his cries would not stop. After taking rest for a while, Baba sent Sakal Dev to call in Shyama Babu. When he was being ushered in, he was still in tears. Baba started showering His love by his sweet words that filled Shyama Babu with happiness and forgetting the severe reprimand, he found himself lost in Baba's Divine Love. He once again reiterated his promise not to drink any more. Later Shyama Babu told us that never had he received such love even from his parents. Such was the style of punishment by Baba aimed at reforming the individual and bestowing maddening love.

### FIRST REVOLUTIONARY MARRIAGE OF ANANDA MARGA

When I was 8-10 years of age, I could not reconcile myself to the Vadic System of marriage which appeared quite meaningless to me- full of ritualistic ostentation. It had created such revulsion in my mind for the system that I was determined not to follow it. And the naked dance of casteism, made it all the more repulsive for me. I conveyed my strong feeling to my parents and forbade them to make any commitment, on my behalf in this regard, as I won't be able to keep their word.

I had hired a room at Muzaffarpur during my M.Sc years. One day, two quite wealthy persons came from Jamshedpur, looking for me. They said that my father had told them that any decision regarding the matrimony would be taken by me alone. I was a brilliant student with a bright future. My elder brother was a doctor and had gone to America for his Ph.D. The two gentlemen presented a blank cheque which amused me a little. After a moment's thought, I filled an enormous amount of Rs. 5 lac and waited for their response which was prompt and positive. I lit a matchstick, burnt the cheque and rebuked them as they had come to purchase me and should be ashamed of their brazen attitude. I told them categorically that I would prefer to marry a worthy poor girl rather than entering into matrimonial alliance with a family that prided on their riches, and dismissed them summarily.

When I joined Ananda Marga and understood the high ideals of Baba, I really felt proud that I was right.

Once, during a casual talk with the General Secretary of the Marga, I had apprised him of my views on Casteism, Vadic Rituals and Priestdom, Dowry etc- that I despised all these.

The family, wherein I got married, had perhaps initiated some proposal in the Organization, which later on I came to

know of through Ác Gangasharan, guardian of the family, who suggested the arrangement to Baba with the help of Ác Nagina. Baba did not say anything directly, and I came to know that the proposal was mooted again. Perhaps, the General Secretary would have suggested the possibility of the marriage knowing my dislike for Casteism. Meanwhile, I had told Baba in private of my inability to enter into wedlock due to my organizational commitments, and I had neither time nor money, so I could never think of starting a family life. Baba said, “Do as you wish”. Hearing this, I concluded that I had my way, but Baba had planned something different. It was the end of June 1960 that I thought of visiting Jamalpur. Ác Sakal Dev asked me to extend an invitation to Baba to visit Muzaffarpur on behalf of their unit and if Baba consented, I should accompany Him. I presented the invitation letter to Baba which He happily accepted and I acted as Baba’s PA on way to Muzaffarpur on 30th June.

We arrived at Muzaffarpur on 1st July and proceeded straight to the house of Ác Sakal Dev, where arrangements were made for Baba’s stay. Margiis started gathering from different places and Baba addressed them twice a day.

I was residing with my younger brother Arun, who later became Ác Svarupananda Avadhuta, and at that time was doing MA and LLB course. The office of the UPSF was also housed in that room. Both of us thought of inviting Baba to the office and solicit His blessings. We made up our mind and purchased a new bed sheet for Baba to sit on and prepared sweets from green gram pulse and arranged garlands for His welcome.

On the 7th of July, I went to Baba at about 8.00 am and met Shashi Ranjanji there who had brought his car to take Baba for the field walk. Baba came out and said, “Where do we go today?” I spontaneously said, “Baba! to our place.” And Baba forthwith obliged. I also accompanied Baba to our room, where He sat for sometime and recorded his words of blessing in the register and tasted the home made sweets, relished it and proceeded on the field walk.

In the evening, I was in Baba’s room along with some other Margiis and massaging His feet, when Baba got up asking us to leave as it was the time for his evening Puja. I cycled my way home and was astonished, when one Margii brother came telling me that Baba had called me. I was wondering that Baba had said nothing about it when I was with Him and what the urgency was.

It was Baba’s order and I followed it. On reaching there, my Ácarya told me that Baba’s room was shut but He had left some directions for me. Ác Ramtanuk and Shashi Ranjan were also there, so he took me aside and said, “Baba has asked you to marry immediately” “I said, “how is it possible? I have expressed my inability to do so and Baba knows it, why then such directions? He continued, “This is true that Baba has said so, that is why a messenger was sent to call you.” At this I was flabbergasted and found myself bloodless. I was in a state of dilemma: To do or not to do. If I told my parents they would surely oppose any such arrangement. All such thoughts started haunting my mind and I was unable to arrive at some decision, Pondering over my predicament, I reached my room

and shared my problem with my brother Arun. Surprisingly, he was extremely elated: perhaps he could not gauge my internal conflict.

The biggest obstacle to marriage was how I would maintain family when round the year I would be busy in the organizational work all the time. I was in intense mental conflict and full of tears with no solution in sight. Only one thought was hammering me, “Guru’s word is supreme and that was what mattered most.”

I finished my meditation in this condition and took Arun along to Ác Sakal Dev’s residence. Baba was already present in the adjoining house, where He delivered talk every day. All the Margiis were lost in the devotional flow enjoying Baba’s blissful presence. I addressed Ác Sakal Dev to confirm the truth about the marriage. He said, “It is Baba who desires so, He has approved of the marriage, otherwise, why should we say so; you can confirm it from Baba, if you so desire.” I could not doubt the veracity of my Ácarya’s statement. I said, “I have no such wish, but I can’t say ‘no’ to Baba under any circumstances. Please inform Baba that I have given my consent but the identity of the girl and the time of marriage should be known to me.”

He conveyed this to Baba, who said the marriage was to be solemnized within two days with Indu, the daughter of Ác Gangasharan’s sister whose family was already there and was informed about the marriage. When Baba’s talk was over, Ác Gangasharan went up to Baba and extended invitation first of all to Him, then to Margii brothers and then to all sadhakas who were disciples of Baba but not part of the mainstream Organization.

The time for the marriage was fixed at 8’0 clock the next day and the venue was Ác Gangasharan’s residence and all the arrangements were to be made in a single day. I returned to my room thinking that I did not have enough money to purchase one good dress. Unable to sleep, I was haunted by sundry thoughts. At about 9.00 a.m I went to a Margii Sh. Ramchandra and requested for a loan of Rs. 100 with which I purchased cloth and got the dress stitched and within hours the whole work was done and then I returned to my room.

Meanwhile, Gangasharanji was looking after the marriage and catering arrangements and much to our surprise all works were completed in a very short time. As per the schedule, I was to reach fully dressed at Ác Sakal Dev’s residence where Baba was staying, which I did accordingly: there a car was arranged for me. At 7.30 p.m, I along with my brother, Arun and a couple of Margiis boarded the car.

Our car was followed by Baba’s car and a procession of Margiis on foot. There was auspicious light drizzle momentarily. The marriage party kept on moving when Baba expressed His desire to Shashi Ranjanji that He should go for a walk for a while to give sufficient time to complete marriage preparations. By the time Baba reached, the exchange of garlands between the bride and the bridegroom was over.

The roof top, where the marriage was to be solemnized, was full to the capacity. Even the people of the locality had thronged to witness a unique marriage function. Baba, too,

arrived and was seated at an elevated dais. Indu and I were seated on the floor beside Ác Shyamanand, a high ranking railway official, and some members of the family. I was hardly in a conscious state of mind and so was the case with Indu: all I recollect is that I was asked to repeat something which I was able to and the marriage was over in a few minutes.

Baba returned to His place of stay and I was made to lie on the cot in the hall downstairs in a state of spiritual trance. I was not conscious of what happened afterwords and I could not eat inspite of efforts of people, because the effect of deep trance lasted till next morning, I was told that the number of guests who had dined on the occasion exceeded the estimated number for whom catering arrangements were made. The food was quite sufficient even when all the guests had left. Ác Gangasharan came out to see if any more were left without taking food, to his utter surprise, he found about a dozen holy men standing in the posture of showering blessings. They refused to take food and went away thanking for the 'Prasad' they were already blessed with.

Ladies of the house went to Baba's place to offer Him food prepared on the occasion. Later, Baba told that the marriage was most unique in the whole world, adding that when Lord Shiva got married, He was obviously present on the occasion, as it was His own marriage, but Lord Shiva was Himself present when this marriage was being solemnized and showered blessings on the couple making it all the more momentous. Baba added that He had seen that the celestial spheres from above were making the environment more and more effulgent by bowing in reverence.

### MY TALK AT THE SHANTI NIKETAN UNIVERSITY

The day after my marriage when Baba was returning to Jamalpur by train I went to meet Him at the station, Baba said, "You come with Indu and meet me at Jamalpur after some days". After one and half months, it so happened that I, alongwith Indu, went to Jamalpur.

On reaching Jamalpur, I went to the Ashram leaving Indu at the residence of one Margii devotee Sh. Bindeshwari. I asked Indu to be ready at 4.00 p.m, so that I could take her to the field where Baba sat every evening. This would provide us an opportunity to have Baba's darshan on His way back from office and so it happened. We met Baba who was returning from office, alongwith other Margiis, and offered our salutations to Him while Ác Sukurmar took me aside and told me that a lecture had been arranged at Shanti Niketan University. The General Secretary was already there on Baba's instructions to inspect local units and arrange a lecture and he had informed by post that the lecture had been fixed and someone having the knowledge of PROUT and command over English be sent to address the meeting. I proposed the name of A'c Lalan who was not considered for he lacked proficiency in speaking English and Baba had asked for my consent. I expressed my willingness as I had no problem in speaking English.

By that time, I had this conviction that the real doer is Baba, not I, so I gave my consent. The only problem was

that I had brought Indu on a pleasure trip and she would be disappointed, but I said ‘Yes’ and left everything to Baba’s wish. I showed Indu all the places in the field and then left her at Bindeshwari’s house. Then I returned to the Ashram after the field walk with Baba.

The next day being a holiday, Baba was scheduled to come to Ashram. Hence, I brought Indu along and we offered our salutations to Baba who blessed us saying, “The practical beginning of Ananda Marga social movement started on 8th of July 1960 when Prithwi Sharan (my original name) and Indu got married at Muzaffarpur. This was the first revolutionary marriage and the day marked the beginning of our social movement and will continue in future.” Baba praised Indu and me and said, “I have thought of giving some special spiritual gift to Prithwi Sharan and Indu. They are a blessed couple” I do not know what that special thing was, but I feel coming in contact with Baba is the greatest blessing and there is nothing greater than this. This is my good luck that Baba is mine and I am His.

It was the August of 1960, when I went to Jamalpur with Indu and was asked to go to Shanti Niketan. The next day, I asked Baba about the topic of the lecture. Baba suggested to me a special topic and also gave necessary material for the talk. During the evening walk, Baba said, “You should go there fully prepared as Shanti Niketan is home to scholars of international standard.”

I at once said that if such was the case, it would be better to send some erudite Acarya, as I was a novice.” At this Baba said, “There is nothing to worry. Whatever you speak will be right.” I reached there in time.

There was none to receive me at Bolpur station at 1.00 a.m., though Ác Pranay Kumar had informed that some Margii would be there to guide me, I was worried as nobody arrived till morning. Somehow, I took bath, performed Puja and went to the Department of Education’ after having my breakfast at 9.00 a.m. I made enquires of the Head of the Department who expressed his ignorance about any such proposed lecture. I requested that I had come from far off place and it would be nice of him to arrange the lecture. He at once agreed and called student leaders and a meeting was fixed for 6.00 p.m. I reached the venue in time after passing the day in a leisurely mood and found Ác Pranay Kumar and Dr. Sachinanadan there who were apologetic as nobody was sent to receive me. The hall was packed to its capacity and I did not like that I should address the audience when senior Margiis were there but they declined my request and I was asked to speak. I remembered Baba and spoke for about 45 minutes and the talk was highly appreciated. It was followed by an interactive session and the audience was satisfied. Later, Ác Pranayji appreciated my lecture and praised me a lot in front of Baba. On my return, I narrated everything to Baba at Jamalpur and took Indu back to Muzaffarpur.

My revolutionary marriage was initially opposed by my parents, that too only on account of not being invited; and gradually everything was normal. After sometime Indu went to live with my parents, but she had to come back to

Muzaffarpur as there was no spiritual environment there and she had to pursue her studies also.

### ÁCARYA GANGASHARAN

Ácarya Gangasharan was a devout sadhaka and a revolutionary, he got a bullet wound during the freedom struggle. Besides, he was a good hunter. He worked with a private Insurance Company when he joined the Marga. One day, he asked for Baba's views on Insurance as per the Ideology of Ananda Marga. Baba said, “One who has complete faith in God, he does not need life insurance, he is automatically insured.” Having absolute faith in Baba, he gave up his job with the Insurance Company and became a local whole time worker of Marga. Spending some time in farming, he offered his services for the Marga and consequently had to face financial hardships. In spite of all this, he worked so diligently that he is still remembered by the people. His dynamism, sacrifice, spirit of service and love inspired people of the nearby villages to the ideals of Marga and reverence for Baba.

I addressed hundreds of meetings arranged by him. In the entire North Bihar, 8-10 camps were held every year where young Sadhakas were imparted training in self defence and managerial skills which they executed if the need be. The credit for arranging all such programmes goes to Gangasharan.

He was my guardian and looked after my family. I would only visit my family for a few days in 3-4 months and work for the Marga the rest of the time. I am extremely grateful to him and his obligations are an irredeemable debt. I only worked for the Marga, but he not only worked for the Marga, but also fulfilled his duties to my family very patiently.

Once Baba said of him, “Gangasharan is a rose flower.” He was initiated into Kapalika Sadhana which he had learnt from Baba a couple of months before me. He practiced it with great sincerity and had many experiences which he narrated to us, occasionally. As it invariably happens with good Sadhakas, in his last days he too, suffered from many ailments. He got a cardiac problem and had paralytic attack and attained the Abode of Baba after undergoing intense pain. Thus ended the life of a noble soul, my mentor, guardian, a devotee of the highest order, a dedicated Sadhaka, my soulmate and the one loved by Baba – leaving an indelible stamp in this transitory world. I think, it was his last sojourn on this planet, perhaps, in this universe.

### MY EARLY YEARS IN ANANDA MARGA

I was assigned the duty to disseminate the Ideology of PROUT and Ananda Marga. I spent most of the time from 1960-1971 in South India. I was posted for one and half years at Ananda Nagar during those years. There was no cadre of wholetime workers in 1960 and early 1961. I, along with Ác Ramtanuk, Ác Raghav, Ác Suresh and Ác Lalan used to work whole time for Ananda Marga. Later Ác Raghav went to America. Ác Lalan was the first Principal of Ananda Marga

Degree College from 1965 to 1966 and subsequently Ác Svarupananda was appointed Principal and still continues to hold the post. Ác Ramtanuk was an advocate; hence, Baba utilized his services for contesting hundreds of Marga cases. With Baba’s blessings, he went on winning all the cases.

In those days, we got a meagre amount of Rs. 60 as subsistence: it included boarding, lodging and tour expenses which was extremely difficult. Sometimes we fasted, while at times we had to take single meal— that too without curry as it contained onion and garlic. For months on end, I took single meal a day, that too, consisting of rice and whey called Majjiga in the South. There was no option but to travel on foot: transport was not a viable option. Long distances were to be covered in passenger trains (general class) so as to save money and we had to pass nights in Dharamshala or on the railway platform as there were only a few Margiis in those days and hardly any Ashram in South India. Even such miserable conditions would not dampen our enthusiasm and spirit of service for the great Ideal. And our meetings with Baba would make our hardships vanish and we would feel invigorated in mind and heart. Once, it so happened that on returning from tour, I went to meet Baba who instantly said, “What am I seeing! There is no blood in your body.” I thought Baba would ask me to stay at Jamalpur for some days for rest, but nothing of the sort happened. On the contrary, I was deputed to work in the flood ravaged area and collect money for relief, while others in the field doing the relief work. Baba’s approach was so practical that wherever I was sent, I got such nutritious food that my health improved quickly because of Baba’s grace.

I remember, Shashi Ranjanji suffered a heart attack, when he was an MP and was admitted to Ram Manohar Lohia hospital, where I visited him many times. When Baba was informed of this, he immediately called for him at Patna and assigned him some duties at different places and by Baba’s grace he was alright. None of Baba’s directions were without some special purpose behind it. His blessings were always there, however formidable the task was.

The Organization has always been in need of money, but it has never happened that the work was not accomplished for want of money. There were obstacles of course, which are key to success. His words were “Struggle is life and it leads to success and progress”.

Once, Ác Shankarananda was massaging Baba but his mind was on money he needed for some work. Baba promptly asked, “Shankarananda! How much money do you want?” Shankarananda got the hint and realizing his mistake promised never to think of money, as Baba was with him.

I had to confront such financial hardship for more than a decade, but I kept on working with paltry amount of money. The Organization went on expanding with the formation of more units, schools and Ashrams in every District, Block and Village. In South India, several DMCs were held and many schools were opened and the number of Avadhutas also increased and they were posted at many places. Now the system had changed and money was collected from the local

Margiis and the report of expenditure sent to the centre. Such was the beginning of Ananda Marga—full of intense physical and mental clash, but the mind was full of vigour and suffused with exalted sentiment of devotion, dynamism and love.

### MY POSTING AT ANANDA NAGAR

The land at Ananda Nagar was donated by the Queen of Garhjaipur, where Baba had got constructed a two room mud hut In the Oct. of 1961, I was suddenly called to Jamalpur and posted at Ananda Nagar as Diocesen Eraws Organiser in the ERAWS—which was very much like the present Rector Master (RM) and the work assigned to me was opening of school, college, running of hospital and relief work.

When I reached there, I found it a complete wilderness, no house in sight, no population around, no electricity, no roads: it was a noman’s land, inhabited by simple tribals. I initially found myself ill at ease, but by Baba’s grace, I started liking it as an ideal place for meditation. It was quite a serene place surcharged with spiritual vibrations and conducive for meditation: later it was revealed by Baba that it was a Jain Tantra Piitha (seat), full of spiritual vibrations. Within one month, Baba started giving targets like opening school by 2nd Jan. 1962, holding camp in Dec. 1961 and a weekly market. I plunged myself in the task with great enthusiasm which picked speed by the help of Margiis of Tata and Ranchi. A Camp was held in tents and the market also started, but school could not be opened without a building. With hardly any option, the school started functioning in tents and a modest structure came up in 15-20 days by taking Rs. 300 from the General Secretary on Baba’s orders to build a house of stone. Baba persuaded His friends at Jamalpur to admit 3 children in the hostel much to my surprise, as how could parents send their children to such desolate place, but it did happen and parents got their children admitted knowing fully well that we sleep on the floor, with scorpions and snakes rearing their heads from under the pillows, and inadequate boarding facilities; still we were happy. Within a few days, a teacher was arranged, the mess was made functional and the school got recognition till 8th class.

It was extremely difficult to work at Ananda Nagar. All work was done manually and by walking on foot. I remember

having travelled on foot from Ananda Nagar to Kotshila, 12kms away with my baggage. Once due to some exigency, I started moving along the railway track at 1.00 a.m to catch the morning 5.30 am train at Kotshila, as only one train stopped at Pundag station those days, that was Ranchi-Vardhaman passenger train. Patna-Ranchi Express stopped at Kotshila, so I had to go there. That night, at around 1.00 a.m, when I was on my way to Kotshila, I came across a village marriage party who took me for a ghost and ran away. Then I called them to explain that I was a sadhu from Ananda Nagar Ashram on my way to Kotshila station and recognizing me on coming nearer, they became normal.

Many a time, I would go to purchase timber for windows and doors from Purulia by bus and load it on the goods train with the Station Master’s help and take it to Pundag and from there to the Ashram on bullock cart. All the work done was by me, single handedly.

Later, there was a lot of hustle and bustle, but I had to work round the clock. In the adverse situations, I have felt Baba’s special grace and found that success can be achieved only by working according to Baba’s directions. Baba has said on many occasions that success is possible when Cosmic will coincides with the individual will.

In 1962, Baba planned a visit to Ananda Nagar in October. It so happened that during my visit to Jamalpur in Sept. Baba told me of His schedule, with the instruction to construct two houses before the visit, one for the Marga Guru and the other for the General Secretary and the office. And I made a demand of 3-4 hundred rupees from the General Secretary as it was sufficient in those days. Somehow, the money was arranged and I plunged myself in the new task and completed the construction work in seven days. Both these houses were built opposite to the present day Central Office where the temporarily set up market functions during the Dharma Maha Sammelan. The work picked up speed, as the day of Baba’s arrival approached nearer. Baba reached Pundag by Hatia-Vardhaman passenger train and we were there at the station to welcome Him and I drove the jeep with Baba sitting in front along with the General Secretary and other Margiis.

Baba stayed there for three days and I was fortunate to do all the three jobs of cooking, taking Baba for a walk and taking His personal care: all of which was extremely pleasant and gratifying. One evening, I had cooked vegetable, Kheer (delicious dessert) and Tawa chapattis prepared on the coal fire were a delectably pleasant gustatory delight. Baba greatly relished eating buttered chapattis offered to Him and after finishing His meal, Baba appreciated the food and commented, “At Jamalpur, I only eat 2/3 chapatis (hand made bread), but here I ate six. The food here is very delicious and I award you Ph.D degree in Home Science”. All this was said in a humorous vein and filled us with joy. I told Baba, “You please stay here for a longer period and I would enjoy rendering service to You”.

This was Baba’s first historic visit to Ananda Nagar which accelerated the speed of unit formation and setting up organizational units at Ananda Nagar.

I remained there till April 1963 and Baba paid another visit to Ananda Nagar in March 1963. In the meantime, the General Secretary, Sh. Pranay Kumar started residing there alongwith his wife after resigning his job and so did Ác Shripati who later became Ác Shraddhananda Avadhuta and the first Purodha Pramukh of Ananda Marga Pracaraka Samgha after Baba left His physical body. Ác Shripati was a Superintendent in Customs Department, who resigned from service in 1963 to become a wholetimer. He first became Brahmachari (celibate) and then Avadhuta, which is the rule. The plan of starting a college was also finalized during my stay at Ananda Nagar, at the place marked by Baba and of which I had got the foundation stone laid by the DM of Purulia.

Besides the developmental work assigned to me, I was to act as trainer by taking classes of selected Margiis coming from different places and many of them later became whole timers. The first training centre was established at Jamalpur and the second one at Ananda Nagar. Once Baba sent a photographer from Jamalpur to take pictures of buildings, school and the place where I took classes in the shade of a tree, and which Baba Himself showed to people.

After my departure from there the works were looked after by Pranayji and other workers. The General Secretary’s office of AMPS was shifted to Ananda Nagar which was declared as the Headquarter of Ananda Marga Pracaraka Samgha. So long as Baba stayed at Jamalpur, the office of the President of Purodha Pramukh functioned from Jamalpur and Ác Abhedananda was appointed as His PA to facilitate the organizational work.

It is known to all that Baba resigned from his job on 30th Dec. 1966 and left for Ananda Nagar to reside there. A DMC was held at Jamalpur, a day before His departure. The next day when Baba was leaving Ananda Nagar, the Margiis burst into uncontrollable tears. It is impossible to describe the parting scene. Perhaps that could be compared to Lord Krishna’s departure from Vrindavan to Mathura. Baba too was in tears treading softly with his palms closed in the posture of Namaskar: the day marked the beginning of the second phase of Ananda Marga— Ananda Nagar phase.

### HOW THE JAIL DOCTOR GAVE UP DRINKING

Baba adopted a unique method to make the jail doctor give up drinking. It so happened that the jail doctor administered poison in the name of medicine on the instructions of high ups, and consequently Baba became extremely sick. There were great demonstrations on account of this in Patna, thousands of letters and telegrams of protest were sent to President, PM and Governor etc. Newspaper reports held the Central Govt. responsible for Baba’s victimization. Judicial Enquiry was demanded and Margiis went to jail enmasse. As a result, the Jail doctor was shifted. The new incumbent was of devotional bent of mind and gradually developed strong reverence for Baba and would sit with Baba and get intoxicated with His devotion and would invariably spend sometime listening to Him every day. One day, a very strange thing happened. The jail doctor was non-plussed on not finding Baba on His cot and started harbouring thoughts of His escape from jail that would lead to his own imprisonment for abetting the escape. Overwhelmed by such thoughts, he saw Lord Shiva seated on the bed in His familiar posture. After sometime, Lord Krsna appeared and then came Baba. The doctor was wonder struck at this enigmatic event and exclaimed, “Oh! Baba is Shiva! Baba is Krsn’a. How wondrous to have the glimpse of Shiva and Krs’na whom I see everyday in Baba’s form.” He felt blessed but could not reconcile that such great entity be languishing in jail.

The Jail doctor had the bad habit of drinking every evening without fail and could not live without it. Baba advised him against it, but he expressed his helplessness. Once, Baba asked Ác Keshvananda to get a bottle of costly wine and present it to the jail doctor saying it was from Baba and so it was done and the doctor was quite repentant and castigated himself and became a teetotaler. Baba’s ways are strange and mysterious: He reforms people sometimes by admonishing them in the dream, or gifting a wine bottle or by sundry other ways.

### BABA BLESSES ME WITH SPIRITUAL REALIZATION (SAMADHI)

Once I was on my way to South India from Jamalpur; the train would leave Jamalpur at 8.00 p.m for Kiul and from there to Howrah and to Chennai. Those days we simply bought the ticket and boarded the general compartment without bothering about reservation.

I decided to accompany Baba on His evening walk at 7.30 p.m, requesting one Margii brother in the Ashram to purchase ticket and be at the station at 7.45 p.m with my baggage and I would board the train taking luggage and ticket from him.

Everything worked as per the schedule. I accompanied Baba on the evening walk alone. He was touching diverse subjects and together we reached a desolate place in the field. I was experiencing irresistible attraction and never wanted to leave. I was in a state of ineffable bliss and forgot that I had to catch the train. Baba said, “See, You better leave; your train is at the station. But, I was in a state of trance, quite oblivious of the mundane world. Baba then embraced me drawing me close to Him and after some moments I found myself riding a chariot like vehicle driven by Baba, which I very clearly perceived and some mysterious invisible force dropped me at the station and eventually I came to my senses, collected my luggage and ticket and boarded the train in a semi wakeful state and reached Kiul after an hour.

### MY WIFE GETS CURED

On Baba’s directions, I set out on conducting seminars at thirty one places, one seminar lasted for a week and in course of these, I reached Annamalai (Tamil Nadu), where some professors and students of the University were initiated. One of them was the professor of Philosophy and the other Head of the Department of Sanskrit. For the purpose of correspondence, I had given the address of the professor

of Philosophy to my family and communicated the same to Jamalpur Ashram. The seminar was held at the University Campus, each class being of one hour duration. People were very happy that they were getting lucid and practical spiritual knowledge, especially the research scholars, who got a lot of research material.

One day, when I was taking the class, the Professor of Philosophy came a bit late and said, “Ácaryaji, here is telegram for you.” I momentarily stopped the class to read the contents of the telegram which said that my wife was fighting a life and death battle in a hospital and I should return. I read the telegram, remembered Baba and continued with the class without telling anyone about it.

The Professor, expecting that perhaps I have been called back, asked me, “What is the message in the telegram?” After revealing the contents, I set his speculations at rest.

After finishing the class, I immediately went to the Telegraph Office and sent a reply enquiring about the latest condition of my wife’s health. On the third day, I got the reply that there was nothing to worry and details were being sent through the letter.

My wife who had gone to Deogarh where her mother was a Government teacher, suddenly fell sick and suffered from profuse bleeding and was admitted to the Govt. Hospital. She was under the care of Dr. Ramesh, a Margii devotee and a reputed doctor. My wife was unconscious and was not responding to any treatment and her maternal uncle who was the guardian of my family knew nothing about it. He on a sudden impulse, went to Jamalpur to meet Baba who asked him, “What are you doing here? You leave for Deogarh instantly and take care of Indu.” He expressed his inability about his making any useful contribution regarding her health as everything was to be done by Baba. Baba said, “You do whatever you like but you go quickly.” He reached Deogarh and met Dr. Ramesh who said, “We are doing our level best but she is not regaining consciousness due to excessive blood loss”. Ác Gangasharan met my wife at the hospital and remembering Baba’s words: ‘Do what you like’, purchased some Homeopathic medicine and administered it to my wife, though, he was not a Homeopathic practitioner and had superficial knowledge of Homeopathy. In the morning, my wife regained her consciousness and said, “Take me home, I don’t want to stay here”: and she was discharged a day after and gradually got well.

At Annamalai, I was in a dilemma: thinking of my duty to return to my wife and at the same time had full faith in Baba.

I thought the cure is to be effected by Baba and there was nothing I could do about it and sent the telegram saying I would return if the condition was still serious. I had firm faith that when Baba had assigned the task to me, He would definitely shower His grace and find some way out and that materialized as Baba made her uncle the medium of her getting cured.

When I learnt the whole story through a letter, I realized that Baba had been guiding me throughout and I had nothing to worry.

### THE DUMB CHILD SPEAKS

I cannot recollect the year, once Baba was sitting in the Airport lounge on arrival at Lucknow amid a crowd of Margiis with their eyes fixed on Him, while some were singing devotional songs. Suddenly a child of 5-6 years made his way towards Baba, who lovingly pulled him close and patted him. The parents were wonderstruck to see their child speaking, as he was dumb by birth. How true the wise saying is that by the grace of Parampurusha the dumb speak and the lame climb mountains. It is a practical experience of Margiis and workers that Baba's grace makes the impossible task easy, but if a sadhaka develops a sense of pride, his downfall is certain.

### BABA'S LIILA ON EGO

Once it so happened that one Avadhuta and Ác Shivananda went to Thirucnapalli in the South to address a meeting. The former was very confident of his command over English, whereas, Ác Shivananda was fully conscious that he lacked fluency in English: and what happened was contrary to expectations. The Avadhuta started fumbling and had to cut short his speech after a few minutes. Ác Shivananda came to his rescue, as it was a question of reputation of Marga and delivered the lecture fluently. He himself was astonished how the impossible was made possible. Guru never tolerates a Sadhaka's pride and teaches him a lesson.

Another story centres round Ác Dashrath, who was sent by Baba to hold DMS. As per the routine, chants of Baba and Marga victory were heard, which inflated Dashrath's Ego and when he started to address the gathering, he fumbled and could scarcely speak and started sweating. For the next program, he surrendered himself at the Lotus Feet of Baba and apologized for his mistake and all was well.

I have had the same experience many a time that when pride overtook me, the speech would flop, while complete surrender ensured success.

In a nutshell, we do Sadhana to get rid of the Ego. Hence, Sadguru demands complete surrender from us. Sadhakas make this mistake, but Sadguru strikes at the ego to teach a lesson which is absolutely necessary.

### JATASHANKARJI'S EGO IS SHATTERED

Jatashankarji, hailing from the Madhepura district of Bihar was an Avidya Tantrik (worshipper of Extroversial Force) and his father too practised Tantra Sadhana: I knew both of them and had spent a night with them. Jatashankar got initiated in the Marga and occasionally came to meet Baba. He prided himself on being a most fearless person and would even boast of it among Margiis. One evening he went to Jamalpur field for Baba's darshan. He was directed to wait there, the very moment he started trembling and fainted and was lying in the same state when Baba arrived with other devotees who sprinkled water on him and he became normal and felt ashamed that he had fainted from fear. This is an example how Baba removes the Ego of His disciples.

### BABA THE GREAT VISIONARY, SOCIAL PRECEPTOR AND SADGURU

Baba was a spiritual personality nonpareil. Sometimes He would become a Friend, a loving Father, sometimes astute Philosopher, mysterious Guru, a Poet, Historian and Literateur and embodiment of knowledge. ### ASTRAL BODIES – DEMYSTIFIED

Baba had discussed in His Philosophy a special existential entity called Astral Beings which mortals get to see occasionally and call them as Ghosts or Spirits or Yaks'ha. Sometimes these beings appear as images of light which people start worshipping mistaking them for gods, but in fact they are Astral Beings. When a Sadhaka soars high in the realm of spirituality by dint of his intense Sadhana and still retains some unfulfilled worldly desires; neither does he attain Liberation because of his desire nor can he get human life because his existence is too subtle to manifest itself through crude material factors. Such souls float in the cosmic void as Astral Beings. Baba has categorized these Astral Bodies into following types: Yaksha, Gandharva, Kinnara, Prakritilina, Videhlina, Vidyadhar and Siddha.

Yaksha—the desire to accumulate more and more wealth at the time of death leads to this type of astral being.

Gandharva—the unfulfilled desire to be a musician makes one a Gandharva.

Kinnera—the desire to become beautiful leads one to this level of existence.

Prakritilina—the wish for crude physical pleasures leads to the life of Prakritilina.

Videhalina—the feeling that this world is full of miseries and the pain is intolerable and one wants an escape from it, such people after death are distracted from Parampurusha and become Videhlina.

Vidyadhara—the deep desire to attain good qualities and knowledge at the time of death makes one Vidyadhara.

Siddha—those with the highest level of spirituality and yearning for mystic realization before death enter this state.

Many times, it has been observed that a column of light appears to move at night in some desolate place, but nothing is seen on going closer. One gets scared and relates the experience to people who interpret it in different ways. Some call it a figment of imagination or ghost or Cosmic light (Brahma Jyoti) one who sees the moving light, is adored by all, because only the highly fortunate are blessed with such glimpses.

The greatest problem such Astral bodies face is that they cannot attain human body, but human form is indispensable to fulfil the wish which transformed them into astral existence.

Perhaps, Tarak Brahma alone is their Saviour who can liberate such Astral bodies by ordering the Cosmic Operative Principle to provide them the human body in consonance with their desires.

I have personal experience of such astral bodies imploring before Baba, but He would deny their request at that time. One day, I was following Baba, who would, most of the time, keep his hands crossed, while walking waving something aside with His one hand. I could not resist and asked Him why He was doing like that. He replied that some Astral Bodies were the cause of His annoyance. And on reaching the field, Baba explained to me the phenomenon of Astral Beings and what they desired. Baba revealed that several Astral Beings confronted Him at midnight in Ananda Nagar. I asked Him that if He alone can liberate them, why He did not oblige them. Baba replied that everything is done at proper time.

Once, I acted as Baba's Personal Assistant and slept at the threshold of Baba's bedroom. Baba called for me at about 1.00 a.m and I abruptly got up and went inside, when Baba asked me to sleep inside the room, as Astral Beings were disturbing His sleep and my presence would keep them away. I pretended to be asleep but kept watching. I experienced that Baba never sleep but only gives the impression that He is sleeping. At times, He would be snoring and in a moment He would be speaking to me. I asked Him the reason for doing so. Baba said, "If I sleep, there will be chaos! How can I have a wink of sleep? His language was very cryptic and very difficult to decipher. In my humble opinion sleep and wakefulness are all one to the Creator, as He has to control the Universe all the time. Of course, the physical body of Maha Sambhuti requires some rest: that is why Baba only took rest and never slept.

### ANANDA MARGA SADHANA IS NOT FOR POWER

One enjoys a blissful feeling after doing Ananda Marga Sadhana, but even after a longtime one does not attain any power, as it would be misused and lead to one’s downfall. Sadguru never desires this and that is why in Baba’s system of sadhana one cannot gauge his spiritual elevation.

When confronted with the questions of Sadhakas about their spiritual elevation, my reply is like this:-

Let us assume that a labourer starts his day’s labour after his meal and returns home in the evening with wages of Rs. 100 and spends it on purchase of daily needs of his family like rice, flour, pulses, salt and vegetables etc and the total amount spent comes to 100 and the balance is zero. Exactly the same is true of a sadhaka, his modest attainment makes his mind subtler, less distracted, his concentration increases and he feels mental happiness. All this is the fruit of his Sadhana. The spiritual power that he achieved was used in bringing about psychic metamorphosis and there was no remainder and he finds his attainment to be zero.

Ácarya Kishunji took initiation in 1951, perhaps, and attended the first DMC. Baba said about all those who were present in the DMC that they would not need to be born as human beings again.

After doing sadhana for 30-35 years Ác Kishunji was analyzing his attainment and finding none, thought of its futility and found it difficult to convince himself that at the end of his life he would attain samadhi, he went to Baba who said, “You will come to know of it two minutes before your death,” and he returned satisfied.

As mentioned earlier that powers attained through sadhana inflate one’s Ego, which leads to one’s degradation.

Once Ramakrishna Paramhansa touched Vivekananda and granted him samadhi and he became ecstatic. One of his fellow disciples said to him, “Vivekananda you are fortunate that Gurudev has granted you Samadhi, but we are not so lucky.” Not liking this, Vivekananda thought

of helping his friend and said, “Sit down! I will grant you samadhi.” Asking him to sit in meditation, he touched him and granted samadhi, which both of them enjoyed and went to meet their Guru. Ramakrishna Paramhansa reprimanded Vivekananda saying, “Who has authorized you to misuse your spiritual power? You got some sweets and you have started distributing these. I take back your powers, which will remain in my control and I will release whenever you need these or else these will be subject to misuse.” And he took total control of Vivekananda. It is the sacred duty of Sadguru to make such an arrangement as sadhakas are devoid of their Ego and reach the pinnacle of spiritual excellence: they attain their goal and do not merely get entangled in the mesh of psychic powers.

Subhash Chandra Bose emerged as a great revolutionary on the political horizon of India. During the course He transformed a notorious dacoit named Kalicharan into a great spiritual personality so much so that he attained occult powers and was transmuted into Kalikananda from Kalicharan, that too within minutes, by His supernal touch. M.N Roy and Subhash were so much impressed by Baba's knowledge, that M.N Roy, who was a communist and was considered the right hand man of Lenin, abandoned his belief and developed a new Philosophy called 'Radical Humanism'. Subhash was so much fascinated by Baba's spiritual talks that he took initiation and became His disciple. I do not exactly remember the year, but

one thing Baba definitely mentioned was that he (Subhash) too, like us, practised Tantra Sadhana.

The same can be said of Haraprasad's initiation. One person named Sadhan planned to kill Baba, reacting against Haraprasad's initiation, but ended up being His disciple, on the contrary. By doing Kiirtana, mind, pranah, sensory and motor organs, all move towards Parampurusha. "Mad Bhakta Yatra Gayanti Tatra Tishthami Narada"

Not only will the persons doing kiirtana get the benefit, but even those who are not taking part in it, or do not like it, will also be blessed by it.

# HOW BABA LEARNED LINGUISTICS

It is known to all that Baba is Omniscient from the very birth and had no need to acquire knowledge, but like Lord Krsna, who spent sometime at Gurukul giving utmost regard to teachers, fulfilled the expectations of His friends. Baba too, attended school and college. He did all what an ordinary man does from the worldly point of view. Sometimes, Baba spoke about how he acquired knowledge of various languages, as described below:

Mohd. Yunus, His schoolmate used to teach Him Urdu and Baba would teach him Bangla and Bhojpuri. At the age of seven Baba learnt Arabic from Prabodh Jawardar, who was not a Moulvi (priest), but came from a Brahmin family and had proficiency in Arabic. A Moulvi teacher taught Him Persian and an interesting incident took place, as he awarded Baba 110 marks out of 100 in Persian, because His answer script was perfect and on being asked about the reason for doing so, he very politely said, "Sir, what can I do? The boy has made an excellent attempt and I awarded 10 extra marks from my pocket". Similarly Baba has described, how He learnt Samskrt and Music.

Baba's expertise in Music and Dance is known to all and He used to earn Rs. 80 every month by giving tuitions in dancing while pursuing studies in Kolkata and also through journalism. Not only did He pay His fees from His earnings, but also sent some money to his mother to meet the domestic expenditure and spent some amount in helping the poor.

Baba's younger brother, Himanshuji has written in his book that Baba wrote His name in His book, in fifteen languages when He was in 8th standard like-English, Tamil, Telgu, Oriya, Bangla, Assamese, Gurmukhi, Hindi, Urdu, Malyalam, Marathi, Gujrati, Kannar, Arabic and Persian. ### SENSATIONAL REPORT OF CAKRAVARTI COMMISSION

Baba was arrested by the CBI and sent to jail on 29th December 1971. on the basis of fabricated cases with the nefarious design that His devotees faith would be shattered and the Organization would be finished. PROUT, the social philosophy of Ananda Marga, which posed a serious threat to Communism and Capitalism, had created enemies of Ananda Marga the world over and Indian Government being their ally naturally opposed it.

After Baba's imprisonment, the newspapers were full of false propaganda against Marga under Govt. pressure. But devotees were fearless and they faced the onslaught courageously by holding protest-marches, press-conferences, demonstrations and courting arrests.

Meanwhile, Baba was administered poison in the form of a drug on 12th February 1973 at Bankipur Central jail in Patna, which had damaging effect on His health and His vision was impaired besides aggravating other problems as well. On learning about this, Margiis were extremely enraged and demanded a judicial enquiry into the poisoning episode.

Baba Himself wrote a letter to the Govt. warning them that if Judicial Enquiry was not instituted, he would start fast unto death. As the Govt. did not concede the demand, Baba went on an indefinite fast. The matter was taken up by the Organization that if Govt. does not order judicial inquiry, a private judicial enquiry could take place.

I was entrusted with the job. The Headquarter of Ananda Marga was at Kolkata at that time. I met the senior Margiis of Patna and formed a society called 'Peoples' Welfare Council, with Karpuri Thakur, the opposition leader in the Bihar Assembly, as its Chairman: I was the Secretary while

many advocates and professors were its members. Then, I went to Kolkata and approached senior leaders of ‘Forward Bloc’ Sh. Amar Prasad Cakravarti (Advocate, Kolkata High Court and Former Minister and MP) and Sh. Bhakti Bhushan Mandal (Advocate, Kolkata High Court, senior leader Forward Bloc) and they happily gave their consent to conduct an enquiry in the poisoning episode. ‘People’s Welfare Council’ made a written request to them and both of them obliged by acceding to the request. The Commission was constituted on 15th May 1974 with the following terms of reference:

1. The attempt to poison Sh. Prabhat Ranjan Sarkar (Sh. Sh. Ananda Murtiji) in jail on 12th February 1973.
2. Victimization of Sh. Sh. Ananda Murtiji and His disciples.
3. Deliberate attempts of the Central and Bihar Govt. to cause damage to the Organization.

Advertisements were put in the Patna newspapers, the information about the probe was given and witnesses were invited to depose before the commission in its office. Many people sent their written statements and the enquiry began.

All the proceedings were conducted as per the Enquiry Act and advocates were hired to cross-examine the witnesses. In all, forty-three persons gave witnesses which included journalists, MLAs, and Professors, etc. A good number of Margiis and workers, who met Baba in jail and saw the victimization of Margiis, the motive behind its opposition, the underlying political conspiracy and Baba’s imprisonment on false cases etc., recorded their statements.

The commission sent notices to the Central Govt., Bihar Govt., and CBI, but they observed criminal silence over the issue. The commission held six sittings at Patna, one at Kolkata and other at Delhi. After thorough investigation the report was handed to the ‘People’s Welfare Council’ on 29th December 1974. Some excerpts are given below:

1. Sh. Ananda Murtiji, kept as a prisoner in the Bankipur Central jail, was administered an overdose of poisonous drug on 12th of Feb. 1973. The administration of the poisonous drug was done at the behest of Govt., the jail authorities and the doctor for political reasons and it had harmful effect on His body. It is certain that if an overdose of some poisonous drug, by its chemical reaction, produces deleterious effect on health; it falls in the category of poison. Therefore, Govt. officials had administered Barbiturate-like poisonous drug to Sh. Ananda Murtiji without testing and without any medical prescription; hence they are guilty on this account.
2. The Government has no regard whatsoever for the human and democratic rights of a citizen. The inhuman treatment meted out to Sh. Ananda Murtiji and His Organization is a direct assault on the democratic and human rights having the constitutional sanction.

The report created a sensation in India and abroad. The Government remained mute to demands of immediate release of Sh. Ananda Murtiji and His disciples all over the world, just to execute their nefarious designs. The authorities, first of all isolated Baba from other Margii prisoners by shifting them to Buxar and Daltonganj jails so that there were none to look after Him, causing discomfort in his daily routine. A guard was stationed outside His cell, who was a source of annoyance, as he often entered the cell under the pretext of checking Baba’s belongings on CBI’s instructions. All these factors gave a serious setback to Baba’s health.

That evening the jail officials completely ignored Baba’s request to inform the jail doctor and call civil surgeon and His close disciples. Baba lost consciousness on the night of 12th Feb. 1973 at 11 p.m, after the jail doctor administered a dose of medicine and experienced the following symptoms on 13th morning, when He regained consciousness.

1) Extreme weakness.
2) Complete depletion of vital force.
3) Profuse lachrymation and dim vision
4) Unbearable pain in the brain
5) Nervous debility

p.m in the evening and what he said, on meeting me, is worth mentioning. He said, "We the members of the family were with Baba, as per His directions. On hearing this from Himanshuji, my anxiety vanished and same was the case with my wife; when I told her about it. I gathered all the medicines prescribed by the doctor and threw these into the dustbin.

Both of my brothers, who were doctors, were against discontinuation of the medicine, but I said, "Why should the medicines continue when Baba has cured him?" Truly speaking my son has never suffered from any fits, since then. He rides bicycle, bike with a free mind after that and is fit and fine. Baba's grace is Panacea for all problems. His mere words can dispel the clouds of all ailments.

### BABA LIBERATES A STREET DOG

I am going to cite yet another instance of Baba's mysterious and inscrutable 'Liila'. This is known to all, how at

Jamalpur, Baba touched an old and thirsty cow, who fell down lifeless producing grunting sound like Hum! Hum!, never to rise again. Meanwhile, a dog appeared from somewhere and tried to be close to Him, but was chased away by volunteers thinking it to be dirty. The dog was equally stubborn and made one successful attempt and managed to touch Baba and ran away, never to return. People took the incident casually and forgot about it and Baba, too, returned after the walk. The moment Baba alighted from the car, He called the volunteers and said, "You have to perform a noble task. You dig a pit, 3-4 feet deep, going inside the jungle and give the dog a respectful burial by placing garlands on it. After finishing the job, inform me that the work was done." The volunteers followed Baba's directions and found the dead dog at the place indicated by Baba and after the burial, came back and gave the report to Baba, who was very happy and appreciated them for the work they had done. Then Baba narrated the history of the dog which is as under:

"Long time back, a person living in a family, had an altercation over a piece of land and killed someone in the heat of the moment and ran away out of fear, towards the far off forest and mountains, where he came across a Guru, who taught him 'sadhana'. He became a good 'sadhaka' by dint of his intense sadhana. He grew old and died, but had to be reborn as a dog because of his samskara of murder. Remaining in the body of a dog for five years, he exhausted that samskara too and needed just my touch, which he got today and got Liberation and is free from the cycle of birth and death". How mysterious the rolling out of samskaras is!

Baba had to work enormously on the spiritual as well as social plane and pave the way for Liberation of many beings, be they living or non-living as per their samskaras and would schedule His daily programme accordingly.

### BABA ACCEPTS HIS COLLEAGUE'S PRAŃAM (SALUTATION)

After resigning from the Railway Service, Baba was camping at Ranchi after His sojourn at Ananda Nagar. One of the Marga workers was coming from Jamalpur to Ranchi. One person, who was Baba's childhood friend met the Marga worker and said, "Dada (Brother)! Would you oblige me by conveying my Pranam (Salutation) to Baba, when you meet

Him, telling Him my name (R.K Mandal)\". To which Margii brother readily agreed. When the worker met Baba, he said, \"Baba, one person, who met me at Jamalpur has asked me to convey his Pranam (Salutation) to You.\" When Baba asked him the name of the person, he was unable to recall it. Then Baba asked him, \"Hold my big toe.\" The moment he held Baba's toe, Baba said, \"Isn't his name R.K Mandal?\"

The worker sheepishly replied, \"Yes, Baba, that was his name.\" Baba told him that he was his childhood friend.

### BABA POTENTISES MANTRA ON MY REQUEST

This incident dates back to year 1962, when I was accompanying Baba, on His field walk, along with a couple of Margiis. A feeling had been haunting my mind for the last many days that there are two words which are very spiritual and I should request Baba to potentise them so that those can be used as 'Siddha Mantra' (Word infused with spiritual Energy by Guru) and would benefit sadhakas for ages. With this thought in my mind, I accompanied Baba on His evening walk towards the field. In the serene environment of the place, I said to Baba, \"Baba, I want to say something in private.\" Baba said, \"Alright.\" Then He asked the other Margiis to move a little further away and said to me, \"What's the matter?\" I told Him what was on my mind, Baba said, \"See, this one (referring to one), I have already potentised and the second one, I will do, if you wish.\" Baba closed His eyes for a moment and said, \"It is done. From today, you can use this as 'Siddha Mantra'\" (I am not writing the Mantra as its use is permitted only by Ácaryas).

After sometime, Baba announced in a meeting of Ácaryas that He had potentised the Mantra, on my insistence and could be used by them.

### AN INTERESTING SCRIPTURAL DEBATE WITH BHAYANKARÁCARYA

A DMC was to be held in the native village of Ác Deepnarayan, perhaps in the month of May, in 1960. Ác Deepnarayan was an Inspector in the Customs Department and was a very dynamic worker of the Marga. His village Arraha was situated in the Saharsa district of Bihar. The railway station named Mithai was 3-4 kms away from Arraha village. By the tireless efforts of the Ácarya, some persons had become Margiis and a modest Jagriti building had also come into being. But people of that village and other surrounding villages strongly opposed Ananda Marga, as it was against wearing of the Shikha, Sutra (The tail hair and Sacred thread)—a symbol of Casteism. Those who had become Margiis would adhere so strictly to the tenets of Ananda Marga that any voice of opposition would automatically die down.

When villagers came to know that a Dharma Maha Cákra was being held in the village, the opponents of Marga arranged a meeting, in which it was decided that a Pundit (Scholar) from Varanasi be invited to oppose Ananda Marga. His visit was made to coincide with the DMC dates, so that he could demolish Marga by his scholastic sophistry.

They pitched their marquee (temporary tented structure) alongside that of Ananda Marga. Baba was accorded a magnificent reception on His arrival at Mithai station. Baba was made to sit on a bedecked elephant and was taken in a procession with band and bugle to the site of the DMC. The procession consisted of a huge number of Margiis shouting slogans of Marga's victory, which dampened the spirits of the opponents, but even then they were bent upon creating hurdles. When Baba reached there, Pundit Bhayankaracarya gave a clarion call that Ananda Murtiji should hold a debate with him. At this, some senior Margiis sought Baba's advice after telling Him the plan of the opponents. I too, was present there at that time. Baba told them that there was nothing to worry and even an ordinary sadhaka or Ácarya of Marga would vanquish the Pundit. Baba told them that they should ask Ác Indra Dev to represent them in the debate and blessed him with the assurance of victory.

The debate was scheduled to be held the next day at 11.00 a.m in the marquee (Pandal) of the opponents of Marga, the debate created an unusually surcharged atmosphere, as the battle lines were drawn with Margiis on one side and the sworn opponents on the other. Pundit Bhayankaracarya was seated in the middle with his associates and their leaders occupying the second row. We too were seated, just in front of them in proper formation with Ác Indra Dev occupying the front row with senior Margiis.

It was resolved that the debate would be adjudicated by their Pundits and Bhayankaracarya would first ask the question. The debate commenced with the question in Samskrta by Pundit Bhayankaracarya. The question was—how the Universe was created and the Scriptural views in this regard. Ác Indra Dev sat in Dhyan Mudra (Meditation posture) and listened to the question, then said in Samskrta that he would first point out the grammatical errors in the question and then reply to the question. Thus Ác Indra Dev exposed the defects of Pundits language and pulverized his ego at the very outset. Everybody was stunned and speechless.

Then Ác Indra Dev explained the phenomenon of Creation of the Universe and sealed the argument. Now the decision was left to the adjudicators, five in number and they, after mutual consultation, announced that Pundit Bhayankaracarya had been vanquished and there was no scope for further discussion. At this, the spirit of the opponents was completely shattered and they were completely dejected and crest-fallen. Bhayankara'carya could not muster courage to say anything. All the Margiis returned with triumphant chants of Baba and started their usual activities, making the Dharma Maha Ca'kra a grand success.
