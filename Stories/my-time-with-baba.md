# FOREWORD

My life has been an ordinary life. There has been nothing extraordinary about it. I have not been an artist or scientist or sage or anything that I can think of that would give me prestige. I was an average student in school. I was an average student of yoga. The only extraordinary thing in my life has been my touching the path of a Great One. One that loves so sweetly that the eyes cannot remain dry and the mind is in a mess, knowing that we cannot do anything. We think that the bird flies high over the mountain peak, but it is only our angle of vision. He stands majestic. The mountains, even the sky, cling to His feet. They are the wise ones. We are here only to decorate his feet with star dust.

### BABA IN MY EAST WINDOW

As a small child I could see the sun rise in my bedroom window. In winter when the trees were leafless the colors of dawn awoke my young mind.

One morning before the first light of the dawn my room was very dark. I was sleeping. I woke to the Divine radiant form of my loving Guru. I had some exposure to religion. I had some ideas in my mind about God. The vision had no words, everything was known to me instantly. This was God. I was in great fear due to the proximity of Him. I pulled my legs and feet away from His direction. My heart was choked with fear and at the same time a great attraction pulled me. I was six years old. The year was 1954. From that moment on, my mind was obsessed with the thought of a personal, all-knowing and loving Father.

### THE YEARS AFTER

The years after were long and hard. I was attracted to the forest and lake around my parent's house. By the stream was a large

granite stone for sitting. My love for nature grew. My spiritual life and my interest in the natural sciences grew. I went to the university to study physics.

### A SHORT DRIVE THROUGH CAMPUS

One night I drove past the physics building on my way to the department of microbiology to finish some work. In front of the mathematics building my car radio interrupted with the news that Martin Luther King was shot dead. So was my academic career. My mind was distracted and pulled deeper into the socio-political arena. I was radicalized.

### MY BABA RETURNS

Once again I was sleeping in the early morning. I left my body quite suddenly. The Earth, the stars and the Cosmos were rapidly falling away. I arrived near a Radiant Reality surrounded by a void. It was not a sun. It infused me with the knowledge that this was a blessed place. It was the source

I began to travel back. Shortly thereafter, I came in close proximity to a particle. Love burst from my heart as though I had found my dear brother or my dear sister. That we were the only two objects in visible existence made the expression of love extremely intense.

I moved with accelerating speed. Finally, I fell against my will back into my body. In fading ecstasy I turned to my wife. I exclaimed, "I exist!". The words were hollow and did not carry the meaning of what I wanted to share. I wanted to tell all my family - mother, father, brother and sister that our soul is eternal. Life and death does not start or finish us. It cannot be communicated.

### A YOGI ON T.V.

The founder of Transcendental Meditation was on T.V. for a few minutes on The Today Show. This was my first contact with a yogi.

I sat on the floor and pulled my legs in lotus posture. It was painful, but intriguing. I continued to try this posture for several years - not knowing why I was drawn to this asana.

### THE GRADUATE

After graduation I went to work in Birmingham, Alabama. Work was OK, but my personal life was very painful. In my pain, I came to accept that I was experiencing reactions to my own actions. I had no one else to blame. This realization gave me strength. I decided to change my path.

### THE MYSTERIOUS SUMMER ICE

My mother and father's house was next to a lake. My father was a fisherman. He taught me his skills. The killing of fish greatly disturbed me. For many years I wondered why we had teeth to chew meat. It seemed to give some justification for killing and eating other living beings. The path of vegetarianism did not occur to me.

At the age of twenty two I was fishing on a private lake. I was alone on the lake and the fishing was excellent. It was summer and the weather was hot. I was surprised to find a large piece of ice about the size of a football floating in the water! I could not imagine where it came from, since I was the only person at this small private lake. I took the ice and placed it in the bucket of water holding my fish. In the afternoon I prepared to go home. I poured the cool water from the bucket. The fish were alive and very full of energy. They flopped on the ground with such life. My heart and face filled with grief at seeing what I had done. I never fished or ate meat again.

### FRONT PORCH VISITOR

A few months before my contact with Ananda Marga and my initiation in January 1973 I had a most unusual visitor. I was sitting on my front porch reading an inspirational book. It was a book illustrated by Peter Max. Below each picture was a short quote from a sage or a philosopher.

As I was reading an old man came walking from a side alley next to the porch. He stopped and with great authority said, "What are you reading?". I showed him the book. He would read a quote then give me a discourse on its deeper meaning. Then he pointed to a tree stump at the end of the

alley near the street. He said this tree should not have been cut. He spoke about the tree's life and value of all life. I could not understand fully the depth of his words. I cannot even remember them clearly. I do remember that I had never seen such a wise man in all my life.

### MY EARLY DAYS AS A VEGETARIAN

I worked in a medical research laboratory at the University of Alabama in Birmingham. A large hospital was attached to the research building. I walked every day to the hospital cafeteria for lunch.

One day as I walked to the cafeteria my body and mind felt very light. I remember ordering some rice, lima beans and cornbread. When I saw the rice I felt as though I was flying over a rice paddy in Asia. I could see the tall green rice plants moving in a gentle breeze. I looked at all the people and I saw all of us as living beings just like the rice plants. The feeling lasted all day.

### THE YOGA STUDENT

I purchased a book on yoga. I started a daily program of asanas. I began to meditate. I began to feel an old friend guiding me.

### BEYOND RECOGNITION – BEYOND COMPREHENSION

#### (He is Always With Us)

I worked in the anti-Vietnam war effort. The U.S. was dropping large bombs into areas where men, women and children lived. I was shocked in my naive youth to see diversity of opinion over such a straightforward issue.

One day in the privacy of my home I took my draft card and burned it. Shortly thereafter, I received a notice from the government that I had been drafted into the army and should report for my physical examination!

At the time I had just begun learning asanas and meditation from a book. I had also purchased a copy of the Koran and a couple of books on Zen Buddhism. I could not label myself, however, I was very attracted to the ideas of Buddha. I decided not to

enter the army but to go to jail in protest. My plan was to defend myself in court as a non-violent Buddhist and use the time in jail to become proficient in meditation. Also, I thought it would be a strong statement to the government. It would force it to take a young technician out of a university medical research laboratory and put him in jail due to the war issue.

My family became very distressed and pleaded for me to use a lawyer. I told them I would plead my own case. I told them I would take the physical exam. If I failed the exam, then there would be no problem. With Baba's grace this was the case.

The day came for my physical examination. I left Birmingham by military bus to Montgomery. The passenger next to me was a future Margii! He was called Marshmellow by his family and close friends because of his mild and smooth personality. We talked about spirituality on the way to Montgomery. We became instant friends. He was the only person with whom I could share my deep inner feelings.

The examination lasted all day. At the end of the day we all stood together for a room one by one for the completion of our papers. When I entered the room the doctor in charge of my papers was young and friendly. He looked at me with much compassion. He did not ask me anything about my personal life, but I felt a deep non-verbal communication between us. He went to a file cabinet and removed a booklet describing acceptable ranges for hearing. He took my papers and wrote in some numbers. He looked at me and smiled. He said, "Your hearing range is unacceptable. You can not be drafted into the military. You have nothing to worry about."

I left the room and later was ordered to go to my bus for the return trip home. I sat by Marshmellow. He failed his physical too! As the bus began to move I saw the young doctor walking past our bus. He got into a small British sports car and sped away. I did not know what was happening, but I felt it was cosmic.

Today, looking back, I realize that BABA WAS SAVING ME FROM JAIL AT THE SAME TIME HE WAS GOING INTO JAIL IN INDIA! What else can I say with ink or even with tears.

Baba sent an Acarya to the southeast area of the U.S.A. I had no knowledge of

Ananda Marga. In my meditation I felt myself in orbit around the Supreme Reality. I knew that I should align all my propensities toward that nucleus in order to shorten the radius and make the most efficient movement back to Him. I did not know what was happening to me, but I became more and more enthusiastic about meditation. The Acarya came to my city to see the Margiis and give a lecture at the university. Posters were displayed and the radio announced the lecture.

I heard the radio announcement for the lecture on yoga. I went to the large lecture hall at the university to see the yogi. Only a few people came! I saw only about five Margiis and 3-4 others sitting in the front. I expected at least a hundred people in my imagination. I walked slowly down the aisle until I reached the front. I moved toward the others and sat beside one brother. I looked at him and could not believe my eyes. It was Marshmellow from the army bus! We were both to become Margiis. We knew our second meeting was planned by a higher consciousness.

Such a large city, yet Baba brought His children together to see the Acarya. Baba is beyond my comprehension. If only I could have recognized my Sweet Guru in the form of that army doctor—what a lucky man I would have been.

I can tell you that I felt something at that lecture. It vibrated me to the core of my intuition. It was an ancient vibration from my past. It was like an ancient vibration from the doorbell of my ancient lost home. At the jagrti Baba's revolutionary words caressed me like my mother's arms. The completeness of Ananda Marga was beyond my greatest dreams—beyond anything I thought possible. Baba's constant love continues to be beyond my comprehension.

### BABA, WHO ARE YOU?

My first contact with my Acarya was followed by a second contact the next day at a second lecture. Following the lecture I went home carrying an issue of a newspaper called *Sadvipra*.

The next day I was sitting in my office reading the paper. The phrase BABA NAM KEVALAM kept coming to mind. My lunch time came and I left the laboratory and walked two blocks to a vacant grassy lot to have my lunch. I walked up some steps to a cement

walkway on a grassy area where a house once stood.

I was sitting in quiet contemplation when I noticed a broken brick beside me. I picked it up and noticed one side was covered with cement. I raised my eyes and glanced across the city. A brick building caught my attention. Suddenly my heart and mind began to soar. I realized that the cement on that brick in my hand was like the love in one's heart. I realized that love will cement together human society into a true civilization. Poetry began to flood my mind and I composed a poem about this analogy.

As I finished the poem I heard a loud noise. It was coming down the road in front of me. As it approached, I noticed that it was an orange cement truck. It was very clean and new. The driver turned his head and smiled at me. He gave me an enthusiastic wave with his hand. I smiled and returned the wave.

### MY FIRST DHARMACAKRA

I was invited to the jagrti for a group meditation. When I arrived no one answered the door, but I could hear singing upstairs. I entered the house and walked upstairs. I opened the door to D.C. Kiirtan was in progress. I did not understand, but it felt good to be there.

### MY FIRST RETREAT (MY INITIATION)

Shortly after my first D.C. I was told about a regional retreat at Cheaha Mountain (the highest point in Alabama). It was January and it was cold.

The second day people were lining up for private consultations with Dada J. We signed a list and waited for our turn. My name was far down the list, so I decided to go outside and look for firewood for a large fireplace in the retreat lodge. I walked out back and walked through the mountain top forest looking for dry wood. An ice storm the previous year had brought down many limbs, so I easily gathered an armful. As I returned to the lodge, I approached the fireplace. Someone said, "You are next to see Dada!" I entered the room.

### ANANDA SUTRAM

Dada Y. came to Birmingham for one day. The next day he was going to New Orleans. Transportation was not provided, so I drove him there.

At the New Orleans jagrti I noticed a table with Baba books and other publications. It was a good opportunity to learn more about Ananda Marga, so I decided to pick a book. Actually, I was a poor reader and I did not enjoy reading. I saw books like *Human Society* and *Subhasita Samgraha*. I saw a small thin book called *Ananda Sutram*. I decided to choose it, because it was short and I was a poor reader.

I took the book to the front steps of the jagrti to read it. It was a quiet and shady spot. I noticed a potted plant on the steps. It had beautiful orange flowers with six petals. The petals were thick and succulent and pointed like a star. I sat beside the plant and read the book. I was glad it was short (this was the most difficult book in Ananda Marga!).

### SELMA RETREAT (A Lesson In Service)

The Margiis in Birmingham organized a retreat in Selma, Alabama. The bridge over the river at Selma was the starting point for a historical civil rights march led by Martin Luther King.

The retreat site was an old plantation house owned by a margii's family. A few miles before arriving at the site we noticed a car stopped on the side of the road. It was very late at night and we knew they needed help. Dada J. asked the driver to stop. The car contained two black men and two black women. The car was out of gas on a lonely country road. We were helpless to assist them, because no gas stations were open in Selma at that late hour. We left them and drove on to the retreat site.

We arrived at the retreat and began unloading the car. I noticed an old wooden barn near the house. Something told me to go there and look inside. I did. I saw a 5 gallon can of gasoline! I took the can to Dada J. He said, "Let's go".

When we arrived at the car the people were frightened to be alone in the dark. We put about 4 gallons of gas into the tank, wished them a good evening and returned to

the retreat site.

As we arrived, we noticed the car had followed us. They stopped and thanked us one more time. As they drove away, I told Dada that I was glad we found the gasoline. He told me that Baba always gives us an opportunity to do service. I never forgot his words.

### THE BIG RETREAT

That summer I attended the main Ananda Marga retreat for North America. After the retreat I began to have Baba dreams.

### THE DREAM JARGRTI

Just before the big retreat Shivam and I rented the first floor of a house for the Birmingham jargrti. Shivam left for LFT training and then to India for Whole Timer training. I was a new Margii left alone to maintain the house and teach the classes he began.

One night in a dream I saw the Earth evolve into solid form. Then I moved down to a shoreline. It was volcanic and lifeless. Time moved very fast. The sea and atmosphere changed. Plants began to grow on land. The animals moved on land too. My mind began to soar. I cannot remember all. Beautiful music began to flood my mind. I woke wishing I could share the music that was beautiful beyond description.

### THE BURNING STONES

Another night in the jargrti my dream was in Denver. A group of about twelve Margiis took a trip into the nearby mountains. On a mountain top we collected stones and placed them in a wire basket. We did akanda kiirtan around the basket. Suddenly, the stones became hot. The stones began to glow as though they were on fire. We continued to dance kiirtan. A voice came from the stones and spoke to each Margii individually. I had never heard Baba's voice but the voice was His. When the voice came to my ears it was the voice of my Guru. It said, "Keep your sword to the throat of injustice – that not a single individual shall be enslaved."

The experience of hearing Baba's voice and thinking upon His words affected me deeply.

### THE CEILING INCIDENT

(This story carries no deeper meaning that occurs to me. Baba was just being playful with his little boy.)

One morning I was sitting in meditation. I became distracted by a "drip-drip-drip" sound coming from the kitchen. I thought to myself: "Do not let this sound distract you. You can meditate over this sound." The sound became louder! Again I said to myself, "You are a yogi. You will not let this sound break your meditation." Then the sound became a continual stream! I am thinking: "I can not let this stop me." The stream of water became even louder. I stood up. I walked into the kitchen. I looked up and saw a stream of water pouring from the ceiling. Then, in the next moment, the ceiling plaster collapsed with a crash onto the kitchen table. Gallons of water came down like a waterfall! I ran upstairs, but no one would answer the door. I forced open the door. My upstairs neighbor had left his sink running full force and the water was pouring onto the floor. What a mess! What a messy sadhana. My neighbor was a Chinese student at the university. He was sorry about the mess. He surprised me the next day with a dish of fried rice and an apology. Unfortunately the fried rice was full of eggs.

### A TIME FOR TRAVEL

Soon after receiving 2nd lesson I left Birmingham. I was in Wichita for one month, then Atlanta. The Atlanta jargrti was the place for my 3rd lesson given by Dada Y. He also gave me my name at a retreat in the Okefenokee Swamp in southeast Georgia. From Atlanta, I went to Anniston, Alabama. Anniston is a small town at the foot of Chehaw Mountain where I was initiated.

### BABA'S GRACE IN ANNISTON

Anniston will remain close to my heart because of the grace I felt there. I was very active teaching classes in Anniston and Birmingham. I was trying to learn my 3rd lesson. I felt close to Baba. Chehaw Mountain was clearly visible almost daily. It constantly reminded me of the cold January morning of my initiation.

I had several dreams with Baba, but one

morning in Anniston His Grace came. Suddenly, I realized I was sitting in front of my Lord. He was sitting, although He was not sitting on anything. There was nothing in this place of our meeting. It was a void. I sat looking at my Baba when He slowly bent forward. Earlier I could see His whole form, but now my attention was fixed on the top of His head. My hands moved as though He was moving them. My fingertips moved toward the top of His head as though to touch Him there.

The moment my fingertips touched His head something happened which I will say was something like touching a hydrogen bomb at the moment of its explosion. A force so tremendous I cannot grasp was expanding my consciousness so rapidly that it seemed no time had passed before I was in a place rapidly moving to "someplace else". The place I was leaving was Saguna Brahma. I did not enter or do not remember Nirguna. I experienced inexpressible vibration, sound and "music" as I returned to my body in Anniston. The room vibrated with the feeling of creation and my heart was filled with the joy of being with Baba.

### THE ICE CREAM DREAM

I dreamed I was at an Ananda Marga retreat. I decided to leave and go to the Himalayan mountains to meditate in the solitude of the mountains.

As I hiked away from the retreat, I walked through a forest. On the trail was a copperhead snake (viper), but I walked around it. After the forest I entered a swamp. A poisonous water snake tried to block my path, but after some effort I was able to go around it. Finally, I was moving along a small mountain stream with the steep slopes of the Himalayas on either side. Suddenly, a king cobra came from under a rock and blocked my path. This time I could not go around the snake. The snake would not move and was determined to strike at me. I had no choice. I could not reach the mountains. I returned to the retreat site. Baba was there! He told me Ananda Marga was the proper path for me. He said I should work in this world. He then smiled and gave me an ice cream cone to welcome me back. Now I know that it is His Grace only that I need, not the icy Himalayans. Baba Nam Kevalam.

### BACK TO BIRMINGHAM

My employer moved his company from Anniston to Birmingham. We hired a new chemist. He came to meditation class, was initiated and was given the name of Jagadmitra. He became a close friend. Classes went well at the university and a jagrti was established by the new Margiis.

It was a very special time in my life.

### A WISH FULFILLED (Baba Saves the Fish)

Jagadmitra lived with his wife and two children on the shore of a large lake. The house was rented. His landlord owned a boat dock attached to Jagadmitra's front yard. The landlord placed a submerged light in the water to attract fish, so he could come at night and catch them. Jagadmitra's family would watch the fish at night. We felt pain to know that some of these fish would die when the landlord would come to fish. We wished this would not happen, but we felt powerless to stop it.

One evening Jagadmitra and I decided to do sadhana in the yard of a vacant house next door to his house. The yard was flat and had a nice view of the lake. As we meditated, the landlord and another fisherman came in a boat to fish near the light in the water. The landlord saw us and thought that we were thieves coming to steal from the vacant house. He and his friend left their boat and walked slowly and quietly behind us. Suddenly, they approached us in a very aggressive and rude manner. When he realized that he had mistaken Jagadmitra for a thief he felt extremely embarrassed and asked for our forgiveness. We told him not to worry, everything was OK. He and his friend returned to their boat and left.

The next day the landlord came to the door with a basket full of grapes as an act of apology. He was very embarrassed and never returned to fish at the boat dock.

Baba fulfilled our wish. The fish were saved from the fisherman!

### A RIDE TO TAHLEQUAH

I decided to hitch hike to the New York sector retreat held in Tahlequah, Oklahoma. I left Birmingham and reached Memphis with only two rides. In Memphis I walked over the Mississippi bridge. The moon was bright and reflected on the river. I decided to meditate, sleep and resume my trip the next day. I was concerned about arriving on time, but I was too tired to continue. After morning meditation, I walked to the highway. In about five minutes a car stopped. I got in. He said, "Where are you going?". I said that I was going to Oklahoma. He said, "Me too.". I told him I was going to Tahlequah. He said, "Me too.". I said that I was going to a yoga retreat of an organization called Ananda Marga. He said, "So am I!".

I would like to say more about this incident, but I can find no words. Baba has said that there are no accidents. What can I say?

### MY MEDITATION BLANKET

I received 4th and 5th lesson that first year in Birmingham. 5th lesson was given to me late in the evening. I was to perform this lesson during morning sadhana the next day. That morning I began to meditate. When I began 5th lesson something began to happen to me. I felt myself sitting on my white sheepskin used for my sadhana. Then I felt myself flying over Australia. I was flying over herds of sheep enclosed with barbed wire. Then I saw the life and murder of the sheep whose skin I was sitting on! I felt my own ignorance and shame. I felt guilty in the participation in the murder of a relative.

### A BIG WISH FULFILLED

I remember my early days as a Margii taking long car rides to retreats. I remember looking at the scenery pass my view. I had a habit of looking for Baba. I thought that someday I would maybe see Him standing in the forest looking back at me. I wanted to see Him physically.

Just before moving from Birmingham to Atlanta, I decided to drive a friend to Boulder, Colorado. I meditated at the Naropa School meditation hall. I left the school and drove west to a mountain park. I hiked up a mountain and camped for a week at the tree line near an ice flow.

After a week of solitude, I began to drive south into New Mexico. I often wished my car was a van, so I could more easily meditate and sleep at night. This thought continued in my mind.

One night I set up my tent at a rest stop east of San Antonio. When morning came I had no water for a half bath. I noticed a park not far to the east, so I decided to drive there, have a half bath and do morning sadhana. When I arrived at the park I found a campsite with water. I took a full bath and meditated. Something mysterious about the park kept me there. I could not leave. Late in the afternoon a van of the type I had been thinking of drove into the park. It stopped about 100 meters from my campsite. I saw a man get out of the van, but I could not see him clearly.

Next morning, as I finished sadhana, a small boy walked up to me. We talked for a while. He said he was traveling with his grandfather. I saw the boy's grandfather walking down the road toward us. The boy jumped on his bicycle and pedaled off to meet him. They met and began to move together back to their camping trailer. I noticed that the man in the van was out for a walk. He came upon the grandfather and small boy. They stopped and talked. Then the man left them. He looked at me as I sat at a picnic table. I felt a strong communication between us. He walked closer and closer. Finally, he walked to my table and said, "May I sit down?" He sat before me. My mind was in a mess. My eyes were looking upon Baba. My mind was somehow changed and I could not fully realize that the moment was real. He spoke to me in simple conversation and I answered politely. All the time I was thinking thoughts like: "This is Parama Purusa. How beautiful are your eyes, your cheeks, your smile." The wind changed and blew from His direction. I could smell gardenias. He left and I sat to meditate. When I finished meditation his van was gone. He was gone.

I felt that the park was empty for me. I packed my car and drove away. As I exited

the park gate, my car was filled with the fragrance of gardenias. With the scent, my mind returned and I had the full realization that I had been with my Lord physically. I began to weep and could not drive my car.

### MY PROTECTOR

As I returned to Birmingham, I thought about Baba. I wanted to be with Him again.

In south Alabama my right front wheel began to fail. I stopped my car to check the noise and noticed the wheel bearing was hot. I stopped at a service station, but they could not remove the wheel to make the repair. They told me to drive a few miles down the road to the next station. As I drove, however, the wheel began to shake. I drove on. The shaking became worse. I stopped at the first service station. I pulled in and asked for help. The mechanic looked at the wheel and moved it back and forth with his hands. He jacked up the car and moved the wheel again. It fell off the car! He looked at me in amazement and said, "How did you get here? There is nothing holding this wheel to your car!" I said, "God must have brought me here."

The mechanic brought the owner of the service station over to show him the wheel and tell him the story. They felt they had witnessed a miracle. They ordered the parts to fix the car. The next day the car was ready. They fixed the car for free. We all felt the protective love of God.

### THE DOG AND THE SANDWICH

This dream happened to me during a period of great mental stress. I was working in a clinical laboratory of a government health agency. I discovered a serious problem in the lab which resulted in routine misdiagnosis of tuberculosis. Many people were harmed and many more would be harmed if the defective test was not corrected. Because the situation had existed for years, several officials' reputations and jobs were on the line. I became involved in a struggle with these bureaucrats. The problem was finally corrected, however, I developed negative feelings for several of these officials. These feelings were harming my sadhana.

One night I dreamt that Baba had come to the jagrti and many Margiis were there to see Him. I waited a very long time, but it appeared that I would not be allowed to enter the jagrti to get P.C. I brought a sandwich for my meal and decided to eat while I waited. I went to the back door and sat to eat. As I ate a hungry dog came to me to beg for food. I gave the dog half of my sandwich and ate the other half. As I finished, the back door opened and Baba came out to greet me. He was alone. It was only He and I. I was surprised and happy. He came to me lovingly and opened my mouth. With His finger He cleaned the inside of my mouth.

I woke in tears and with my heart bursting with love. I thought of the officials at the government office with whom I had felt such negativity. My heart was full of compassion and I had a great desire to embrace them and tell them about the happiness of being with Baba.

### BABA AT THE JAMAICA AIRPORT

When Baba came to Jamaica I had a chance to go see Him. I arrived in Kingston a few hours before His plane landed. The Margiis arranged a greeting celebration outside the airport terminal. I heard a Margii say that Baba's plane had landed and everyone was excited. I wanted to go inside the terminal to look for Baba, however, when I tried to enter a guard stopped me at the exit. No one was permitted to re-enter the building. I found a wheelchair and sat in it. A Margii sister, Soulocana, pushed me to the exit door and tried to enter. The police guard stopped us. "We must go to the bathroom inside the terminal." He allowed us to pass! Once inside, I rolled myself to a hiding spot against the wall. Soon I saw the orange of several Acaryas moving towards the line of people at the customs inspection point. Then I saw Baba. Baba was allowed through customs with no wait. The others in His party had to wait for inspection of papers and luggage. Baba came through customs and stood alone in an empty area of the building. I rolled my wheelchair near Baba. I stood up and walked near my Lord. He was quiet and dignified. I stood beside Him to guard Him and be with Him. My knees were shaking and I could hardly stand. When the Acaryas arrived we walked to the greeting celebration. I walked behind Baba as close as I could.

### THE NEXT DAY I SAW HIM AGAIN

Baba was going on a field walk. I stood outside His house with the other Margiis. He finally came out. I noticed that He stopped and said something to a sister from El Salvador. He walked past me and got into a car. I was overwhelmed with emotion and tears. I remember holding a tree limb, so that I would not fall.

### First Darshan ("I Love You Too")

That evening I attended the Darshan. I arrived very early, so I could sit in the front center and be very close to Him. As I sat, I remembered my past years as a Margii. I could not control my weeping. As I wept someone put his hand on my shoulder and said, "Hey, man. What is happening here?". He was a taxi driver. I told him a great yogi master will be here soon. He said, "Can I stay here and see him?" I said he could stay and sit beside me. Soon, a Margii guard came and told him he must leave—that he was not initiated. I told the guard to let him stay but he said, "No, he is not initiated.". I said, "But he is here isn't he?". Later the guard came to me and said, "Where is that man, can you help me find him?". I looked for the man and finally I found him in the hotel bar. I told him to come with me. He could see Baba. I gave him to the guard.

I had lost my front row seat! There was only room for me on the back row against the glass wall. I sat and waited for Baba.

A short time later I heard someone say, "Baba is coming.". Baba's path of entry into the room was along a sidewalk behind me and through a side door. I turned to see Him walking along the sidewalk toward the doorway. As He passed behind me, I noticed an Acarya sitting on the patio with the taxi driver giving him initiation. I could see the Universe as though it was in a single row—held with a single thread. It was a mystical vision. A straight line could be drawn from Baba's chair, across the Darshan room, through me, through Baba, through the Acarya and taxi driver, down the mountain, through Kingston, across the bay, across the Atlantic ocean and into the night sky. It was an incredible sight.

Baba entered the room. He sat in front. As a Margii, I dreamed of the time when I would actually attend a Darshan. I thought I might just listen to every word of the Guru. I thought I might close my eyes and meditate, since He is everywhere. I thought I might just sit and gaze at Him while repeating my mantra. All these thoughts and plans left my mind! I could not close my eyes. I could not say my mantra. I could only look at Him and say, "I love You." over and over as though this was my mantra. I wanted Him to look at me and see me saying "I love You.", but He would not! He looked over the whole room of devotees. Every time His eyes came near me He would look away to some other area. Still I kept saying, "I love you, I love you, Baba."

At the end of Darshan, Baba turned from his seat and stood up. He gave namaskar to everyone and began to walk toward the door. Everyone came to their feet and were crying to Baba. At that moment, Baba turned once more. He looked at everyone, then He looked directly at me. Looking deeply into my eyes He said, "I love you too.".

### HIS SMILE

The next day I was determined to be with Him. I remembered Him saying that we should not leave anyone behind. This gave me an idea for a plan to be with Him.

Baba gave Darshan. I left the Darshan early and ran down the mountain road and stopped at a sharp curve. I knew Baba's car would be traveling slowly at this point. When I saw Baba's car coming I pretended to be a hitchhiker. The driver, however, did not stop to pick me up! I gave namaskar to Baba as the car made the sharp curve. Baba returned the namaskar with a most beautiful smile. I keep this smile in my memory. It is one of my greatest treasures.

### MY PERSONAL CONTACT

I ran down the mountain road towards Baba's house. I was passed by other Margiis running to get P.C. We waited in a room for a long time. Many people were called before me. Finally, it was my turn. I took a half bath and waited at Baba's door. I could not believe I would finally get personal contact with Taraka Brahma. The door opened and

Margiis exited. An Acarya said there would be no more P.C. today. I pleaded, but to no avail. I stood by the door hoping to be there for a few more minutes in close proximity to Him. The door opened slightly and I peeped in the crack of the door. Baba was sitting on a bed in a posture of meditation. His face radiated a state of bliss. I had never seen Him like this.

This vision of Himself was His gift to me. It was my P.C. Still I see Him there and still this vision speaks to me, teaches me and loves me. My P.C. was only a peek in His room, but it remains with me constantly and no one can take it away. I enter that crack in the door and I have Him always.

### THE LAST MOMENT IN JAMAICA

I sat beside Mahendra at the last Darshan. Mahendra had dropped out of a Birmingham meditation class, but returned to class saying, "I read the book you recommended. I didn't know this was about God". At the end of the Jamaican Darshan, the Margii in front of Mahendra fell into samadhi. He fell backwards into Mahendra's lap.

As Baba began to walk from the room, I moved close to Him. He paused and I thought I heard Him say, "Don't let anyone suffer." He walked on. He was gone.

### MANY YEARS PASSED

For many years I had little contact with other Margiis. My meditation was dry. I did not realize how dry it was. I had an egotistical feeling that I was an accomplished yogi. When Baba left His body I cried, but I didn't feel a need to go to the winter convention. When summer came, I decided to go to the global convention for my wife's benefit — she had never attended a retreat or convention. I thought it would help her grow spiritually. I did not feel a need to go.

### I WAS MISTAKEN

We arrived at the convention late at night. It was July, 1991. In the morning I decided to skip Dharmacakra and sleep. Finally, I got up and took a bath. I had a great urge to do sadhana. I was waiting for

Vimala, my wife, to exit the bath area. I could not wait. I sat in the grass beside the family dormitory and began my 1st lesson. Soon, Vimala came and I broke my meditation and we walked to the D.C. hall.

The meditation hall was almost empty, everyone was having breakfast. I walked in and sat to meditate. A few breaths after I began repeating Ista Mantra my kundalinii jumped. A jolt erected my spine. My eyes rolled back and Baba's voice came flooding down from the Infinite. He said, "Be humble like the dust, be humble like the sky, be humble like the trees." My heart felt like bursting. Again and again and again my body was jolted. I tried to bring my mind back to my 1st lesson point of concentration. Again and again my body was jolted. I began to realize that all my accomplishments were nothing. All my knowledge was nothing. I could not breathe and tears rolled from my eyes. Again I tried to do 1st lesson, but the force of kundalinii keep jolting my body. My head was so far back that I could not breathe. I was crying out, "Baba, Baba. It is You, I can not do anything." I felt myself as on a chariot. The point of my concentration was surrounded by Baba's hands and arms in the act of holding the reigns of a chariot. Baba said to me, "Think only upon me, my hands are on the reigns. You need only think upon me." Then I saw Baba's hands and arms coming down toward my chest. His hands were in the pose of Guru Puja. His hands entered my chest and entered my heart cakra in an offering gesture. I felt love pouring out of His hands into my heart, filling my heart with love.

Baba is a completely unknowable entity. The only thing worth knowing is 16-Points.

Gurukrpa hi Kevalam

### IMAGINE

On August 9, 1991 I attended Dharmacakra in Atlanta. Kiirtan was very inspiring. Sadhana was difficult. I struggled, but could not concentrate. I decided to imagine myself at Baba's room in Jamaica. Often I remember Him sitting so beautifully on His bed as I waited for personal contact. I imagined myself flying through the crack in the door and landing on His lap. I held Him and He held me. I was thinking that I was

not allowed to actually go into His room for P.C. in Jamaica, but I could remember that day with Baba. I felt happy to imagine that I go into the room and sit lovingly in His lap. I said to Baba in my meditation, "Thank you, Baba, for letting me imagine being with you like this." Then I heard Baba's voice speaking clearly and firmly. I felt a flow of grace moving over me. He said, "My son, you are in my imagination. I am imagining you. You cannot imagine me. You think you are imagining me, but you are not. You cannot imagine me. When you think upon me I am with you!"

### A LARGE HOUSE

On August 12, 1991 I did my morning sadhana. Again, I struggled. Then I felt myself in a very large house. It was Baba's house. I sat in His lap and held Him. I thanked Him for letting me be with Him in His house. Then I heard His voice. It said, "I do not want to be alone in a large house. You have come here to be with me. I have locked the doors." He meant that I could not unlock the doors and leave Him. He continued, "Do not worry, you can never leave my house. You can never leave me."

At the end of my sadhana I performed Guru Puja. With my forehead against the floor I repeated the last line of Guru Puja over and over. I could not stop repeating this mantra. My head seemed stuck to the floor. It felt so good to repeat it over and over. The English translation is (Unto You only I Surrender). Much time passed. Finally I stood up. I noticed Vimala standing in the doorway looking at me. I walked to her and paused. She said, "You did Guru Puja for a long time didn't you." I could only say, "Yes".

### A BUTTERFLY

On August 22, 1991 a touring Didi was staying at our house. Since she was from a mountainous country, I decided to take her and my wife to the top of Kennesaw mountain for meditation. Didi A. found a good spot for sadhana. We sat and talked for a while. I noticed a large hawk circling above and I pointed to it. I was surprised not to be able to identify the species of this bird. It was large like a red-tailed hawk, but it

was very different. It was of uniform color. The feathers appeared very soft and fluffy. The soaring bird actually appeared to be a little overweight! It circled for a few more moments directly above us before sailing away.

As Didi spoke, I noticed a most unusual butterfly. In fact, I thought it was a large moth due to the size and soft texture of the wings. It was a butterfly. A species I have never seen and I have lived in this area for 43 years. It fluttered around in a most unusual manner. The wings seemed too large and soft – very flexible. The movement of the wings was slow and graceful – almost like the soft fins of a fish in water. It seemed to float (not fly). I felt that Baba had come in the form of this mysterious creature. I was so sad to see it fly away out of sight.

We did kiirtan and sadhana. We ended our meditation and began Guru Puja. The mysterious butterfly returned! It fluttered around us, then fluttered in front of my hands. It gently landed on a leaf just a few inches from my fingertips. A few moments passed, then it flew away into the forest. I looked at Vimala with watery eyes.

### OCTOBER 21, 1990

The day after Baba's departure I woke from a Baba dream. In the dream I saw Him lying as a corpse. A woman was standing at His side sprinkling perfumed water on His body. His feet were uncovered and three children were crawling over His feet. The children were only infants. The woman was full of sorrow; however, the infants were unaware of the situation. They were absorbed only in their play at His feet. I looked at Baba's face and He was smiling with pleasure. The play of the children was giving Him pleasure and He moved His head back and forth in a gesture of pleasure and love.
