# Dada 1a (born around 1953 – 1973?)
## How I got Initiation
I was born in a small village in India. My father was a farmer. His father died when he was very young, so he worked very hard since his young days and raised his two younger brothers and one sister. He was a very religious person who every day used to go to pray to God and Goddess. He had built a temple of Lord Shiva. He used to visit it every day and pray. If he could not go, my mother would go. If my mother was not able to go, then my sister in law would go to visit the temple. Many monks, yogis and other religious people visited my house.
My father was the first person in the area that sent his sons to college. My eldest brother was political rather than religious. He had many connections and contacts with local politicians. From high school on, it was politics that was in his blood. The good thing about my brother was that he was a liberal type of person who was not wedded to the caste system and other kinds of dogmas. So both my father and my brother were great and admirable influences on me when I was growing up.
My father also kept a teacher who used to tutor me after school hours. This didn’t give me much time to play when I was a child. When I wasn’t at school I was at home with my private teacher who received room and board from my family.
I read a great deal in childhood but soon got tired of reading school textbooks over and over. When I was still young I had almost finished all the books from the school library. I especially read many books about great personalities, like Lord Buddha, Lord Shiva, Jesus Christ, Prophet Mohammad, Gandhi, Guru Nanak, Vivekananda, and other moral stories.
From age five to twelve I remained in my home village going to school. To finish my schooling though, I had to go elsewhere and attend three other schools in three different villages.
When I finished my middle school, I went to a larger town to study high school. For the first time I was free and on my own. I stayed in a paying guesthouse. Every day after school I’d go out to visit the town and listen to lectures of various social, political and religious leaders. I attended communist meetings, Hindu meetings, socialist meetings, ruling party meetings, etc.
My mind was looking for something. I had read the life of great people and I was searching for a great person. But I couldn’t find that. In the place where I was staying during high school every day I saw the landlord meditating in the courtyard beneath four pictures hanging on the wall. The pictures were Lord Buddha, Lord Shiva, Ramakrishna Paramahansa, and Swami Vivekananda. He was meditating to these four people. I got curious about the reason behind this and asked the house owner what he was doing. He explained it to me but I was unable to understand very much. So, he asked me to go with him on Sunday to attend Dharmachakra or collective meditation.
Dharmachakra was very inspiring. People were singing spiritual songs, reciting Sanskrit mantras and sitting in silence during meditation. So I started to go every Sunday. It created more and more desire to pursue spiritual practice. Moreover, they engaged in social service work. Eventually I expressed a desire to learn meditation to an Acharya. I went several times to learn meditation from this Acharya. But the Acharya refused to teach me, saying I was not yet ready. I almost got fed up with him. Finally, I made one last attempt with a friend of mine and we were both initiated.
After initiation I was asked to offer Guru Daksina. In general, people offer money or material things to the meditation teacher. But Ananda Marga forbids accepting any material things or money after initiation. I was searching my pocket to offer Guru Daksina to my Acharya. He looked at me and asked me what I was doing. I told him I was searching my pocket to pay him money for my initiation. He told me that the Guru did not accept any material things, didn’t accept money. So I was confused as to what I could give the Guru. So he explained to me about Guru Daksina. I was so happy that day after getting the initiation that I offered Guru Daksina as per his instruction. My initiation was over.
We stood up and started to talk. My Acharya started a conversation and told me that I had given everything to the Guru, so now everything belonged to him. “So is it true?” He asked. “Yes.” I said. Then he continued, “So now if I tell you to go to Varanasi and become a monk you will do it?”
I was really confused. I was troubled. I had never expected that he would ask me to become a monk. I started to think about monk life. Many monks had visited my house. They used to play beautiful music with a beautiful instrument known as Sarangi. Sometimes they would cry playing this music. They used to tell stories about the renunciant’s** **life through the music.
Only elders were allowed to listen to that music, not children, because if some young person listened to that music he might get inspired and become a monk. My parents never allowed me to listen to the music played by the monks, but secretly sometimes I had listened. This music was about detachment, lost love, or bereavement and love for God. And the most important thing about these monks was that they had to beg a certain amount of wealth to please the Guru. It was a certain amount of either gold, silver, grain, etc. Many of them had to beg their whole lives to satisfy the Guru. I had heard this several times.
I was haunted by all this. When my Acharya asked me to become monk I was not so sure what to reply. But I had already given everything to Guru. Nothing belonged to me. So I told my Acharya, “Yes, if you ask me to go to Varanasi I will go.”
The Acharya laughed at me. He said, “You are too young. We don’t make monks before eighteen and you are only fourteen, a teenager. So you have to wait.” These words of my Acharya went very deep inside my mind, I had made a promise to the Guru. Many obstacles came in my life not to be a monk.
## After Initiation
After initiation I never missed a single Dharmachakra because It was very inspiring to me. I learned many new things during those times from different people.
Spiritual life should be taken care of from the very beginning. It is just like a small plant. A small plant needs water, fertilizer and a fence. Otherwise, the plant will die. In a similar way a new initiate needs a lot of satsanga, or good company.
After some time, I had to go to my village for summer vacation. It was very hard to meditate because in my village it was said that yoga was practiced by yogis, and yogis lived in caves away from society. My family became very upset. Whenever I tried to meditate some family members tried to disturb me.
One way to meditate was after dinner when everyone had gone to bed. This was one of my life’s great experiences. I started my meditation at 10 or 11 pm and finished around 5 or 6 am. I enjoyed it so much I would have been willing to continue my meditation for 24 hours, but it was day so I was not able to do so. My sleep was gone but I felt completely relaxed peaceful and joyful.
During the day, I would escape from my house after breakfast and go the river and meditate by the river, or sometimes on the top of a hill. It was the most enjoyable experience of my spiritual life. It created more and more desire to do meditation.
My summer vacation was over and I returned to town. I got busy with my studies so I missed my night and day meditations at home in my village, but I had the very good company of the Marga family. We had a weekly Dharmachakra on Sundays. I was the youngest member of Ananda Marga in my unit. Everybody loved me and took care of me.
## Rameshachandra Misra
When I joined Ananda Marga many of my friends also joined. One of my student friends became a very serious practitioner. His name was Rameshachandra Misra and he was the best student of the school. He was always getting the best grades.
When his family came to know about his meditation practice of Ananda Marga they became very upset. He was living with his elder brother. His elder brother was supporting his studies financially. So his brother threatened him that if he kept practicing Ananda Marga meditation he would stop supporting him.
Rameshchandra tried to practice secretly, but his family followed him everywhere. In the end he couldn’t practice meditation. He told me, “I have no choice, I have to study, otherwise my brother will throw me out of his house and I will have to go back to the village and I will remain uneducated.”
He regretted very much not to be able to practice meditation. Unfortunately, he didn’t have any kind of financial support. He gave up his spiritual practice due to financial problems.
## Disapproval from My Family
One day in 1968, me and a couple of friends decided to move to an Ananda Marga Jagrti and live there. We were seven students. We hired a cook to prepare our meals. Actually, I didn’t get permission from my family to move from the householder’s house to the Jagrti. They became furious. They threatened me saying either I should study or go back home. It was very difficult. Still, I moved to the Ananda Marga center and remained there.
My monthly pocket expenses were decreased. I used to get a good amount of money. To punish me they decreased my expenses money. In the meantime, they found out that I was attending Dharmamahachakra in different cities.
## Brahmadevji
During this time, I came in contact with a family acharya. His name was Acharya Brahmadevji. He was very well known figure in Ananda Marga in that area where I became a margii.
The story on how Brahmadev joined Ananda Marga is very interesting. Once he was traveling by train. In the middle of the forest the train stopped. When a train stops in India many people customarily get off to see what is happening. He also got down from the train. He wanted to know what had happened. People thought it was a ghost that had stopped the train and they were afraid to go see the ghost. Acharya Brahmadev was a very courageous person. And so he asked where the ghost was. People pointed to the ghost that was walking and leaving the railway track. So he ran after the ghost. It was not a ghost. It was a person walking home. Acharya Brahmadev asked, “Why did you stop the train?” He answered, “I didn’t stop the train. I was just meditating on the railroad track and the train automatically stopped.” Acharya Brahmadev asked, “How could the train stop if you don’t have power to stop it?” He answered, “I don’t have powers but if someone does meditation he may get power. Moreover, I am not so powerful but my master is very powerful. He has all the power.” Brahmadev asked, “Can I meet your master?” The man said, “Yes, you can, but you have to learn meditation from me.”
Now this person was very lean and thin, His name was Acharya Vishvamohan. Acharya Brahmadev was a very large man, big and burly. He got curious and wanted to meet his master. He made an appointment to learn meditation. After some time, he wanted to take a test of his master. Acharya Brahmadev always thought that he was the most powerful person on the planet. He had no fear of anyone. Finally, he went to see Baba, Shrii Shrii Anandamurti for his Personal Contact, or PC.
He got a chance to have this meeting. He went in his room and he did Satsaunga Pranam (prostrating oneself). During PC, only Guru and disciple are present no one else is allowed to be there. His PC took a long time, many people were waiting for PC, but Acharya Brahmadev was not leaving the room. It was time for Baba’s lunch. The time for PCs had expired. Baba’s personal assistant opened the door and looked inside the room. Baba saw his PA and told him that this man was not leaving and just kept crying. He further said he wanted to end the PC and that he would like to go out of the room. Baba was escorted from his room and brought to his residence.
Brahmadevji kept crying and weeping on the floor. Outside there was a big commotion. People were wondering about what had happened. They asked Brahmadev to leave the place and stop crying. They also asked him why he was crying and if Baba had beaten him heavily. He said “No. Baba didn’t beat me. He didn’t say a word to me. When I went into the room I saw Baba to be a hundred times bigger than me. I was scared. I thought he was going to eat me. I was so afraid of him, and that is why I was crying.”
## Brahmadev Protects Daltonganj School
We got a piece of land in Daltonganj near a railway station. Ananda Marga was very small then so they didn’t allow us to build a school. Moreover, they came to complain to the municipality. One day the municipal official was supposed to come to remove our hut.
Acharya Brahmadev came to know of this problem. He sent many people to the river and asked them to bring as many skulls as possible. So many skulls were brought and Acharya Brahmadev put a small tent in the compound and put all the skulls there and he made a big fire pit. He put red vermillion on his head, a religious symbol. And he sat on a tiger skin and meditated.
So the day came when they were going to remove the structure of the school building, but some official wanted to talk to us first. They were requested to go and visit Acharya Brahmadev. When the official came and looked at him in meditation surrounded by skulls with a fire pit he ran away. He told his truckload of people, “Let’s go and leave this place immediately otherwise there could be serious consequences.” After that nobody bothered about the construction.
Ananda Marga school still stands to this day on that site. He just applied a little psychological warfare game.
In a later occasion he also applied this same kind of psychology. The people surrounding Ananda Nagar were tribal. Communists were misguiding them and making false charges against Ananda Marga. It was very difficult to convince tribal people. Acharya Brahmadev created a group of four people, members of Ananda Marga. Their names were Lakadas, Khapadas, Pahardas, etc. They used to put on langotas (traditional Indian underwear) and walk with spears and tridents. Now they looked more tribal than the tribal people because tribal people had on more clothes than these people. So tribal people got a little bit scared of these people. Moreover, they were hanging human skulls around their necks and they put red vermillion on their heads. After that no tribal people disturbed Marga work in the area.
## Living in the Ananda Marga Center
In the Ananda Marga Center I had a friend whose name was Dukhia. One day I told him that many poor people were suffering due to food being expensive. That year there was a drought. There was no rain for two years. Agriculture in that area depended mostly on rainfall. Most of the Margiis were busy since they had jobs.
Dukhia and I discussed running a cheap kitchen. There were people who had very little money to get food. Mostly Dukhia did the job. I and Dukhia worked to provide very cheap food during my high school years.
But my family was always bothering me. They wanted me to go to school and study afterwards. They were already upset when I moved to the Ananda Marga center.
A monk was working tirelessly when I started to live in the Ananda Marga center. I saw this monk get up at four o’clock and wash the clothes of orphan children since we were running orphanage. There were six children at that time. After washing clothes, he would work in the kitchen. After working there, he would do his morning duties, taking bath etc. and prepare the breakfast for the little kids. After that he would go to run the school.
He had 80 students at that time. So from ten to four he was running the school. After teaching he would run a small clinic where he would give free medicine to the poor people. Around six o’clock he would take his bike and visit the children’s parents. Ananda Marga educational system teaches that it is very important to have contact between principle, teacher and parents. He would return about nine o’clock. He would then help prepare food for the children. After that he would do his meditation and go to bed. It was almost midnight.
Many times he had to initiate people. He had no time left for himself. I was very much inspired by the sacrifice and service of this monk. He was a very loving, kind and compassionate person.
During my stay in Ananda Marga center many monks used to visit us. Some of them always gave me something to learn. They wanted to make sure whether I knew the basic principles of Ananda Marga or not. It was a kind of homework. They asked me about moral principles of Ananda Marga, Yama and Niyama and Ananda Marga basic philosophies, some knowledge of PROUT philosophy etc.
One day about five monks were sitting together and decided to give me a new name and asked me to follow the rules like a monk. I was a teenager and I tried my best but many times I wasn’t able to follow them fully. It was not explained to me that these were the rules for monks.
## I Meet my Guru
One day, one monk came to our center. His name was Acharya Nirmohananda Avadhuta. He was very much fond of palmistry and astrology. I did not believe in those things since I became a Margi. Most of the monks had told me that you could change your fate by spiritual practices, so why worry about astrology and palmistry?
I had no interest at all, but this monk saw my palm anyway. After seeing my palm, he told me, “You must meet your Guru, so you must come with me to Ranchi.” I was a little bit scared to go to Ranchi. It was the beginning of 1969. If my family came to know, they would become furious. I already had so many conflicts with my family. I pleaded with Dada that I didn’t want to go. I told him I would get in trouble with my family but he did not care. He said, “No, I won’t leave you. Your hand is very interesting. You must meet the Guru.” Finally, I went with him and other monks to visit my Guru.
This was not going to be a normal visit, but Personal Contact with my Guru.
When I arrived in Ranchi there were many people waiting for PC. I was the last. When I came to know that so many people wanted PC I knew it would take many days and I wanted to return back to the place where I was studying. I was pleading with Dada to please let me go back to my town but he didn’t let me go.
Finally, Baba came to the Ranchi Jagrti where he was going to give PC. I was surprised that I was called first. It was an unbelievable experience. I went to Baba’s door and the security person stopped me from entering the room. Baba’s bodyguard asked me about moral principles first and then asked me if I knew how to do Satsaunga Pranam, prostrate. I answered the questions about moral principles but regarding Satsaunga Pranam, I asked what it was. The bodyguard became furious and asked me to leave.
Many Dadas (monks) were waiting there and asked me why I was disturbed. I told them about the incident with Baba’s bodyguard and asked them what Satsaunga Pranam was, nobody had taught me. A Dada told me that meant to prostrate in front of Baba on the floor. He asked, “Do you know how to prostrate?” I told him I knew. That is something I used to do every day in my house before the God and Goddess.
I went back to the bodyguard and I told him I knew how to do the Satsaunga Pranam. The door was opened, I did Satsaunga Pranam in front of Baba. Then the door closed behind me and Baba and I were left alone in the room.
I was a bit nervous there because I was meeting face to face with Baba. I was afraid too. I had heard that Baba used to beat people who made mistakes very hard.
“What is your name?” He said, and I told Him my name. The second question was, “What is your father’s name?” I told him my father’s name. When I did this Baba said, “No, I am your father.” I thought baba was joking.
After that Baba told me that I had made many mistakes. So he said he was going to punish me. I was very, very scared. Baba then changed his mood. He said that since I was such a small boy, how could he beat me? I got really happy. He told me to come closer because Baba was on a cot and I was on the floor. I moved. Baba said again, “No, come closer.”
Then Baba said, “You are my little son, so you can sit on my lap.” It was the most beautiful experience of my life. My biological father at that age never asked me to sit on his lap. It was a great experience. He really became a true father to me and took care of me all my life. I was delighted after this PC. I was ready to do anything to serve my Guru.
## McCluskienganj
After a few days I went back to Daltonganj, where I was studying. My family had come to look for me in the ashram and they didn’t find me. They were very upset when they came to know I had gone to Ranchi. So the next time I met my family members they threatened me. I should either give up my studies or give up Ananda Marga. I was helpless. It was very difficult.
Anyway I came back from my village and kept going to school. But I started to lose interest in studying. Moreover, threats from my family made me very much disturbed. I had enjoyed the test of meeting Baba. I started to visit Ranchi very often to see my Guru. It was eighty or a hundred miles away. I didn’t care about my family. I visited many times Ranchi.
That year was known as sadhana year, meditation year, in Ananda Marga. That year Baba did many demonstrations about life, rebirth, death, etc. Many times I saw these demonstrations but I was too young to understand what I was seeing.
In 1969 Baba did a demonstration in a Dharmamahachakra in Ranchi. Baba called one avadhuta to the front and asked him, “How many people are in this gathering?” He said there were many thousands. Then Baba asked him to close his eyes and told him to look all around and describe what he was seeing. He saw nothing but Baba everywhere. After some time the avadhuta fell down on the ground. This was one of the public demonstration and many people witnessed it.
Due to clash with my family I started to run away to a place called McCluskienganj where I used to spend time with Acharya Brahmadev. He was living nearby in the middle of the forest.
This had become my hideout. It was a very quiet place and I could do very good meditation there. There was a worker posted there because there was a small master unit to help tribal people of the area. Acharya Brahmadev was traveling most of the time.
When the time of my high school examination came I had not been attending school regularly, but I passed. In 1970 I joined college. I liked to study in college more than high school. I was very interested to complete my education, but I also had very much attraction towards my Guru. I often went to visit my Guru in Ranchi.
My family came to know about this, they were very angry. They threatened the person in charge of the center. They told him that if I didn’t leave Ananda Marga they would burn the Ananda Marga center. This complaint went to Ranchi. After this, those in charge of Ranchi never wanted to see me. They would become very upset and asked me to leave as soon as possible.
I was really tormented. Nobody was there to help me. The situation got worse and worse. I was disturbed because I could not get to see my Guru. I went back Daltonganj to attend my college. I was going to collage regularly.
One day I went to see a family friend from whom I used to get money for my college education. He told me that my family had stopped giving money for me. I came back. I was very disturbed. They had already threatened me that I should leave Ananda Marga or to leave my studies. I brought out all my books and notebooks and papers, everything I had for college. I put kerosene oil on them and made a bonfire.
I thought that if I realized Brahma, God, then I wouldn’t need a college degree. This is why I took that drastic action in my life without thinking about my future. Now I couldn’t go home and I could not go to Ranchi. The only place for me was left to go McCluskienganj, where Acharya Brahmadev was living.
The family that took me in was very happy to see me. The family man, a margii, told me that if I could survive in his house I could survive as a monk. He said this because the food was of a very low quality. A meal was only half piece of flat bread (chapati), with very little vegetables. That was the everyday meal for dinner and lunch. I felt very hungry, but there was nothing else to eat. Sometimes I would go and try to find some guava and eat to satisfy my hunger.
In the night it got very cold and I didn’t have enough clothes to cover myself, I was very cold at night. Moreover, we were in a forest so I was afraid of wild animals. This way I spent some time over there.
One day while I was living there, a margii came with an Acharya and they told us Baba had given Baba Nam Kevalam mantra, a universal mantra for us to sing and dance. Baba didn’t teach how to dance kiirtan (lalita marmika) until later.
When I was near McCluskienganj at the ashram of Acharya Brahmadev, I had a dream. Baba came in the dream. He instructed me to leave the place immediately. I got up from bed. I started to think about this but I had no place to go to. If I went back home, I had to give up Ananda Marga. I couldn’t go to Ranchi either. Also, I had already decided not to go back to college. That day I was very disturbed. I didn’t know what to do. At noon I saw my father. It was unbelievable. He forced Ananda Marga in Daltonganj to tell him where I was. When he looked at me he told me, “What the hell are you doing here? You can come back home. You have a hundred acres of land. You have fifty cows, twenty buffaloes, horses and businesses. Whatever you want to do you can do there. Yet you are working like a servant for this man.” I couldn’t say anything to him. One must be polite to one’s father in India. Moreover, the family Acharya told my father that I had nowhere to go and that was why I was there. “If you want to take him, take him”, he had said.
## Back Home
With a heavy heart I went back home with my father. I did not follow the order my Guru gave in my dream and I had to face the consequences. Even if it si in a dream that the Guru tells you to do something, you should do it.
After going home, I became very sad. After a few days 1970 would be ending and the new years Dharmamahachakra would be happening. I wanted to attend. My mother, my sister, sister in law and all the other member of my family started to weep and cry. In the end I failed to attend the Dharmamahachakra.
I was getting more disturbed day by day. I was not getting help from God, Guru, Acharya, margiis or friends. My mind was slowly moving away from spirituality, but two things always were in my mind. Firstly, the Baba who had given love during Personal Contact was invaluable. The second thing was meditation and realization of bliss during meditation, especially in the night. This was also invaluable.
I had no choice though. After a few days I was sent to a relative’s house. My mind became more and more tamasic. I was not able to meditate. Neither did I have good company around me.
After two months I returned back home. One day, one of my friends who was not a member of the marga told me that Baba was coming. Meanwhile my father had opened a general store for me to run but I had no interest in it. When I came to know that Baba was coming to Daltonganj I got excited. My view of life changed. I was totally under the control of my family though. My father wanted to make a lawyer of me. I convinced him that I had nothing to do with Ananda Marga now. I told them I would like to enter college and become a lawyer. My parents got convinced and gave me quite a bit of money to go to college. But I had some other plans.
I took the bus and went to Daltonganj, when I got off the bus I saw a Dada standing at the bus station. I was delighted. I ran to him and asked him where was Baba staying. He said that he was in Ranchi. I told him that my friend told me that He was coming to Daltonganj today. The Dada told me I had the wrong information. He said that he was the security in charge of Baba. But I did not believe it and I ran to the Ananda Marga center in Daltonganj. I met the local Dada there and asked about Baba. He said that Baba was in Ranchi. I was totally confused because my friend had told me that Baba was coming to Daltonganj that day. I didn’t know what to do.
The Dada who met me at the bus station told me, “Would you like to see Baba?” Of course I said yes, but I was afraid to go to Ranchi, I was afraid of the Dada in charge of Ranchi. I knew that he wouldn’t allow me to stay very long there, so I was perturbed.
I was very much interested in seeing Baba. I told the Dada who was in charge of Baba’s security that I would like to go with him to Ranchi. That Dada’s name was Shyamananada. I was delighted to be in Ranchi. Moreover, the Dada who was not allowing me to stay there was not there anymore, so I could stay as long as I wanted. And I had a lot of money in my pocket.
I was staying in Ranchi and attending the general darshan (public talks) of Baba. One day, Dada Shyamananada and other Dadas convinced me to take training for LFT (Local Full Timer). The training was happening in front of Baba’s house. It was only a two-week training. I agreed to take the training.
## Baba Saves My Life
We were a half dozen people in the training center. I was the youngest of the trainees. We learned some basic philosophy, worker’s rules, etc. After the training we had to do SPT (Sadhana Piitha Training). We got a piece of cloth to wrap around us but no slippers, no shirt, no shoes.
We had to keep silence (Maona Brata). We were not allowed to keep any money or any kind of possession. We were instructed to go to beg door to door in the morning and come back before noon. It was a very hard thing for me, but I had no choice.
I used to go far away from the town so that no known person should notice me. One day I went very far away to the tribal village. I had collected some potatoes and rice. I used to utter 3 times “Hari Om Tat Sat”. If they gave me alms I took them, otherwise, I moved to the next house. I was not supposed to talk. If someone talked to me, I could only reply “Hari Om Tat Sat”.
One day I was walking on a road when I met a man. He wanted to talk to me, but I refused to talk with him. He continued and forced me to talk. I explained a little bit about Ananda Marga.
He told me that there was a gathering where around 50 people were sitting. “It will be nice if you come there and talk to them about your philosophy,” he told me. I did not want to go there, but he forcefully persuaded me to go there. I was alone and there was no one else on the street so I followed him.
When we arrived at the gathering people were sitting in a courtyard and drinking tribal liquor and in the other side some ritual was going on. I wanted to leave the place and I told so to the person who had brought me there. He told me that he couldn’t do anything, as the Sardar (head of the tribe) was responsible for this place. He told me to speak with the Sardar. I asked permission from the head of tribe to leave. The Sardar told me, “You were brought here as a sacrifice to our Goddess. You cannot leave this place.”
I was surrounded by many people and I started to cry on the inside. The Dadas had told me that if you had any problem you should use second lesson of meditation. I started to take second lesson, but the mantra did nothing. I was confused, but I did not cry. I was remembering Baba, but it did not work. I started to think that I was going to be killed and offered to the goddess and no one was going to know about it.
The complete memory of my life started to come back. Every one used to love me. All my family members were so close to me. For some time, I was thinking about my father, sometimes about my mother, sometimes about my brothers, sisters, and sisters-in-law. Before becoming a Margii everyone loved me so much. I thought about my nephew and niece and how I used to play with them. I started to think that my family was right. I should not have joined Ananda Marga.
I was going to die and no one would know. No mantra, no Baba, no God was going to help me. I was a heart-broken man. Death without reason was meaningless. Every second I was thinking about death. I had lost all hope in God, Guru, Mantra, tantra. I was just thinking about my death every second.
I had reached there around 8 in the morning. I had passed almost eight hours there. They were chanting and sharping some axe and sword. I was watching and counting my time. Next they brought some homemade tribal liqueur called handia. I never had any kind of alcohol in my life. I refused it but they wanted to drug me. I was helpless. Four people came and some one held my hands, then some one opened my mouth and kept pouring liquor in my mouth. I did not know how many bottles they put inside me as I was half dead. They brought some rice to feed me but I refused, so again forcibly they put rice in my mouth and I somehow swallowed it.
It is impossible to express my feeling about this unwanted death as I had lost all hope. I had some potatoes in my begging bag. One person came and snatched my bag. He told me that he wanted to eat my potatoes. Then somebody appeared out of nowhere. He told the man who had snatched my potatoes, “Do not touch those potatoes”. He further said, “Do not take food from a beggar. This person may be saint, a swami, or God on earth. If you take his things, then I will smash your head.” The person who was going to take my potatoes asked him, “Who are you?” He replied, “That it is none of your business.” While this was going on a riot broke out. People were very drunk and they started to beat each other.
The unknown person who had appeared told me, “Run away from here!” but I had no energy to move. I was scared to death of the sharp axe and sword. The thought of death was moving through my head as they could kill me at any time. I did not move. The person again shouted, “Run away from here!” In the end he came to me, grabbed my hand and we started to run. I was still afraid that they would follow me. We ran and ran until we came to the main road. This person told me, “Go, go to Baba, I will be here until you reach your place. If any one comes here I am going to chop off their heads one by one.”
It was almost dark when I reached my place. Dada Shyamananada was moving back and forth in front of Baba’s gate. When he saw me he became very angry. He was very upset. He told me that Baba had not taken food and water since the morning. He had told them that if his son didn’t come back, He would not eat or drink. I told Dada to go and tell Baba that I was back and that I would narrate the whole story later.
He went to tell the news to Baba. After a few minutes Dada came back and told me that the food and water which were for Baba had to be given to me. “That is an instruction of Baba, there will be no Prasad today,” Dada told me. I had wonderful meal.
## Baba Checking on Daltonganj
It may have been around 1969 or 1970 in Ranchi. I was in the printing press using the toilet when somebody started to bang on the door. I told him to wait and that I was coming out. The printing place office was near Baba’s office. This man said Baba was looking everywhere to find me. When I came out he didn’t say a word. He held my hand and started to run and brought me into Baba’s office.
There was a small room where Baba had an office. Baba was sitting on a chair and all the Dadas were standing there. I didn’t know the protocol about meeting Baba in his office. I was standing there folding my hands. Baba asked me, “How are you?” I told Him that I was ok. “When did you come from Daltonganj?” asked Baba. I said to Him, “Two days ago, Baba.” Baba kept questioning me, “How is everything in your unit?” I said hesitantly, “ok Baba.” Baba said, “Everything is fine?” I then became nervous. Baba saw me and He caught me.
He said, “Tell me what is happening, how is the school? How is the children’s home? How are the margiis?” I was hesitant to tell Him, because news was bad. The school was running badly. Children were not being taken care of properly. And margiis were very dissatisfied and angry with new person in charge of Ananda Marga, a new Dada who was taking care of things. He was very arrogant. I saw him misbehaving with a very respectable doctor, the most famous in the city, who was a new margii. He was always arguing with the margiis and was not taking care of the school properly, and the guardians were very disturbed and unhappy. The children of the children’s home (orphanage) were very unhappy.
Then I said, “What can I do Baba? I discussed among the Margiis, I also told several Dadas who were visiting Daltonganj.” Baba said, “Is there anyone here in this room to whom you have complained?” There were a couple of Dadas who were present at that time in Baba’s room. I pointed my finger to where they were and said “Them Baba.” Baba said, “Your work is done. You may leave now.”
I know these Dadas must have been punished by Baba because they didn’t take action and that Dada who was in charge of Daltonganj had left without handing over charge of center. If they had acted in time things would have been different.
From this incident, now I understand that Ananda Marga belongs to every margii. It does not belong only to sanyasins, monks. Sanyasins are responsible to take care of the Ananda Marga centers.
## The Gatekeeper
After my training was over in 1970, my main duty was to collect resources to maintain the volunteers. Food, clothes, everything that was needed. Also, my duty was to provide security to Baba and his house.
When I was in Ranchi, everyday I would go with Baba for field walks. When Baba was in His house my job was to be at the gate. You needed special permission to see Baba at His residence, so my job was to make sure no one got in, unless they were authorized.
One day, one central Dada wanted to visit Baba in his residence. But that Dada had not attended seminars. Our security department had instructed me that if that person came to visit Baba he should not be allowed in. Usually we had no right to stop a central worker, we could only stop people with a lower rank. So this was a special instruction for this specific person.
When this Dada came and asked me to open the gate to go inside to see Baba, I respectfully told him that he was not allowed in because he had not attended seminars. He said, “How dare you! I am a central worker. I don’t’ need any permission.” He started shouting at me. Many times I politely told him to return, but he refused. He threatened to have me punished. I had no choice but to tell Dada that if he forced me then I would blow my whistle and the security would come running. I told him to please leave because if the whistle was blown then all the security would come. I was a little bit afraid because I was a very small worker and he was a central Dada, so I told my security in charge that he had threatened to punish me.
My security in charge wrote the complaint and went to Baba. Baba said, “That volunteer should not be punished but rewarded for his work. He has done his work.” When I was told by my superior that Baba wanted to reward me I became a little relaxed from the fear of the central authorities.
## Baba and the Lying Spy
Once there was a man living in an Ananda Marga jagrti and he was having darshan with Baba (meeting with Baba) every day. He was behaving normally. One day Baba came out of his room and saw this fellow and told him to leave our center. Baba told everybody that that was an ashram. He said, “Anybody can come and stay if they don’t have bad intentions.” Then he addressed that person, “Until this day you have been sending real reports about Ananda Marga to the government, but today you have sent a false report about Ananda Marga to the government.”
He was a spy. He had been sent by government to spy on Ananda Marga and was reporting every day what he saw there. His bosses wanted something against Ananda Marga so he had made up a story and sent a false report to please his bosses.
## UKK Year, Kiirtan Around Baba
1971 was the year we were celebrating the Golden Jubilee of Baba’s birthday, that is his 50th birthday.
There was a big program going on all over India, and especially in Ranchi. Ranchi was the place where Baba was living at that time. That was the office of Ananda Marga. I was also living in Ranchi at that time and I was working with Baba. Basically I was posted for Baba’s security, and my job was to take Baba to the office with four or five other volunteers. Also taking Baba on field walks, taking Baba to the Jagrti, and when Baba was at home, guarding his house. That was our job.
From time to time I was going to raise funds to support the volunteers. Because of this sometimes I used to be in Ranchi and sometimes I was somewhere else. I was very busy. Everywhere there was kiirtan going on that year, so much kiirtan everywhere. Kiirtan in the morning, kiirtan in the evening.
This year was called UKK year, Utilization of Katha and Kiirtan. All over there was Kiirtan. There was so much devotional flow in that year. In Ranchi people were dancing everywhere.
One day, Baba came to the office in the evening. Someone put a chair outside of the office and asked him to sit on the chair. Baba sat on the chair and Baba requested Kiirtan be done around him. I was present in that group during Kiirtan around Baba. It was one of my most beautiful experiences of kiirtan.
Seminars and kiirtan were attracting a lot of people to Ranchi. And many people were stopping by there.
In this period some non-Indian margiis also arrived to see Baba. There was big publicity about Ananda Marga and PROUT in the biggest Hindi and English newspaper of the state. This was the first time that Baba’s picture and story about Ananda Marga came out on a big scale.
## Baba Holds Special Dharmamahachakra for Me
The summer approached and the annual DMC was coming. Just two or three days before DMC one of my superiors asked me to go to Allhabad to take care of some things.
Allhabad was the head office of the volunteers at that time. I knew that if I went I would not be able to attend DMC, so when Dada told me to go I was really shocked. I felt so bad about it because I had missed all the previous DMC’s in 1971. I didn’t like to miss DMC. I pleaded to Dada that he should keep me in Ranchi because I wanted very much to attend this DMC, but Dada said that there was no alternative and I had to go. I agreed to go, but with a very heavy heart.
I had very bad feelings and thought, “I am going to miss DMC”. DMC was something very important in my life. There are two things I never liked to miss, my Dharmachakra, and Dharmamahachakra.
It was so hard it is impossible to explain. But when Dada asked me to go I had no choice, I was a worker and I had to follow the discipline of the organization. I had to follow the orders of my superiors. I took a bus and train ride to Allhabad, but all the way from Ranchi to Allhabad I was thinking about DMC.
When I arrived in Allhabad I went to a place called Mumford Ganj. This is where the volunteer office was located. There was one man living in the office who was the caretaker of the office. I had a letter for him from Dada and I gave him the letter.
He was a family man and he had to go to Ranchi to arrange his daughter’s marriage, so I gave him the letter and told him to go to Ranchi. He said he did not have time to go there and that he did not want to go to Ranchi because there was no time to arrange the marriage for his daughter. I told him that I could stay there in his place but he said, “No, no, no. You can’t stay here, you are not allowed to stay here”.
I said, “Dada told me to come here and stay and to give the letter to you.” This man replied, “No. I cannot allow that you stay for even one hour here”. I had a bit of a struggle with him. I don’t know whether he was unhappy or what, but he didn’t want to leave the office. So finally he said, “No, you just eat and go. Don’t stay here, go back to Ranchi”.
I thought to myself, “Oh my God, if I go back to Ranchi Dada is going to get mad at me because I didn’t want to come in the first place and he will think that I am just making up some story.” I had no choice however, I couldn’t stay in the office because that man was very big and he was some ex-army official. I was so scared as I was only 17 years old at the time and a very thin and skinny guy. How could I fight with him? So finally I had no choice but to leave the place.
Again I took the train, but I was scared to death. I was worried that I would go and that Dada was going to scold me saying that I was making excuses. I went back to Ranchi thinking about all the bad things.
When I went back Dada he looked at me and said, “Well, you did not go.” I tried to convince him that I had gone, but he was very mad at me. He said, “Well I know that you didn’t want to go and you are just making up a story”. I said, “No Dada, I went there”. He told me that my punishment was that I would not be allowed to see Baba. “You are not going to see Baba during the DMC time, you are removed from the job of security”.
I actually remained in the security department of Ananda Marga, but they said that when Baba came, I had to go a certain distance from Baba and watch to see if some trouble makers were coming. I had to go to the roadside, in a place where I couldn’t see Baba, and I could only return to the grounds when Baba had left the pandal (tent), after the programs had ended.
Three days passed and after the third day DMC was over and Baba was going to go back. At that time, Baba was staying just near the pandal in a small place they had rented, not in his usual quarters. Now DMC was over and everything had finished. I had not seen Baba’s face even one day.
I was very, very sad this day. I was feeling a kind of remorse. How come I was being punished for no reason? I felt very bad.
That evening I got a call while we were just sitting in the security tent. The same Dada who was angry with me along with another Dada came and said there was going to be a special DMC for those who had not attended. My name was on the list and already Baba had approved my name.
I had no idea how many people had not attended the DMC. You can’t imagine, I was so excited about it! I thought to myself, “Wow, I am going to be in a special DMC”.
They took me to the place where Baba was staying, a small house. Outside the house they put Baba’s chair and we sat on the floor.
I was the only non-sanyasin. Along with me there were two other Dadas, that was it. There were only three people. I was expecting many other people who had not attended as well. I sat with these two Dadas and after few minutes, Baba came and we stood up. He came in a lungi and shawl cover, not in a formal way. It was just us three guys and Baba’s bodyguard. Altogether we were five in number.
The DMC started. Baba spoke to the first Dada and asked him how come he was afraid of Baba. Whenever he came to Ranchi he did not like to see Baba. He was so afraid that he always wanted to run away from Ranchi. Baba said, “How come you have so much fear of me, you are always staying away from me.” He told Baba he would not make this mistake again.
Baba addressed the other Dada and said, “Now I am teaching you how to go into Samadhi with Baba Nam Kevalam.” He told this Dada to sing kiirtan. This Dada closed his eyes and just started to do kiirtan. The other Dada also joined in the kiirtan. In seeing this I do not know what happened to me. I have no idea about it. Only I remember that I was for sometime there and then I got totally lost. I only remember that I was watching it all and I was amazed to see Baba and these Dadas.
When I came about again it was in the evening and everybody was gone. I was still meditating there and when I opened my eyes I saw one of my superior Dadas scolding me, “Hey, it is time to go, you are just sitting here”. I was in such a blissful state it is hard to describe in words. So he held my hand and took me back to the grounds. This was my last face encounter with Baba, until 1977.
The main thing I learned is that the Guru, Baba, knows my inner desires, He knows my pain, He knows. Nobody understands, but the Guru knows. Nobody wanted to help, and even they were kind of suspicious. Even out of all this trouble I had a very good experience.
This is a wonderful thing I got from the Baba. There were maybe 30,000 people. I was the only margii, the only non-acharya person to get that golden chance to have a personal DMC with Baba. To hold a DMC for me, I cannot forget, because this is something. If God wants, anything is possible, if Baba wants everything is possible.
## Baba Leaves, I Remain in Ranchi
One day some fellow misbehaved with a female margii. Another margii saw this, so the first margii got caught. They punished him. He left and came back with a big group of people to fight with the margii that told on him.
All this was preplanned by the government. As I have stated earlier this was when we were removing spies from the Ananda Marga centers and the government was disturbed by the seminars because some much work was going on. All India was full of wall writing by Ananda Marga against corruption, injustice, against capitalism, communism, etc.
Anyway a gang of people attacked Ananda Marga at Ranchi. A kind of riot broke out. Police mostly arrested Ananda Marga members because they could spot them in orange robes while the criminals escaped.
After this incident, seeing the situation in Ranchi, Baba was moved to Patna. I was going to go with baba to Patna for his security, but my authority gave me a duty to take care of Ranchi, Baba’s house. I felt bad because I would miss Baba, but I had to follow my orders and I remained in Ranchi.
## We Go to Prison
Ranchi was a little bit tense when I was there. After a few days I left to travel to get funds to support volunteers who used to take care of Baba’s quarters. When I returned to Ranchi there were almost fifteen people living there. When I was leaving there, we were only five.
The next day the police came and raided Baba’s quarters, that is the central office, Ananda Marga Press, school etc. They arrested everyone that was there.
When I was giving the guards the duties of Baba’s quarters a policeman came and started to talk to me. When he came to know about my family he said, “I just met your brother and father a few days ago.” My brother was a politician and a teacher so these police officers knew my family. He asked me what I was doing there and I explained that that used to be my Guru’s house and now I was taking care of it in his absence.
The police that arrested us were from a southern state so they spoke a different language. When they put us in the police van they just started beating us. Luckily, this one policeman who knew me, who had visited my house and knew my family, intervened and asked why they were beating me. He told them to stop. He said, “He belongs to a very respectable family. He is the care taker of this house.” With the help of this policeman I was saved. But the other margiis and volunteers were mercilessly beaten. On top of that they were speaking to us in a language we didn’t understand because the languages in north and south India are completely different.
They took us to the police station and put us in a holding room. There were thirty-three of us. They put us in different rooms. In my holding room there were eight of us. I was cold and it was raining. They didn’t allow us for sixteen hours to even go to the bathroom. There was no toilet in the room, so we had to remain there and urinate in the same room. It was a living hell. The room stank from the previous occupants.
In the morning we all were interrogated one by one. When my turn came I went there in the interrogation room and without saying a word they started to abuse me. I was totally shocked. They were making false accusations. I told them I had just come there yesterday and mostly I just took care of the place. When I told them about my father and my brother they let me go back to my holding cell in the police station.
After that all thirty-three of us were sent to prison. We were given very bad tamasic food. They divided us up into different buildings and kept us separated. In India prisons were run by inmates; thieves, rapists, murderers, all the hard-core criminals. We were about five people in a room. They put us in a place where everybody was urinating and going to the toilet. We could not sleep in the night because all night long people were coming and going. The government was planning to break us. They wanted somehow to make us leave Ananda Marga.
After two days we requested a place where we could all stay, meditate together and eat sentient food. The prison authorities didn’t allow us this. After that we all declared a hunger strike. Fast to death. It was very difficult for me. I had never lived without food. I had never done fasting in the past. So I had a lot of pain in my stomach. But the treatment that we were getting was way worse than that, so I decided to die fasting rather than by being treated like that.
In the evening we were not going to our rooms. Sometimes they would come and forcibly take us to our rooms.
I was very young looking, I was not even eighteen at that time, and so they put me in a juvenile ward. It was a little better. There were many young children and some of them were very kind. They would share their chapati with me. They would hide it and bring it to me and tell me, “Brother, eat or you will die. Nobody cares here. Many people have died here. Many children have died.” But I told them I couldn’t accept their food because I was protesting against injustice. We wanted to live with our own members so that we could eat properly, a sentient diet and meditate. I explained to them we were not criminals but political prisoners. I had done no crime.
Many children told us they had been put in the jail for very minor things. Sometimes they would cry when they told me these things.
Later, during our stay in another prison in Gaya we say a boy who looked hardly 10 years old. In seven months he had been released about seven times, but he would always come back. He had nowhere else to go. Jail had become his home.
It is the duty of government and the society to look after this kind of children. If these children, along with poor people and minor criminals are always in the company of hard-core criminals they can easily become hard-core criminals themselves. The government should provide free lawyers to the poor people and also allow bails for small crimes.
After three days, the jail authorities accepted our demands. They gave us a room, but this room was not meant for people to stay there. This was a kind of garbage room where they had put all the ashes from the wood fires along with other garbage. It was full of ashes. On those ashes they had put broken bricks and stones to hold blankets. It was very painful to sleep but we were happy because we were together.
Now there was no toilet there so we made a hole in one corner so that we could pass urine and use it as a toilet. And on the other side of the room we dug a hole so that we could cook.
The day we got these facilities we came to know in the evening that Baba was arrested and was coming to jail. When we heard that Baba was coming to jail we all started to cry and weep. How could Baba live in such a place, it was like hell. Fortunately, Baba was released by night by a special judge.
There was a funny story about it. I heard this story while in prison. The judge asked one of the government witnesses, “Do you know Baba?’ He said “Yes, very well. He is very tall, he has long hair and beard and wears orange clothes and I saw him living in his quarters when the police raided his house.” Of course it was easy to prove that Baba was in Patna and came by plane, had not been in Ranchi, was not very tall and never wore orange clothes. So the judge laughed at him.
In the morning news came that Baba had gone back to Patna and we were very happy.
One thing in the jail was the latrine. There was no toilet room but a latrine, with no privacy, you had to sit right next to other people, it was very smelly and always filled with excrement. It was horrible.
While in jail we made a routine. Morning and evening we did collective meditation with a great deal of singing and chanting. Also Dadas gave us classes. We did it everyday. In this way we passed one month. We were never brought to court before a judge.
After one month we were moved to Gaya, the central jail, (it is near Bodhgaya where Buddha got his liberation). Sometimes police are very brutal. I wanted to urinate when they were taking me from Ranchi to Gaya but they didn’t allow me to get down from the police van. I had to pee in my pants.
We were all handcuffed and they had also put a rope between our hands at our back so that there was no way to escape. When we went to jail we already had a room. Also in that room there was a latrine separated from the room. And outside of the room there was a place where we used to cook our food. We used to stay twelve hours inside of the room and twelve hours outside in our compound.
There were several problems. One thing was that there was an open drain, a latrine which was always flowing very near us, so it was very unhygienic. Another problem was that there were bed bugs. Some that would suck your blood along with lice, and other bugs which infested our clothing. And also mosquitoes. You might say it was inhumane to treat people like that in the modern world.
We only survived by singing, chanting, doing collective meditation and reading religious books. Moreover, every day we had a class or a debate or discussion. In the night we had storytelling. We kept ourselves very busy.
## My Family Tries to Get Me Out of Prison, But I Refuse
My family came to know that I was in prison. They went to the ruling party headquarters because my family belonged to the ruling party and had influence. The ruling party could actually depend on ten thousand votes from my family. They suggested that I had to write a letter stating that I was giving up on Ananda Marga. They knew I was a young boy had committed no serious crime. Some of them used to visit my family, so I had met several of them.
My father and brother came several times and asked me to write a letter stating that I was giving up Ananda Marga. They both broke down and started to weep and cry when they saw me in that situation. They never expected me to be in jail.
I told my brother and my father, “What is to give up Ananda Marga? Ananda Marga made me to follow morality should I give it up? Ananda Marga taught me to meditate and love God. Should I give it up? Ananda Marga taught me to do service for others. Should I give it up?” They had no reply.
They came several times to meet me and asked me to do the same thing. Sign a piece of paper stating that I would renounce Ananda Marga. The party leaders were getting disturbed when my family would explain about me. Then they suggested that they should get my signature on a blank sheet of paper and they would fill in the rest. I refused. I told them I had done no crime so I wouldn’t sign a blank paper.
## Long Kiirtan in Prison
We had regular collective meditation twice a day. We also had kiirtan and spiritual singing regularly. During those days I was either reading a religious book or chanting my ista mantra (first lesson of meditation in Ananda Marga). My mind was always filled with spiritual vibrations. One of the good things in jail was that there were no distractions by any material things. Some time bad things happen in our lives for good.
One time we did Akhanda Sadhana for twenty four hours. We used to sit in groups and each group would sit for three hours minimum. If someone wanted to stop for a long period, there were no restrictions. It was a wonderful experience. We only had very simple drinks or snacks, we didn’t take a heavy meal. Most of the jail guards respected us after watching our conduct, spiritual practices and having a sentient diet.
## Complete Peace
After six months of my arrest it was difficult to live in jail. But on the other side I was very much charged with spiritual energy. Many times when I started to sing a devotional song and I would forget myself, my voice was chocked up and complete peace descended on and I could not tell how long I had been there, unconscious of the outer world. After I came back to my senses everyone had finished their meditation and gone to bed and there was a plate of food left for me. I would eat it and go to bed. I had no watch. I have no idea how long I would remain in that state. I had the most beautiful experiences of spirituality when I was in jail and I always remember that.
After some time, they told me not to sing any devotional songs during meditation. Otherwise, I would go into this other world and disturb the singing and meditation. It was painful to accept that but as we were living collectively I accepted it to keep the flow.
## Counting Devotees
There was one guard that had the duty to count us every morning and evening. He was a bad guy. Sometimes he was rude. So we decided to teach him a lesson. When he came for the counting we had to sit in a row. Some times people would move from one place to another place. So the count increased and sometimes decreased. He couldn’t figure out what was happening. He looked to be a religious person and wore religious symbols, but his behavior was very bad. After some time, his behavior changed and we also stopped harassing him.
## The Guards
Some of the guards were so close to us that we could talk to them about anything. One day one guard came near to me I saw something in his pocket. Generally, their pockets were empty, so I asked him about it. He took out some potato and eggplants mixed with spices. He explained “You see we get very little money from the government so it is very hard to support our families. The big officers of the jail, like superintendent, superior guards and doctors steal all the good food, like fresh vegetables and fruits. We have no contact with contractors who bring food, but they do. So what I do when the prisoners are cooking is that I just pick up some of the vegetables and put them in my pocket to take home to my family.” I was totally shocked.
Many times we read in the newspapers that thousands of new blankets had been purchased for the prison. But when we talked to the prisoners and guards we found out that the officials in charge had already sold all the materials that were meant for us. This was a normal thing. They were not even providing the minimum ration proscribed by the government for prisoners in their care. So the prisoners were always hungry and cold. Many prisoners had to wait many years without trial since they were too poor to hire lawyers.
## Prison Protest
Once the prisoners became very angry and they started to protest against the authorities of the jail. The whole jail, all the prisoners were protesting. Luckily we were kept separate from ordinary prisoners. Whatever they found like bricks, stones, wood, they would use as weapons. Many of them went to the rooftops. For almost three days the siege went on. Finally, the police came with dogs and started to shoot. We don’t really know how many died or how many were injured but we heard many, many gunshots.
After that some of them were transferred to other jails. It was a horrible condition to see. However, the situation of the common prisoners and ordinary guards remained the same; with low quality food and clothes.
Prison should be made to reform people. Not to create a situation that converts an ordinary prisoner into a hard-core criminal. When an ordinary man has no hope he becomes a hard and desperate criminal.
## Laksameshevara Sharma
There was a margii, Laksameshevara Sharma, who used to collect vegetables for our center. He had a job during the day but almost every evening we would go and collect vegetables for different centers of the marga in Ranchi. The day the office got raided he had come to deliver vegetables to Baba’s house in Ranchi for the volunteers. He also got arrested merely because he was there.
All the extra money he would earn he would give to the organization, so he had no savings for his own family. His wife was illiterate and had four small children. She couldn’t get a job or do anything to feed the children. Sometimes this margii was very unhappy because he had no information about his family and how they were surviving without his help.
When Baba visited Ranchi, He visited his family. Baba told his wife that he shouldn’t have gone to His house. He knew the situation was tense so he should have avoided going there. He consoled her and told her that everything would be fine.
His wife survived by selling paper bags made out of newspapers for two years.
She was always worried that his husband would become a monk and leave her. Laksameshevara didn’t cut his hair and beard, as tantric monks do, so this sign would make her worried. Also, in India, many people that go to prison never return home out of shame.
One night she had a dream. Baba appeared in the dream and told her that her husband would never become a monk and would return home once he was released from jail.
One day, while we were in prison, I saw that Laksameshevara had shaved his hair and beard. He was shaved completely. I asked him why he had done this. He looked better with hair and beard. He told me that he had a dream and Baba ordered him to shave his hair and cut his beard. Although the order came from a dream he followed the order of his Guru.
After some time, his wife came to see him in the jail. She was surprised to see him shaven. They kept silent for some time and then started to talk. It was an emotional time after a long period of separation. He told her that Baba had ordered him to shave his hair and beard in the dream. And his wife told him that she had also had a dream and Baba had told her that her husband would never become a monk.
Although his family was going through hardship this incident gave a new love for their mutual mission and towards Baba. After that Laksameshevara’s unhappiness was soon gone. He had always been a very cheerful person, but financial concerns and worry over his family had made him disturbed.
I met him again after a long time, in 2006, in Ananda Nagar. Of course I was a sanyasin and he was a family man. Our meeting was wonderful. He laughed and laughed and laughed. He said, “Dada, now I am a free man. I stay in Ananda Nagar and my duty is to take care of the kiirtan mandap (hall). All my children are married. My wife is taken care of by my children. So I just live, meditate, do kiirtan and take care of this place.”
He took me to his room. His room was so small and he lived almost like a wandering monk. I was very happy to see him.
He told me, “I have only one desire. I would like to travel with you sometime in India.” I told him that I was very busy then. Also, I couldn’t move from Delhi because I was waiting for my green card approval. I couldn’t travel, but if I get a chance sometime, I will.
## Bhagavat Singh
There were three classes of prisoners in India but in our jail there were only two classes, the B class and C class. In the A class were the rich and top political and business leaders. They get good facilities with separate rooms and servants, cooks, nice food, good clothes. In the B class you would find prisoners who were university graduates, educated people and second tier officials. B class people shared a room but had their own bed and an assistant who could wash clothes and cook, they were respected in the jail and were free to move around. C class prisoners were the lowest ones. They didn’t get proper clothes or food. Jail officials stole most of their food and clothing.
There was a prisoner in the B class that was serving a life term with option of parole. His name was Bhagavat Singh.
There had been some fighting in his village and one person got killed by him and that was why he was there.
He was a very simple man. He was well educated and very intelligent. He learned Ananda Marga philosophies very fast. He also got initiated. After initiation he practiced his meditation very sincerely and also started to get realization. He became a very active member of the marga there. He brought some more people for initiation.
As I have said, we were thirty-three people, among them, six or seven were members of Communist party. They had joined Ananda Marga but they didn’t know the difference between Ananda Marga and the Communist ideology.
One day Bhagavat Singh was discussing with these communist members and he found out that some of them had no clue about Ananda Marga at all. He came to us and told to a senior Acharya, “How come these people are with you in jail and are communists and know Ananda Marga at the same time?”
Ananda Marga was infiltrated by communists, criminals and spies at that time. They did some bad things and brought a bad name to Ananda Marga. We must discriminate about who can be a member of Ananda Marga. It is my opinion that people who really want to practice spirituality, do social service and express their love for humanity should be able to join Ananda Marga.
## The Sharma Hotel
The jail’s priest was very angry and had big eyes. He looked like a devil. I couldn’t understand why the jail authority had made him the priest there.
I had seen him several times, but I didn’t know anything about him. One day he came to our room when we were meditating, it was on some Hindu religious day. He just came in and put something on our heads. When I opened my eyes to see what he was doing I saw his ferocious figure. He used to work at the Sharma Hotel.
People used to go to eat at the Sharma Hotel. Their staff looked very religious, they wore religious articles around their necks and on their clothes. This hotel was a higher caste hotel so mostly upper middle class used to go to eat there. People used to come from nearby villages and the local towns to eat there.
One day, one person from a nearby village said he would be going to eat at the Sharma Hotel and then return home, but he never came back home. His family came to look for him in town and finally went to the Sharma Hotel. They were able to recognize one of his shoes when they went to visit the hotel. They became suspicious and reported this to police.
When I was young I had heard about this hotel. People used to sing a song in Hindi – Narabadha Kupa Laheria Tola Sharma Hotel Gaya Me Khola – about an infamous well at Laharia Tola, in Gaya, that was used to dump dead bodies.
People had reported other incidents to the police before. There were stories about people finding human remains in their food at the hotel. But in India police are very relaxed. And they are so poorly paid that they don’t like questioning the hotel owners.
After several complaints the police raided the hotel. When they went to the backyard of the hotel they became aware of a stench. They found several wells filled with dead human bodies. Everyone responsible was arrested and put in jail. They were sentenced for life. I met two of them. One was the priest of the jail. The other one was a man who always kept silent.
This man who never spoke used to come every day to our place. He used to sit in a corner and sometimes a tear would come out from his eyes.
I think he was repenting when he saw the monks and spiritual people in our ward. Almost every day he came and sat in the corner. He gazed at us, wept, and went back to his place. He was very old at that time. I was surprised to see such a hard-core criminal. I had never expected in my life that I’d have to face such criminals as these.
## Fun in Jail
Jail was a difficult and boring place. The good thing about us was that we were living all together and were practicing sadhana and debating philosophies. However, we are all human and need entertainment.
Sometimes we would do an entertainment program in our room. We had a Margi brother, Chandra Bhan Singh. He had just come to Ranchi to visit Baba and he stayed longer than expected. He got arrested with the rest of us.
He was from a very educated family. He was a student in the Engineering College. He was large and bulky. His hair and everything in him was large. He was a very humorous person. He used to make a lot of jokes. Sometimes we asked him to dance because generally men don’t dance in India. Although he was large his body was very flexible. He actually knew traditional Indian dancing. When he started to dance everyone started to dance. He was so big and would hold his stomach in. We used to also sing a lot of songs that inspired our ideals of freedom, justice, exploitation and spiritual elevation.
## News from the Outside
In the month of October 1971, a defection happened in Ananda Marga. We got the news through the newspaper that Baba had removed his personal assistant from his post. That assistant was very close to Baba’s wife. He went and complained to Baba’s wife and told that her that he wanted to get his post back at any cost. She came before Baba and asked Him to give him back his position of personal assistant. To be the personal assistant of Baba was a very respectable post in Ananda Marga. Baba refused to accept her suggestion. She became upset. To put pressure on Baba she told him that if he didn’t give him his post back she would leave. Finally, she left Baba.
Now Baba’s wife and his former PA, Vishokananda, started to protest against Baba. Some of Vishokananda’s friends and Baba’s wife wanted to make an organization. In the end they didn’t succeed. They went to court and filed a case against Baba. We came to know these things when I was in jail. CBI had total control over his wife, Vishokananda and other people who had defected from Marga. CBI had become very active in trying to destroy Ananda Marga.
CBI was also trying to bribe people to leave Ananda Marga. Sometimes they would promise to give high positions in the government.
Baba also became very, very strict. He introduced new conduct rules he called the sixteen points. These covered every aspect of Ananda Marga. The main point of the sixteen points is to create moral and spiritual leaders, who can arouse devotion and serve humanity. We call this moral and spiritual leader sadvipra. Sadvipras are those people who are moralists and spiritualists and are ready to fight against injustice and exploitation to bring peace and prosperity to the world.
We were a little bit shocked by all this news. Every day new stories were coming out and many people got confused. Many people left the organization.
## Madhavananda Avadhuta
One day, we came to know that Madhavananda Avadhuta would be separated from us. During the jail time he was our main leader. He used to give very inspiring talks on many subjects. Also he used to keep everybody in discipline. It was shocking for us. The day he was taken away from our room we started to do kiirtan. Many of us wept.
We came to know that he had joined the other side of camp and became an approver. We came to know that he was heavily tortured. He was kept many days without food. Out of fear of death he accepted responsibility for everything the CBI told him to say. I have heard that Baba said he was a good boy, but for three days he did not get any food and water. If he had been able to stay without food and water he could have withstood them.
In Ananda Marga Baba has written that if an animal should not be starved to death. If you want to kill any dangerous animal, kill it, but don’t starve it. Hunger creates more pain than almost any other thing.
## Discipline
After some time, other Dadas were taken away from us. Soon most of the monks were either released or transferred from Gaya jail. Mostly ordinary members and some LFTs remained there. It was very difficult to maintain discipline but we tried. At least we kept doing regular collective meditations daily.
One day in the middle of collective meditation one of the members lay down on the floor and started to sleep. Some of us protested, we thought that if we allowed things like this to happen then total chaos would follow. We asked him to change his behavior. Of course there was no leader to guide us, we got divided into two groups. One camp wanted him to sleep and another camp wanted him not to sleep. Finally, we came to an agreement that if a person is very sick or tired he may sleep but he had to sleep separately not in the middle of a collective meditation. After that nobody dared to break the rules. Everybody joined collective meditation every day.
Sometimes protest is necessary when things are getting out of control. Most of the people in the world want to avoid discipline in life, but discipline is necessary in every walk of life. Discipline must come from within, but sometimes, external imposition is needed.
The mind moves in both directions upwards and downwards. It can flow upwards toward good thoughts, to serve, to help, to have compassion and love. But the mind may also move in downward direction, which is just the opposite, towards hate, vanity, anger, indiscipline, etc. We all have evolved from animal life so we still have animal tendencies. So people are attracted towards hatred jealously, anger, fear and other bad propensities of the mind.
## Dry Fasting
We used to fast while in prison, but fasting meant we had one meal a day. We used to eat three chapati, some vegetables and sweet rice. The sweet rice was one liter of milk with sugar and water for thirty-three people.
As Baba introduced the sixteen points, we came to know that we had to do fasting even without water. It was already very difficult for us to do fasting with food once a day. When we came to know that we had to do fasting without water for twenty-four hours we were terrified, but we started to fast. Not all members did fasting because there were many people who had no real understanding of Ananda Marga. Some had an extreme communist background.
Fasting without water in jail was especially difficult in summer. It was very hot. But some of us did fast for as long we stayed in the jail.
## Food While in Prison
We were all from India, but from different provinces. In India each state has its own kind of food. Sometimes even within a province there are different variations of dishes.
There was a brother who often complained to me about food. He didn’t like certain ways other people cooked, but he himself never cooked. I had to tell him that since we were in jail we had to adjust and learn to live with others. I told him, “Plus, you do not know how to cook. So what is the solution? Whatever is made you eat, otherwise learn to cook and you can cook for all of us.”
One day he complained about someone putting sugar in vegetables. In spicy vegetables where he was from no one used sugar. I said, try it, to see how it tastes. If it is very bad, we’ll all ask him to stop.
One day he was horrified and came to me. He said they were going to cook garbage. I was curious. He explained to me that when cutting vegetables, you were supposed to throw away the peel and fibrous excess and they were not doing it. I said this, “If you like it, eat. If you don’t, don’t eat.”
We all ate that special dish which was made from so-called garbage.
Actually we were hungry all the time, so we would eat anything that was edible.
## News from Buxar Jail, Baba’s Imprisonment
Varanasi is the oldest cultural city of the world. It is situated at the bank of Ganges River. Many saints, sages, scholars and artists, have lived in Varanasi.
Varanasi was also winter capital of Lord Siva, who lived 7,500 years ago. He used to live in Himalayas in summer time. Varanasi existed even before this, so there are at least 10,000 years of history there.
Baba ended up getting arrested and was sent to prison in 1971. He was arrested just before he was going to give a big New Year’s DMC in Varanasi.
The CBI thought that if they could arrest Baba just before DMC then the people would be very much disturbed and they would be able to destroy Ananda Marga very easily. So Baba was arrested and he was taken to Buxar jail. He would remain imprisoned for the next 7 and a half years.
After the emergency was over I came to know through someone who was a relative of an official at the CBI that before putting Baba in jail they had sent a murder to kill Baba. The murderer arrived to where Baba was staying, he saw Baba walking on the ceiling, he got frightened and left. He tried a second time, but when he got inside Baba’s room there was nobody inside, He had disappeared. Again the same murderer tried to kill Baba and something else happened which stopped him from committing the act.
Then the government decided it was the right time to prosecute Baba because He would have no one to defend him but a lawyer. The defense lawyer pleaded his case strongly. Baba was sentenced to life imprisonment with four other members of the Marga.
One day some prisoners from Buxar jail were transferred to our prison in Gaya. They told us a story about Baba.
The wife of the head of the prison had a dream on the night of Baba’s imprisonment. In her dream she saw that a great man would be coming to the jail. When she woke up she went to the market and bought a lot of fruits, milk, butter, ghee and other items to give these as presents to that great man.
Later that day she told her husband the dream. He confirmed they had someone special imprisoned. He told her, “He is the founder of Ananda Marga and his name is Baba, so if you want to give these things to him you can.”
The head of the jail told this story to his staff and it proceeded to spread like a wildfire throughout the whole jail. From that moment Baba was very much respected in the jail.
## Complete His Mission, Even If I Have to Do It Alone
It was March 31st 1973. We got a message that we were going to be released from the jail. But not all of us. Some of our brothers began to cry and weep because of the uncertainty of leaving jail.
I felt very bad. I was going to be released that day and I was thinking I wouldn´t mind if someone else was released in my place. But that was not possible. People were waiting outside to take us from the jail.
A prison official wanted some money if we wanted to be released that day in spite of the court order, which allowed us to leave. We came to know that the jail official was bribed and we were able to leave jail.
That was the situation of corruption in India. I think someone paid, which is against the rules of Ananda Marga, because otherwise they could’ve made other excuses and kept us there indefinitely. Nobody cared about the law there.
After our release we spent the night in the Ananda Marga school in Gaya. There we met a family Acharya whose name was Acharya Sukhadeva Dharmamitram, he had already initiated 10,000 people. I was very happy to see him. I had known his name for a long time but I had never met him.
Just two months before our release, in February 1973, Baba had been poisoned in prison. He was given more than enough poison to kill a man but he somehow survived. He demanded for an inquest into his poisoning and began fasting in support of this demand. The demand was never met, so he continued his fast for the next five years until he was released from prison. Why was the demand never met? The reason is simple; it was the government itself that was involved in the poisoning.
Around this time Acharya Divyananda Avadhuta committed self-immolation as a way of protest against Baba’s imprisonment. Many Senators from the US, many MPs from Europe, Amnesty International and other organizations had pressed for an inquiry but the Indian government did nothing. To draw the attention this Dada decided to self-immolate.
Around this time, I attended a big protest in Patna to demand an enquiry on Baba’s case. After a while everyone went back home but I stayed there for a little longer.
In the protest there was a Dada whom I knew. He was in charge of the area where I had been initiated. When he found out I was there he came to meet me and told me to go back home. He also made an official order to put in the central office that I should not be allowed to be a Dada (monk). I said to him, “My Guru is in jail, I must become a Dada to serve the society. I don’t' know whether my Guru will be released or killed, so I must become a Dada and complete his mission even if I have to do it alone.” This strong feeling came because I had read the Bible while in prison and I remembered how at the eleventh hour all of the disciples of Christ betrayed him when he was arrested by the Romans.
I said, “I don’t care if I will be hanged with my Guru, but if I am asked, I will say I am a member of Ananda Marga.” That was my determination. But that Dada did not care. He told me I had to go home, and that the central office would not allow me to become a Dada. “I will see to it,” he said.
The reason was that he was afraid of my family. My family had connections with the ruling party that had put us in jail and he wished to be free of their harassment. I went to request from several Dadas for a recommendation letter so that I could go to a training center and become monk of Ananda Marga, but the central office made sure everyone knew that I was supposed to go back home and not become a monk. Nobody was to recommend me. Day and night I was crying. I thought, “I could go back home, but my Guru is suffering in jail, that is not right.” Moreover, I had promised to become a monk the day I got initiation. Both things were haunting me.
Finally, I met one Dada, he had been with me in jail. His name was Dharmameghananda Avadhuta. He was the treasurer of Ananda Marga. I went to him to ask for his recommendation. I told him, “I was in prison with you. There, you people inspired us to work for Baba. Now there are small problems and everyone becomes afraid.” I told him I wouldn’t tell anybody, he just had to sign the recommendation and I would leave immediately and nobody would know. He was a simple man and he wrote it. I took the letter and I headed to the training center in Varanasi.
## 50,000 Monks
Actually, this was the second time I went to the training center in Vanarasi with a recommendation letter in my hands. I was in Daltonganj around 1969 when a new Dada came to the training center there. He had attended DMC recently where Baba had asked for 50,000 monks and nuns. He encouraged me to become a monk since Baba needed 50,000 monks.
I told him that my Acharya had told me that I had to wait until I had turned 18. He said, “You can lie for the great cause.” So I did not argue and took the train and went to the Varanasi training center. The trainer was Acharya Prajinanda Avadhuta. When I arrived I gave him the recommendation letter. He looked at me and told me “You are going to become a monk, so tell me the truth, what is your age? Tell me your age. Don't lie, tell me your birthdate.” I told him my birthdate and he became very upset. He told me to leave immediately. So I went back to Daltonganj.
The Dada who had sent me asked me about what had happened. I said “You told me to lie and he told me to tell the truth. I told the truth.”
## Training in Varanasi
When I turned 18 I went for training. I had seen the original training center in Varanasi, it was in the middle of the city. Now they had moved the center to another place. The place where I was now heading to was completely away from town in Chitaipur Village. The trainers there where Acharya Japananda Avadhuta and Acharya Niramalananda Avadhuta. Of course now Chitaipur has become like a small town and our training center is surrounded by many houses.
There was hardly anyone around our training center back then. Only one other house in front of our center was standing. That house was in very bad shape but still people were living there.
Our training center was called Chitaipur Court. It was an old building. This building was huge; it had two floors and a courtyard. There was also a meeting hall. But it was so old that sometimes we were afraid that the roof could fall down at any time on our heads. We heard a story about the house that whoever came to stay here died. The house was occupied by mice and there were several big trees outside that were occupied by vultures. We were scared to live in that building but we had no choice.
For drinking water, we had a very deep well. For the latrine we used the field because the latrine was unstable and it was in front of a big ditch so everyone was afraid to use it. We used to go take baths in a small pond with the water buffalo. There were people there washing their clothes. It was very muddy water. I don't know whether we were getting clean there or getting dirty but it still felt good in summertime, which had just arrived.
Our breakfast was mostly cracked wheat (dalia) porridge with salt. I had never eaten this kind of plain wheat porridge in my life. For some days I took just a few spoonfuls but after that I had to eat it because there was nothing else to eat and I was very hungry. Once in a while we had chickpeas soaked in water with a little bit of molasses. That was our breakfast for ten months while I stayed in the training center. It was a hard life.
For lunch we had some rice and maybe one or two chapatis, watery dal and watery vegetables. If you found a piece or two of vegetables, you were happy. For dinner we had chapati and some kind of soupy, simple spicy vegetable. That was our life at the training center.
At that time this was the only training center, so there were many non-Indian trainees from the USA, Europe, Singapore, Philippines, etc. They had the same kind of food but they used to get a little bit extra. We used to give them boiled soya beans for protein, fried with a little oil and salt.
There was a grave for Muslims and a temple for Hindus nearby the training center. Sometimes people would come to both these places and leave some sweets as an offering. Often, the trainees would go and take the sweets.
## Memories of Acharya Dineshvaranada
A few days after my arrival at the training center there was a second self-immolation by a monk. His name was Acharya Dineshvaranada Avadhuta. I was very close to him. He used to come to our unit in Daltongaj. At that time he was a Brahmacharii. He used to put white shirt we called Kurta and white dhoti. He also gave me third level initiation to me.
Once we were sleeping outside it was summer. It was very hot inside in the ashram. We woke up in the morning and did our sadhana but we couldn't find his shirt. He had only one shirt. Some thief stole it during the night. He had to go someplace to give a talk. How could he go without a shirt? Luckily I had a white kurta and it fit him. He was very happy. I told him, “Dada, minimum you should have two pairs of clothes.”
Although he was handicapped because of problems in one of his legs, he was a very brave person. Once some people who were against Ananda Marga were distributing a pamphlet against Ananda Marga. He alone went in the crowd and snatched all the leaflets and the sticks they were carrying with them. When he told me the story I couldn’t imagine where he had gotten that strength from. Now I know that he was doing the work of God and Guru, so God and Guru gave him the strength.
I was very much shocked to know this news that but it inspired me for greater dedication and love for Guru.
## CBI Still Harasses Us
Soon after this thing happened, the police raided the training center. They wanted to harass us everywhere. Dada Japananda, one of our trainers, told me to hide somewhere because I had just come back from jail. So I and a few other trainees went and hid. Some of us were taken to the police station but they were released the same day.
## Fasting in the Training Center
Fasting in the training center was very difficult. First of all, we did not get any nutritious food. Also it was very hot in the summer.
After fasting we got lemon water with salt. If we were lucky, we used to get a small a banana. If we were really lucky we would get a good one that was not already half rotten. After fasting we also ate rice porridge. It was so watery that you could drink it.
Once I had a lot of pain in my stomach during fasting. Our trainer came to know I was crying because of the pain. He gave me some homeopathic medicine and the pain lessened.
He told me not to fast as long as I was staying in the training center. He said that I had suffered a lot in jail and I was very weak. I followed his instructions for the next one or two times. I thought to myself, “When I was in jail I could do fasting there, then why can't I fast here?” I went to Dada and told him that I was going to fast. I did fasting for as long as I stayed in the training center.
## Studying at the Training Center
We didn't have the luxury of electricity in the training center. We had a few small kerosene lamps that gave off very little light. We had only one or two lanterns which were for the trainer and the office. It was very difficult to study at night. Classes were given in the day once or twice a week. Mostly it was self study.
We were supposed to learn about the scriptures and philosophy of all the religions. Also basic knowledge of science, geography and history of the world and learn at least four languages. Of course we also had to learn about the social and spiritual philosophy of Ananda Marga. It was also a part of training to know things like self-defense, first aid, cooking, music, etc.
Plus, we had to learn trade training specialties, which comprised education, arts, social affairs, prevention of cruelty to plants and animals, farming, public speaking, disaster relief and a wide range of other things, but we just learned very basic things in the training center.
Baba wanted us to go to the field with whatever we had learned and to teach others by doing. Baba wanted to just throw seeds everywhere. In every sphere of life Baba wanted change. There were a lot of different departments to establish a better world. For example, he made a department to prevent the extinction of plant and animal species. He had created all these to bring a complete and total change in all areas of human life, whether it was physical, intellectual, spiritual, cultural, political, moral, etc. He wanted to overhaul the society.
## Non-Indian Devotion
One of the many interesting things for me was to see these non-Indian trainees who used to play guitar and just sing one or two lines of spiritual songs. They would sing things like “Ananda Murti, Ananda Murti, Ananda Murti, Parama Pita, Parama Pita, Parama Pita” with nice melodies. They would do this for hours and would seemed to be very happy.
I could see in their faces how much love they had for Baba. They would also sing kiirtan beautifully while playing guitar. To see their devotion is something that got imprinted on my mind.
Once baba was talking with me and asked if I had some friends from outside of India. I had been discussing with Baba about devotion. I told Him, “They have great devotion, Baba.”
## Sadhana Piitha Training
One of the most important things in the training center was to complete SPT (Sadhana Piitha Training). When I did it the first time I almost got killed and then Baba saved me, so I had a bitter-sweet memory about it. But anyway, I had to do it again to become a monk.
Varanasi is a very religious city so there are always wandering monks, sadhus and sanyasins around. In SPT you live similarly to some of those sadhus. You have to cook your own food, collect your own firewood, and live with minimum clothing.
I didn't have many problems during these trainings. But for others it was a bit difficult. We had to collect firewood, beg for food and cook in clay pots. And of course, people from Europe and America, were not habituated to cook food like that. There were no exceptions, everyone had to do it. I saw them struggling to collect twigs, leaves and branches of trees to make fire. But people were inspired by devotion so they were ready to do anything and everything for their ideology.
## To Serve Humanity
The environment of a training center is beautiful because from morning to evening you are immersed in a spiritual flow. From the moment you get up you have to do Guru Sakas and panchajanya. At least we had to do collective meditation and kiirtan three to four times a day. We had tremendous spiritual energy within us.
Moreover, when in a training like this, the mind is not distracted because you have to remain in a compound and study philosophy and work. I always loved to be in training centers. Baba blessed me that in many occasions I got an opportunity to be LFT trainer and Whole Timer trainer.
One of the most important things in the training center is to follow routine and discipline. Everyone had to get up around four o'clock in the morning and retire at eleven at night. The people who had come to serve had no problems with routine and discipline, but those who thought that the monk life was easy had some problems.
## Dada Dasarath
The time for the first examination came. I always remained busy and had no time for studying, so I was not ready to take the test. Everybody was going to sit for exam but I was not. I felt a bit sad, but at the same time I felt that work was more important than exams.
Some of my brother trainees came and asked me to take the exam. They said it didn’t matter if I passed it or not, if I failed, at least I would gain some experience. I was hesitant but I sat for the exam. Our examiner was a family Acharya Dasarath. For the examination he asked me only one question. It was about samskara. I replied very simply and I was declared passed. It surprised everyone.
One of my friends who had been studying day and night failed. When he saw me he began to complain and then to weep. I tried to console him but he was angry and disturbed. He actually ended up finishing the training and became a monk. But he left monkhood soon after he had been posted to work somewhere, he could not handle the hardships of monk life.
When I was in the Varanasi training center Dada Dasarath told one story to us about Baba. Dada said that he used to do Satsaunga Pranam (prostration) after every meditation. One day he did this and fell asleep. He then heard a voice, it was Baba, and He was saying, “Wake up, wake up!” Dada all of a sudden woke up and tried to find Baba in his room but couldn’t find Him. He explained that his Guru was watching everything at every moment of his life. Dada Dasarath also had the power to see others minds. He could see whether someone was fit or unfit to have personal contact with baba. Without his clear recommendations nobody was allowed to have personal contact with baba.
## My Family Tries to Bring Me Back
Just a few days after my arrival from Patna to the Varanasi training center my family members and relatives came looking for me. Actually, my maternal uncle didn’t live very far away from the training center and other relatives lived around there too. I saw four persons and one of them was my father. That time we were doing kiirtan in the open courtyard. I saw my family and relatives. I had to take a quick decision otherwise they would take me away from the training center and I would never become a monk.
I was sure they would lock me up in their house this time. When I saw them, there were about fifty people doing kiirtan. Slowly, while I kept dancing, I went to a door which would take me to the backside of the house. I thought they hadn’t noticed me in the crowd. I ran away from the training center because I felt it was the only way to escape. I ran and ran as fast as I could.
When I had run for a mile or so, I saw some trainees running behind me calling after me. I slowed down and I was a bit afraid. “Don't worry”, they said. They just wanted to know where I would be hiding so they could let me know when my family had left. So I hid myself somewhere in the bushes for an entire day. When the night fell I returned to the training center. My family had left after having searched the entire building for me.
When I met the trainees from the Philippines they started to laugh, I couldn’t understand why. So it happens than there was a trainee named Yogavrata from the Philippines. My father had embraced him and started to cry and asked him come back home thinking that this boy was me. He was so confused by then. Of course my father hadn't seen me for nearly two years but through a tiny hole in the jail door and it was difficult to recognize me by then.
## Become a Monk, or Kill Myself
After the training was over we had to send a letter to the local police of our districts to inform that we were going to leave the training center. Also we had to write a letter to our parents informing them that we had become monks. I was afraid to write my parents for fear that would try to come after me again. And it happened.
One of my trainers was Nirmalananda. He was a highly intellectual person but he had certain bad habits that I knew about since our times as margiis together. That made him dislike me.
Without a word to me he handed me over to my family. I was very upset. I thought to myself, “Why did he keep me for a year just to do this to me?”
I met my father and I touched his feet. I also met my brother in law and some other relatives. My father told me all the bad news about home. He told me that my eldest brother was almost killed because of his involvement in politics but was in the hospital.
My brother was traveling nearby our house on bicycle and some people attacked him with knives and a spear and threw him near the road thinking he was dead. By chance his friend doctor Awadheshaprasad Sinha saw him on the road, took him to the hospital and saved his life. It was only luck that my brother survived.
Also, my house had been robbed and my second brother was not in good health. The entire family was disturbed. He wanted me to go home to help look after the property and family.
With all this there was no way to convince them, so something came in my mind. I took my brother in law to one side and I told him that my Guru was in jail. I further told him, “He came to establish a better world but he might get killed for no reason, so this is a very important time for me to become a monk and complete his mission. I have two things in my life now, either I will become a monk or I will kill myself, which would you prefer?” My brother in law put his hand on my mouth and said, “If you want to become a monk, do it. Don't kill yourself, we can take care of the family. I will convince your father.”
After a time, they left the training center. They knew that once I had decided to do something I would do it.
The next day the training center was surrounded by fifty people from the area. I didn't know how to deal with it. I went on the top of the second floor of the building, very high up. I said, “Yesterday I told you that I would become monk or kill myself. Now you have brought all these people to take me away from here. So I am going to jump. The police will come and they will have you arrested.”
With that, fifty percent left immediately. I got nearer to the edge and repeated what I had said, another quarter left. I again got nearer to the edge and spoke to them, so the rest left, only leaving me and my family there.
I threatened my family from the very edge of the rooftop, and then they finally left. It was very dramatic. It was only by this extreme measure that I was able to become a monk.
## After Training
After completing training in Varanasi, everybody had to go to the central headquarters. Although our central head quarters were in Ananda Nagar, due to disturbances, me and 3 other trainees went to Kolkata along with Dada Pranavananda. At the time Dada was the office secretary of Ananda Marga as well as the trainer for Acharyas.
It was the beginning of 1974. We were sent to different places in Bengal for field training.
I visited Howrha, Ulveria, Amta, Besirhat, Berdwan, Nuwbrakpur, as well as our school in Kolkata. I had learned Bengali language in Gaya jail. This proved to be very helpful when communicating with the people of Bengal.
## A Rich Devotee
During these days I went to a village near Amta. In that village there was a poor margii, he was very, very poor. I had never seen such a poor man in my life. The people in Amta gave me his address so I went to his house. I stayed in a mud house and took my bath in the morning nearby in a small pond.
I had arrived in the night. This margii had arranged my dinner at Dada Amrtinanda’s maternal grandparent’s house. Dada was a senior monk of Ananda Marga whom I knew quite well. They made a very nice dinner for me. After dinner I talked in my broken Bengali to his maternal uncles and whatever other relatives were there. Later I was brought back to sleep in the poor man's house.
In the morning again I was supposed to go to this same house for lunch but there was some miscommunication. In the afternoon the poor man got very worried. Now he collected some rice and put some yellow powder, salt and cheese on it. He cooked it and gave it to me. Seeing the situation of his family I told him to remove some portion of food from my plate. But he sadly said, “Dada, this food is not tasty, that is why you are not going to eat it all.” I said, “No, I know that you don't have food in the house and you have children too, how can I eat this alone?” Then he said, “Please eat as much as you want, the rest of the food we will share if any remains.” I was forced to eat alone and after I ate they had their share.
The love that this poor man showed towards me will be printed forever in my life. He was poor in material wealth but his heart was very, very rich.
In most of the places were I went people were very nice to me. Weather they were acharyas or margiis, they always tried to help me.
## Ananda Marga Barber
There is a funny story about a margii who was also from Amta who was by profession a barber. Whenever a customer came to him he would try to tell them about Ananda Marga philosophy. It is said that sometimes he would shave the beard of a man half way, that is only one side, and wait until he finished his talk before shaving the other side.
This same man is the margii who took me to the Ananda Marga school to stay there overnight when I first arrived to Amta. He also brought dinner, puffed rice and a sweet called Bundi.
I had never eaten such a dinner in my life. I had no choice though. Moreover, he had brought these things to me with devotion so I took these things gratefully and slept in the school.
## Subirnanda
I also went to a place called Basirhat where my old jail mate margii was living. He was still a margii and I had become a monk. He was one of our cooks in the jail. We used to make Bengali dishes. He was very happy to see me. When he was arrested he was a university student. Later he would become Baba's bodyguard. His name was Subirnanda. I met him several times during Baba's lifetime. The last time I met him was in Kolkata in 2006. It had been a long time since the last time we saw each other.
## First and Second Posting
After this field training I was posted in Bhopal, Madhya Pradsh in central India as a Diocese Secretary and principal of the Ananda Marga school in Bhopal. But our Regional Secretary told me to go to Betul Diocese instead of Bhopal. He told me that Betul was a small place so I could take care of that place easily.
The Diocese Secretary of Betul was Acharya Shuddhakanta Brahmacarii. I had known him from my margii times because he was posted in my unit. He was a more senior acharya than me and I knew him well.
I did not care whether I went to Bhopal or Betul, I had no preference. I was a missionary worker who wanted to work for Baba. I went to Betul and worked there for a month.
After one month we had IRSS, Inspection-Review-Structure-Solidarity, there I submitted my report of the Betul Diocese, about school, initiations, and other work.
My report reached Baba in prison. Of course when Baba looked at the report he found out that I was posted in Betul. He was furious. He said “I had posted Acharya Iishvarakrsna Brahmacarii in Bhopal. Who made this change?” Baba asked his General Secretary, the ERAWS secretary and the Education Secretary. None of them new about the change in my posting. After further investigation it was found that the regional secretary of Jaipur region had made the transfer. He was immediately removed from the post of Regional Secretary and transferred to Nagpur as a Diocese Secretary. I was not informed of all these things at that time and only found out about it years later.
When I went for the next IRSS the central representative asked me to go to Bhopal which was my original posting. I took charge of the Bhopal Diocese and the Ananda Marga school.
## Rudrananda, the Fixer and Sukla
Before my posting my predecessor was Acharya Rudrananda Avadhuta. One day I was in town and a man met me and asked me about him. He told me that he was the bondsman of this Dada. I had no idea about the incident he was referring to. He told me, “Dada was arrested and I got him released from Bhopal jail. But now he escaped and I am in trouble, please give me some information about him.” In those days many senior monks were getting arrested.
When I came back to the school I talked to the chairman of the school of Ananda Marga about it. He narrated the whole thing to me.
He told me that Acharya Rudrananda Avadhuta had been arrested and put in prison. It was difficult to get him out of jail and they were not in a position to bail him out. They had hired a fixer that somehow arranged for his release. After his release he escaped. It was a dangerous situation for him to be in prison and he had little or no chances of getting out because he was one of the top figures in Ananda Marga in those days.
There was a person whose surname was Sukla. Sukla and his family were very close to Acharya Rudrananda Avadhuta. Sukla was a margii and his younger brother was an Ananda Marga monk. He was very tall and strong. He was also one of the labor union leaders. He heard about this fixer coming to talk to me.
Mr. Sukla came to me and told me, “If you meet the fixer again, send him to me.” Next time I went to town I met this man again. Again he was asking about Acharya Rudrananda. I told him that I knew nothing about him, where he had gone or where he lived, but that there was a man who wanted to meet him and perhaps he would have some information.
Finally, he met with Sukla. After meeting Sukla he never bothered me again. I came to know from Sukla that he had told this man that he had already taken his cut. He also told him, “Don’t bother this young principal of the school. If you disturb him, you will be in trouble.”
## Running The Bhopal School
The Ananda Marga school in Bhopal was very popular with the middle class. Even the lower middle class would send children to our school because it was affordable.
It was a private school. We did not take government aid. It was run on tuition fees. It was located in security lines of BHEL a huge electrical facility. Most of the children were the sons and daughters of the BHEL employees. Also the children of most of the police officers, the police men and the intelligence department of the area were studying in our school.
I used to work day and night to maintain this school. It was running in two shifts.
I had to get up at 4 am and go to bed around midnight. Our bathroom and kitchen were in one of the classrooms so I had to finish my bath, breakfast and meditation before the children arrived in the morning.
From 7 am to 5 pm I was visiting the school either as a teacher or looking after the office because we didn’t have enough staff. I also had to visit the children’s parents. This is one of the most important points in Ananda Marga school system, to have good relations between the school staff and the children’s guardians. That way any kind of problem can be tackled between these.
So in the evening after 5 pm I would go to met with the parents and I would come back after 9 pm. After that I would do sadhana, take my dinner and finish up the remaining office business.
Moreover, I used to sell books and stationary for the children to get a little extra income to support other missionary workers. I had always three or four monks around me. Either they had no school or no way to support themselves financially or were provincial workers who didn’t have a place to stay and eat.
Slowly and gradually I learned to teach and run the administration of the school. I was there just for one year. During this period all central workers came to visit.
I really enjoyed teaching children. Their ages were between four and fourteen.
## What to do?
One day one of the boys came to my office and told me that his parents wanted to meet me. That day I was extremely busy. I told him I could not come. He was a four or five years old boy. He used to come with his younger brother and his cousins. He started to weep in my office. I had no choice. I told him I would be free at 9 pm and would arrive at his house at that time. I went there.
The house was totally silent, no one was talking to each other, the boy’s father was sitting on one side of the room and his wife on the other. When I arrived I asked, “What is going on here, why is everyone so quiet?” The boy’s father broke the silence. He told me, “You see, I go to work every day and don’t have time to look after my children. My wife looks after my children. Now she’s complaining that the children aren’t studying at home but want to go out and play. On the other hand, the children are complaining that they do not go out to play enough as they study most of the time at home. So I am standing between my children and wife and I can’t sort this out. We thought maybe you could. That is why my youngest son, Ravi Shankar told you to come here.”
Now everybody started talking at once. I politely asked them to speak only one person at a time so that I could understand the problem. Everybody was complaining about the other. I listened and gave my verdict. It was very simple. I said, “Let us make a routine from morning to evening from getting up to going to bed.” So we made a routine for the whole day. I said that nobody should go against this verdict, neither the parents or the children. Everybody was satisfied and happy, so I left.
## Ok, Let’s Go to School
There was a boy, he was four years old. He always liked to escape from school. When the teacher was occupied, he’d always sneak off. His parents would come to my office and complain every day. It was a big problem.
One day I was teaching in his classroom and he escaped. I just followed him. I ran to his home. He was with his mother and his mother started to complain. She said, “He escaped from the school again.” I said, “No, he escaped from my class.” So I asked the child, “Why did you leave the school my little boy?” He said “I want to take a bath.” I told him he could have a bath at the school. Then he changed his mind. He said, he wanted to use the toilet. Like this he made many excuses and for every one I had an answer. Finally, he said, “I want to go to school.” I said, “Ok, let’s go to school.” After that he never escaped from school again.
Sometimes you have to give the children what they want as long as it is not harmful, then they will follow the teacher.
## State of Emergency
In India there were many demonstrations and protests going on in 1974 and 1975 against corruption, illiteracy and government influence over the judiciary. India had become independent in 1947 but the people of India remained poor and illiterate. Everywhere there was corruption.
There was a leader called Jay Prakash Narayan. He was a socialist kind of leader and he had declared total revolution. Most of the opposition party in India were supporting his movement. India was heating up.
Ananda Marga was protesting against the government for the injustice done to our Guru. We wanted to have a judicial inquiry for His poisoning in jail. The government did not care. Already there had been several self immolations and demonstrations by Ananda Marga in India and outside of India.
In the meantime, a socialist leader whose name was Mr. Raj Narayan, and that had contested the election against Indira Gandhi in Allahabad filed a case against Indira Gandhi for having misused her power to win the election. The High Court judge gave a verdict against Indira Gandhi. It was around the end of June. Seeing the situation Indira Gandhi declared a State of Emergency in India.
For much of the Emergency, most of Gandhi's political opponents were imprisoned and the press was censored. Several other human rights violations were reported from the time.
The day the emergency was declared I had some official work so I went to Kolkata to meet with some central workers. As soon as I arrived at our headquarters that day, while I was still outside of the office on the road, some members of Ananda Marga told me to leave immediately. All the central workers had been arrested by the government. That morning I had read in the newspaper that many top politicians of India had been arrested but there was no mention of Ananda Marga.
I had to make a quick decision. First of all, I wanted to know whether our sisters (nuns) were safe or not. I didn’t know their address. I found a rickshaw puller and asked him if he knew where our sister’s headquarters were. He said, “Yes I know, I can take you there.” Some Didis were there and I wanted to inform them that all the central workers had been arrested and that they might get arrested as well. At first they were angry, because monks are not supposed to visit the nun’s place. But I explained why I was there and they understood. They gave me a little food. I requested if they could get me a white shirt and they did. I removed my orange monk uniform and dressed with white pants and the white shirt. Then I left for the train station.
I had two things in my mind. Number one, I thought to myself that I wasn’t an important figure and hadn’t done any political activity, like participating in public demonstrations and the like. Why would they bother arresting me? Second thing was, if they arrest me it is better that they arrest me at my post. So I took the train to Bhopal.
When I arrived to Allahabad I had to change to another train but it was overcrowded. There was no place at all to even enter the train. I really wanted to go back to Bhopal as soon as possible. Luckily two porters came and they said they could help me get on the train. I said, “How?” Their idea was that one would be holding my head and the other my legs through the whole journey. But they wanted me to pay them for this service. And I did. When I got on the train everybody was shouting, “There is no room, why did you come?”
I arrived to Bhopal around 2 am, I slept at the bus stand and at 4 am I woke up because there were a lot of people trying to catch a bus. Luckily I got a three wheeler auto rickshaw and I went to the school at Bhopal. I had just removed my clothes and I was going to wash and take a bath. I was wearing just a t-shirt and a white lungi. I heard some sounds outside the building. I came out, it was the police chief talking with his assistant. I greeted them. These policemen were actually worried about their children, who attended the school at that time.
It was around 5:30 am when a police van came with two CBI officials. They told me not to move that I was under arrest. I said, “Look, I am a principal. I don’t even have on proper clothes.” I had to follow them. They didn’t listen to anything I had to say. I went in the police van. They didn’t say anything to me. The CBI officials were sitting in the front and I in the back.
## Skulls, Daggers and Bombs
They took me to another Ananda Marga school about two miles away in a small village in Ananda Nagar. Nobody was living there and no one was there because it was the summer holidays. Then they took me back to the school where I was the principal. In this way seven times they came and went between this school and the one in Ananda Nagar.
Around 10 am they found one other person who had long hair and a beard in my school and he also got arrested. His name was Divyajyoti Brahmacharii. We were brought to the Govindapura police station. The parents of most of the children at my school worked at this station!
I was called in a room where the same two CBI officials were sitting. They told that they had been sent from Delhi to arrest me. They said, “We know that you have been giving training in your school to the children to make bombs and use different kinds of arms.” I said, “Look some children are from this police station. Why don’t you call these children and ask them if I have taught these things to them?” They said, “No, no, we have all our information we don’t need to ask them. We want to ask you.”
I had never in my life learned how to make a bomb or how to use arms, much less considered giving such training to children! It was a totally false allegation. It had no base. Now one of them was getting irritated and using harsh words. He threatened me. I stood up and rolled up the sleeves of my t-shirt and told them, “Look, I am an innocent monk. I have done no crime in all in my life. My family, my father, my mother, my brother, my sister, all have cried for me. I have given up everything. I did not listen to their crying. But look, what will happen to you and your family if you kill an innocent monk? You know the bricks have ears and one day they will tell the story of my death. And think about your family if you kill a monk. Because Karma never leaves. There is a reaction for every action.” The second one said to his partner, “He is the principal of a school, you don’t talk harshly to such a person.”
It was tactics on their part, soft and hard. And then they told me, “Ok, you have done no crime, but your Guru has committed crimes.” I said, “If a father commits a crime does the son get punishment? Is there such a rule? If my Guru has done something you have to ask him, not me.” I went further. I told them, “Nobody changes his father even if his father has committed a crime. He is irreplaceable. Similarly, I am not going to change my Guru even if he has committed a crime. Although I know he hasn’t. He has only helped people. He has inspired hundreds of thousands of people around the world to do something good for society.” After that they asked me no more questions.
Someone had left some skulls in my school. In Ananda Marga Avadhutas use skulls to do a special meditation on the night of new moon so they brought these skulls. I think there were four of them. They brought out the skulls and a small knife and put them in front of me and took pictures. Next day it came out in the newspapers that an Ananda Marga school teacher was arrested with skulls and daggers. Back then I was just a Brahmachari and I really had no idea what use the skulls and dagger had.
## Bhopal Central Jail
It was almost evening and we were taken to Bhopal central jail. Before I was going to enter in the jail campus a policeman came to me. I think he was a friend of one of the parents of the school children. He told me “God has saved your life. They took you to the police station to shoot you and wanted to spread the rumor that you had come to blow up the radio station and you got killed in a shoot out.” There was a tear in his eyes. The policeman said, “May God protect you sir.”
They took Divyajyoti Brahmachari and myself to jail. We were brought to a place where all the top political leaders of the state, social workers and religious workers were present. That day the number was very few. Soon after we were brought to a big hall and were all put in two places in the same campus. In our hall there were around seventy prisoners. We all slept on the floor. It was overcrowded. There were all kinds of people. Some were fundamentalist Muslims. Some were Moderate Muslims. Some were fundamentalist Hindu. Some were political Hindus. Some were liberal Hindu. Some were Marxists. Some Socialists. Some were just criminals. All were in the same hall.
There was a person who had fought against the British and was sentenced to death, in the meantime India got independence and he was released. His name was Maganlal Bagadi. He was very old, around eighty or eighty-five. He used to sleep outside of our hall.
In the Bhopal jail were us two monks of Ananda Marga and two members who were family men. One was Bhagawandin Ranjan who was a bank manager. The other one was Horilal Brokar who was an engineer. Mr. Brokar’s wife was expecting her first baby. There son was born when he was in jail. We all four used to do meditation in the hall where there was a stage, we were surrounded by other political prisoners. Below the stage level there were Muslims and they used to do their prayers there. Now some Muslims were fundamentalists, some were socialists and some were very liberal, but they used to pray together although they had dfferent political backgrounds. Now next to the Muslims were Hindus and they used to pray every day. There was one group called Arya Samajii they used to go out and sing some songs and recite a famous prayer.
It was very interesting to see that all human beings can live together and pray together which is very difficult if you were outside of jail. We had sometimes discussions and debates in the day and some entertainment programs in the evening. But the entertainment was only after we had heard news from BBC. We did not trust Indian news any more because it was totally controlled by the government. Every day we sat to listen to the BBC in Hindi. Only through here could we hear the news of India and the world. The newspapers were also controlled by the government. All the journalists who were supposed to be antigovernment had been closed down or had to be revised by the government.
We had no chance to go to court. Moreover, Ananda Marga and all its branches were banned. Of course there were many other social and political organizations that were banned as well, but of all of these organizations that were banned more than fifty per cent were related to Ananda Marga in one way or the other.
Regarding food, we four margiis had a separate kitchen. We used to cook our own food. We were given one assistant to help us. The rest of the political prisoners were eating together. Sometimes we invited some of the senior or elder leaders of each community within the jail and had lunch together.
The government of India was propagating that Ananda Marga people were murders and criminals through the radio newspapers and news reels at the film theaters. They were showing the skulls and daggers so people who didn’t know about Ananda Marga got frightened. The jail gave us a chance to meet all kinds of people of India in a way they came to know that Ananda Marga was a good organization. They could see that the followers of Ananda Marga are strong moralists and spiritualists. Most of the political prisoners became sympathetic to Ananda Marga at the jail. After we were released from the jail we came to know that many people got initiated into Ananda Marga while they were in prison.
## The Hindu Leader
One day the most senior leader of a political party (it was a Hindu party), asked me to join his group. He wanted to make me head of the religious branch. He tried to scare me saying that my Guru had already been sentenced for life and Ananda Marga was banned. “You will never come out of jail.”, he said. I told him, “This is our culture, and heritage. If the Guru is in trouble should his disciples betray Him and enjoy the luxuries of life? He told me, “We have a sanyasii heading our religious branch but he is not so strict in his spiritual practices. You are so young but you practice so sincerely and deeply every day your spirituality.” In the end he felt a little guilty because he knew the role of Guru and disciple.
## The Muslim Leader
On another occasion I was talking with some young Muslims, we were having just a general conversation. In the middle of it the senior Muslim leader came and sarcastically said to me, “Your life is like that of a cow and calf.” I told him I didn’t understand what he meant. He said, “When the calf grows he will get separated from his mother and father. The life of a monk is like that.” I got the point and and told him, “It is not you speaking but the Prophet Mohammad. He was completely shocked. He just went back and sat down.” I don’t know whether he was angry, shocked or surprised.
I kept talking to the young Muslim fellows. I had read the Koran in Hindi. I explained to them the Koran stated that Mohammad saw that all the good people in society were going to the caves to pray or meditate or do mystic things. He thought that if all the good people left then society would be dominated by bad people. I told them that was the reason he banned monkhood in Islam. I said to them, “I am a monk and I don’t live in a cave. Like this I have more time than to pray, meditate, and work for society. I ran a school and an orphanage, I worked with a disaster relief team and many other social projects. How many of you give your life to God in order to serve society? Everybody kept quiet. They said “You are right, you are doing more good work than us.” It was night so I went to bed.
This old man who was the head of inmate Islam was named Imamur Rahman. His son and one of his grandsons were also in prison with him. The next evening the old man came to me and broke down. He started to weep. He embraced me and told me, “You have more knowledge of the Koran than me. I misunderstood you, please forgive me for what I said to you last night.” I said, “There is nothing to forgive, it is just a misunderstanding. Some times we do not understand the deeper meaning of scripture.”
Of course I didn’t have much knowledge of the Holy book Koran and luckily I was able to answer that question because there was a footnote about not making monks in Islam. The Problem is that people become dogmatic and don’t understand why the rules were made. If people are logical and rational then their minds will not be dogmatic, the problem is religion does not allow logic and rationality. This is why we have so much misunderstanding about other religions because we do not open ourselves and study other scriptures. Rather, priests, mullahs, and pandits of religions do not allow followers to read other scriptures because they are afraid they will lose their own followers. There is always something good in every religion if they follow the good parts of it and leave the other unnecessary parts out. Like this at least they won’t become fanatical and create wars.
So many innocent people are murdered every year in the name of religion. Even in one religion there are many many branches and sub-branches. Then they all fight each other and kill each other every day.
Even if I Have to Burn in Fire, if I Have to Walk on Thorns, My Mind Will Be Fixed on your Feet.
During the State of Emergency in India several times the CBI officials came to interrogate me but I didn’t have much to say in answer to their questions. In the end they said, “Why don’t you give up Ananda Marga? We will get you out of jail and get you a job.” I told them, “There is no reason to give up Ananda Marga. Ananda Marga is a social and spiritual practice and people will do it even if Ananda Marga remains banned.”
I always remember one Urdu poem, “Mujhe asir karo wa juvan kato mere kheyalo ko bedi nahi pahana nahi sakate.” This means, “Put me in jail and cut out my tongue but you cannot stop my thoughts.” I used to sing this in prison. Even during my first experience in jail we used to sing a lot of ideological and devotional songs which kept our minds very high.
There is another one, “I get real pleasure on your lotus feet (in subservience to you). Even if I have to burn in fire, if I have to walk on thorns, if I have to be exiled from my country, my mind will be fixed on your feet. Let the whole world become my enemy, I won’t care, let my life become burdened, let death become my garland, my mind remains fixed on your feet”.
Other political prisoners in our room used to sing patriotic songs and revolutionary songs. Some songs were against the State of Emergency. It somehow kept us inspired. One time all the political prisoners wanted to go on a hunger strike because the jail authorities were not providing the proper facilities. We also joined to protest and added our own demands. Personally I wanted to get my orange robe as the authorities had refused this to me.
All the political prisoners stopped the hunger strike in the evening hours but the other Ananda Marga members and myself remained. I stopped when the superintendent of the jail came to meet me and promised to give me my orange robe. I told him very clearly that the orange robe is the dress of a monk in India. There is no ban of monkhood in India, the ban was of Ananda Marga. He saw my point and accepted my demand.
One of the things I found in the jail is that there were many poor people who were political prisoners. In the night you could hear them crying sometimes. If the man in prison was the breadwinner of the family, then his entire family would face starvation.
## Vinoba Bhave
After Mahatma Gandhi’s death Vinoba Bhave was a very important and respected leader in India. He had started Bhudan, a land donation movement. The movement failed because rich people would not donate good lands only marginal lands for the poor. Most of them gave very little land. Still Vinoba Bhave travelled around India to try to secure land for the movement. During the State of Emergency, he said the emergency was a Shanti Parva which means “a peace festival”. When I read in the newspaper his statement I wrote him a letter from the jail. I told him, “You are a respected person in the country and many innocent people are in jail. How can you say this is a Shanti Parva? I think you should reconsider your position.” I wrote in the letter that I had done no crime. I said, “I am a school teacher and yet I am in jail. This is totally wrong. Even if some member of Ananda Marga had done something wrong I should not be punished. I have no right to go to court. My civil rights have ceased.” He did not reply directly but he got my letter and his private secretary sent me a letter saying that my letter had been passed to Vinoba Bhave.
Another time I found through a newspaper that a magazine was going to publish a whole article against Baba. I wrote a letter to the publisher telling him to stop writing against Baba, who was my Guru. In the letter I said, “My Guru is for us just like Shri Krishna is for Hindus, Jesus Christ if for Christians, Prophet Mohammed is for Muslims, Lord Buddha is for Buddhists and Guru Nanak for the Siikh Relgion. We treat Shrii Shrii Anandamurti our Guru just like any of these religious treat their leaders, so if you publish something against our Guru there could be serious consequences.”
They had set a date to publish the article and made a lot of advertisement but my letter worked. The article ended up coming out but after a long time. I believe my letter worked. I know they read the letter as later I met someone who had read my letter. He said, “You were not afraid to write a letter, even while being in prison? I read your letter, it was very threatening.” I told him, “It was an attack on my Guru. I knew I couldn’t do much from prison, but whatever was possible, I did. I never cared for my death.” These days that same magazine writes more social rather than scandalous things. I read it myself when I am in India. It is known as Maya.
I had some communication with some monks and we used to exchange letters. In this way we used to get some news. Moreover, Baba kept sending Ananda Vanii from prison, it is a message to his devotees which He sent every year both in His birthday and on New Year. Luckily, somehow we used to get it.
On the first of January 1976 Baba said, “The very import of the history of human welfare is the history of struggle and strife. Even the sweet gospels of peace could not be preached in an environment of peace and composure. Devils did not allow the apostle of peace to work peacefully – that is why I say that peace is the outcome of fight.
“This endeavor at the well-being of the human race concerns everyone – it is yours, mine and ours. We may afford to ignore our rights, but we must not forget our responsibilities. Forgetting the responsibility implies the humiliation of the human race.
“In order to march ahead on the road of human welfare, we will have to strengthen ourselves in all the arena of life. The complete seeds of welfare in all the spheres – physical, mental, moral, social and spiritual – are embedded in the sixteen points. Hence be firm on the sixteen points.”
On Baba’s birthday 1976 he told us, “Parama Puruśa has blessed you with hands to work and legs to move; has infused you with the stamina to act; has endowed you with practical intelligence, so make the best use of them in the fight against the demons. You must not sit idle relying on fate. Be vigorously active.”
In January first 1977 he gave us the following message, “The struggle between the good and the evil forces terminates in the latter’s rout – this you have seen, are seeing and will see in future. Only remain vigilant that the evil forces under no circumstances receive any indulgence from your side.”
A few months before the emergency ended Baba told us, “That which renews human existence and makes it full to the brim and overflowing with the joy of living is termed a festival. Let Sháradotsava (autumn festival) inspire all people to live with a new spirit like true human beings. Let it inspire them with the fact that all humanity is an indivisible entity. On this auspicious day this is my inner wish.”
It was always inspiring to get His messages.
## Hospital Arrest
During the Emergency time political prisoners in many places were not well taken care of. In the hall where we were kept one of the prisoners died because he could not get medical help in time. This was very sobering for us. At the same time, I was also getting sick day by day and the government neglected to provide us with proper medical facilities. I had a serious pain in my head and couldn’t sleep in the night. Slowly I started to lose weight, my eyesight, appetite and everything. I was a thin person but I became even thinner, my total weight was around 70 lbs. Seeing the situation, I was sent out of jail to get medical treatment.
During my hospital stay outside of jail we were guarded by police. Seeing our situation and our conduct the police there became very sympathetic. They once told me, “If you want to go for a walk, feel free, I know you will not escape. We know that you people are good people and you are being wrongly kept in prison.” If I wanted to escape it would have been very easy for me. But I would have broken the trust of a simple policeman.
One day when I was in my hospital bed I was surprised to see two of my students and his father, they had come to visit me. I couldn’t tell at first if I was dreaming or it was real, it was very dangerous to visit me and no one had done it before. Whoever came to meet us would be put behind bars. I sat on my bed and asked them, “Why are you here?” Everyone kept mum. Then the father opened his mouth, he told me, “My youngest son Sarad had come to know that you were in the hospital and he has been crying since the day he learned you were in here. He said, “I want to meet my teacher, I want to meet my principal.” He asked us why you were here. We tried to convince him that you were a political prisoner and we might have to go to jail ourselves if we try to meet with you. But he kept asking why, and I had no good answer to why you were imprisoned, that is the reason we came here. If we have to go to jail, we will go to jail, but I will make my son happy.
I was speechless. Evidently even a little boy could understand that I was wrongly imprisoned and he wanted to be with me. I told Sarad that that was a very dangerous time. I cannot forget the scene and recall how much they loved me, that they would be ready to go to jail to see me. To tell the truth, I don’t know how to express such feelings as these. I think that ultimately the police did not report who came to see me because they didn’t care.
There was a government policy to not take care of political prisoners, so I was still not able to get adequate treatment. One day when the doctors were passing down the hallway I jumped out of bed and shouted at them for their negligence.
Luckily one day a local politician came to visit us. He used to be our jail mate but was released in order to contest an election. Seeing our situation, he called the governor of the state and told them that if any more prisoners died he would be held accountable because these prisoners were not being taken care of at this hospital.
In 1994 I met him in Bhopal. He was delighted to see me. Later on he became the chief minister of the state. He is still a minister. He has visited many times our school and attended our annual school functions. After he talked to the government about our lack of treatment they became a bit more conscientious about us and we got better care, but I never got completely well.
## The Emergency Ends
The emergency brought peace in the country, but it was static peace. There were also fear and psychosis everywhere. Out of fear people could not say anything, demonstrate or write. It was a total dictatorship during this time. It was propagated that Indira is India and India is Indira. This slogan was given by the president of the country. It was a very unfortunate time. There were instances of killing, harassment and torture. Also there was Indira Gandhi’s son, who was very involved in politics. He engaged in a lot of mischief during that “emergency”.
One of the terrible things the government of India was doing was a forced mass-sterilization campaign spearheaded by Sanjay Gandhi, the Prime Minister's son. They were sending police to round up people and forcibly sterilizing them so they would not be able to have children. This was their way to control population growth. I considered this to be inhuman.
I think the way to control population growth is to give proper food, housing, education and medical care to the people. When people are educated they are oriented towards higher purposes and they have fewer children.
The Emergency was lifted on March 1977 and a lot of us got out of jail, but Baba remained imprisoned.
## Out of Prison
It had been 22 months for me when I finally got out of jail. We did not want to go out of jail with the other political prisoners. But they said we all had to leave together. We had no idea that outside of the prison there were thousands of people waiting to receive us. We were put in special open cars in a parade for the people to see.
After coming out of jail we had no place to go because our building had been taken over by the government and was locked and sealed. For a few days we stayed in a very cheap hotel. There were four of us Ananda Marga monks but each of us went back to our different jobs, so I remained in Bhopal.
We started meeting political leaders to get our properties returned and to get back our rented place to run the school, which had been given to someone else, now there was a factory there. Eventually, we got our school buildings back.
Our property was in bad condition, even a part of the roof had been stolen so rain would pour in. Ananda Marga followers in Bhopal were mostly government employees or worked at a factory there. They were mainly very afraid because they had read a lot of propaganda against Ananda Marga. Only a few were not afraid.
There was an old lady who was a great devotee. I often went to see here before the emergency. She was very interested in Baba and Ananda Marga and sometimes even cried for Baba. After the Emergency when I went to see her she refused to see me. His son, who was an engineer, took me to another room and told me, “My mother is very disturbed due to seeing the newspapers and radio and even the movie advertisements about Ananda Marga. All this has given her a bad impression, so now she is afraid to talk to you.”
I used to regularly meet the chairman of the Ananda Marga school. His children used to study in our school. But when I went to visit them now his wife, out of fear, told me that she didn’t know who I was. The Emergency was gone but terror and fear remained in the minds of the people, haunting them.
## Meeting Baba in Prison
After getting and fixing both of our school buildings the news went around and soon we had students coming back. After nearly two years we began to run the schools again.
I was still very weak; I could barely walk. If I had to walk a quarter of a mile, I had to stop maybe three times to rest and even to get something to eat. My weight was only around seventy pounds. I had pain all over my body, weakness, difficulty sleeping, blurred vision. I was feeling very bad but still I had to run the schools.
One day I decided to go and see Baba in prison, it was 1977. Baba was in Bankipur jail, in Patna. When I went into his small cell I was shocked. I was meeting Baba for the first time after six years. Baba had a very strong physical body then, he was even kind of chubby. But in the jail he was so lean and thin that I was scared for his health. The reason was simple, since 1973 he was on fast, he was taking only a cup of yogurt mixed with water. He had to take it otherwise the government would force feed him and also His devotes pleaded with Him so He would eat something, otherwise he would have not taken even that, that is the reason he was so thin.
I had so many questions to ask but seeing his condition I forgot about all of my questions. The most important thing was that I was not able to meditate at all due to weakness and sickness. I was worried about my Acharya life. I was thinking that if an Acharya cannot practice meditation how is he going to initiate others. When I went near to Baba, he asked me, “How are you?” I said, “I am OK Baba” but took a deep breath as I said this and said, “But…” And Baba looked at me and asked “But what?” I told him I couldn’t do any meditation any more. Baba took my hand in His and repeated three times in Hindi, “You can do meditation, you can do meditation, you can do meditation.” He added, “But from today you should do kiirtan before doing meditation.” There were a few other visitors in the room. Baba asked me, “What happens when the electricity goes off in a room with a bulb?” I said, “Baba, it becomes dark.” Baba said, “Yes, it becomes so dark that you will not eve be able to see your own hands. So through kiirtan black out your mind. Then there will be very good meditations.” After that we did Pranam and we left his room.
I started to do kiirtan before meditation and it helped me, but my body remained very weak and sick.
## Another Police Abduction
One day, I was on the way to the school at Bhopal in Ananda Nagar which was built on our own property rather than rented. It was a ten-minute walk to get there from the bus stand. A police van came and stopped near me. They asked me where I was going. I told them I was going to my school at Ananda Nagar. They told me that I could go with them and they would drop me off there. I go t in the van as I thought they were going to help me. They took me to my school and then they said, “You have to come to the police station”. I replied, “For what?” They didn’t give me an answer. Anyway I went with them to the station.
I asked them why I was there, but they still wouldn’t say. They kept me all night at the police station. Next day they released me and I asked them again why had they kept me there all night? They told me that the prime minister of India, Mr. Morarji Desai, was visiting Bhopal. They had an instruction from the federal government to keep me at the police station until he left.
I called all the ministers, both provincial and federal, and I told them, “What is going on? Do you know that I was detained at the police station?” Everybody was shocked. They apologized and said they didn’t know this was happening.
When I went back to school some policemen and some security people who were guardians of some of the students came to me. They were very mad at the police officials who detained me. They said that if they had known I was being held they would have tried to do something about it. The children of many police and intelligence officials were studying in my school. The news spread and all of the guardians were very angry against the police and government.
The members of the opposition party who were in jail with me came into power but soon after they started to quarrel among themselves over who would lead. Finally, within two and one half years the government collapsed.
## Baba’s Release and Second PC
Baba’s case finally went to court where he was acquitted from all charges. He was released on the 2nd of August 1978. It was a moment of great joy for all the margiis.
When Baba got released from jail in 1978 and August 2, he had been in jail for almost six years and seven months, at that time most of the Ananda Marga members and monks and nuns went to receive Baba but I was very busy in the school and was not able to make the journey. So after some time I went with three other margiis to Patna, to see Baba. When I went to Patna I wanted to have again a personal contact with Baba as I had come to know that if you had become a monk you got a second opportunity for a PC.
Of course, I was attending every day His pravacana (talks). One day I got a chance to go with Baba in the car to go to a place in nature and take the fresh air. Baba used to go for a field walk every day but he was so weak that He was remaining in a car to get his fresh air.
I was sitting beside Baba in the back seat. In the front seat were Baba’s driver and a margii who was a lawyer of the Supreme Court of India. His name was Ranjan Dvedi. He asked a question to Baba, “Where will You go after Patna?” Baba looked at me and asked me if I could take care of Him in case He went to Bhopal. Of course I replied in the affirmative. He told to Rabjan Dvedi that He was going to go to Bhopal. Another thing I remember from that ride with Baba is that He told me not to accept land, money or a house with conditions attached.
So I was there for a few days in Patna and kept insisting for a PC to his Personal Assistant. He was saying that I had already had a PC in 1979 and I was Baba’s bodyguard in 1971 and I had just gone with Baba in the car a few days before and also I saw Him every day, why did I want personal contact? I said simply that that was the rule, why should I miss my chance after I had become a Dada. Finally, my personal contact was approved.
I went into Baba’s room, prostrated myself and stood up. I was standing silently. Baba took out a big stick and I thought he was going to break my bones, I was scared. I just kept silent. Baba asked why I had not adhered to the sixteen points. I promised baba I would become an adherent of the sixteen points and that was it. My personal contact ended with these words, but I was fully satisfied to have had my second personal contact with Baba. He once explained that PC is not so much about what happens on the external meeting with Baba but rather in a subtler plane.
## The Other Iishvarakrsna
I used to go visit Baba’s house every day. One day, Baba was explaining some philosophical talk and he asked me to repeat in simple language the same thing He had just said. For me it was difficult to explain. Then Baba said, you know, Iishvarakrsna was the greatest preceptor regarding Samkhya philosophy. This philosophy was propounded by Kapil Muni but it was Iishvarrakrsna who brought the philosophy to the common people. Baba said to me, “Can’t you explain my philosophy?” I kept quiet. Until then I had no idea about who my namesake Iishvarakrsna was. Then Baba explained about the philosophy of Krsna, the biological Krsna and Historical Krsna.
## Baba’s Condition for Bhopal’s DMC
One day when I went to Baba’s quarters he asked me which route I was going to take to return to Bhopal. Baba himself explained that one way was to go through Varanasi and the other route was through Raipur. I told Baba I was going through Varanasi. Then Baba told me, “Why don’t you drop your LFT in the Acharya training center?” I told Baba I would do so.
I had come to meet Baba with my LFT and when I told him about this incident he said he wanted to become a monk and he wanted to go to Varanasi. Many margiis had told him before to go to the training center but he had refused. I had given my word to Baba that I would try to send him to the training center after arriving in Bhopal.
The day came when I was going to depart for Bhopal. I went to see Baba in MG quarters. I went to Baba and asked Him when was he planning to come to Bhopal. Baba replied that He was under the organization and hence He had to follow the rules. He had received news from the general secretary, the Dharma Prachar Secretary and ISMUB secretary that they had completed Bhukti Pradhan and Upabhukti Pramukha election in Kolkata region, so now he had to go to Kolkata.
I told Baba that there was no such condition at that time when I talked to Him in the car. I reminded him of what he had said then. But Baba said again that He was under an organization and that He had to follow all the rules. I was insisting to him to come to Bhopal because He promised me He would. He told me, “You do one thing, you go to Bhopal, complete your Bhukti Pradahan and Upabhukti Pramukha election in your diocese and secretly send me a telegram stating that you have completed these things so then I will tell the central authorities that I must go to Bhopal because I have new information that the election has been completed”. But added, “This is just between you and me and no one else will know about it”. After this instruction I had nothing more to say.
I thought that I would not be able to complete the election of BP and UBP as I was very busy with the school. Still I kept in my mind that Baba wanted to come to Bhopal, this was always in my head.
I left for Bhopal and when I got there I got busy with the school. Whenever I had a little time, I went to different districts to contact people and margiis for the elections, still I was never able to complete the election. Nevertheless, Baba would keep His promise and fulfill my desire.
## The LFT
I had one more duty: to send the LFT to the training center. One day I asked him why he didn’t want to become a monk. He said he had some loans to pay. I told him I could help him with the money because there were some holy days coming at the schools and there would be a little spare money. After this he went to the training center. I thought myself that at least I had been able to keep one promise to Baba by sending this LFT to the training center.
Soon after, I had to go to a place called Jabalpur. There was a new Dada posted there and he wanted me to go to have a kiirtan in a haunted house.
Nobody was living in this haunted house though it was still in good condition. Ananda Marga had bought this house because we didn’t have our own property there at that time, it was going to be used as a school.
I went there and was surprised to find the LFT that I had sent to the Varanasi training center. I didn’t want to spoil the spiritual environment so I decided to talk to him later. We had Akhanda Kiirtan for three hours and meditation. After meditation we had a collective meal. When the meal was over I decided to talk to the LFT. I took him in one of the rooms and talked to him.
I asked him why he had come back to Jabalpur. He said that the life in the training center was very hard. It was very difficult for him and that is why he left. I told him that Baba asked me to send him to the training center and I had given my word to Baba, it rarely happened like this. I told him that he should go there and complete his training for this reason and also to help me keep my word. He said that he would go back and he did.
He became a Dada and worked for a few years. After many years in 2006 or 2007, one day I was with someone who got a phone call from this same person. He handed me the phone saying it was someone who knew me very well. When I took the call he said, “Dada I am lost in Maya, I am Thakurdas.” I kept quiet for a few moments and then continued the conversation. He told me that the life of a monk was very difficult for him so he had come back home and was now running a business. He had adult children who were also running a business.
Later I came to know this a Dada who was posted where he lived that he had been one of the biggest supporters of the Marga, he did a lot of work for the Marga and helped many people. I still could not understand why Baba chose him to become a monk.
## Baba in Bhopal
Baba had already visited four main cities in India after coming out of prison in 1978 and held DMC in Delhi, Kolkata, Bombay, and Chennai. I had attended the Kolkata and Delhi DMCs where up to ten or fifteen thousand people had attended. I had enjoyed these DMCs so much that it cannot be put into words.
It was the end of 1978 and there was a DMC to be held in Patna for the new year. I was there in Patna for the DMC and heard that Baba was planning the rest of His tour as He wanted to tour all of India. I came to know through my regional secretary, my superior, that Baba would like to visit first Bhopal if I could manage the organization. The regional secretary wanted to take Him to Jaipur which was his own headquarters. He argued that in Jaipur there were many important people. Mr. Mongol Bihari, who was a top government official in the state and would later become defense secretary of India, there was a top lawyer who was advocate general, Mr. M.L. Agarwal who was principal of degree college of Jaipur, Major Daolatsingh and several other prominent people. In Bhopal we had not a single important person except a lady doctor who was very active, her name was Vididyajin. There were very ordinary people such as Radhe Shyam, Mr. D. Kodwani, Mr. Patil etc. Now the regional secretary, my superior, was discouraging me of having Baba visit Bhopal first.
The RS kept repeating his arguments to me. He told me to say that I could not manage Baba’s visit in Bhopal, but I remembered that when I was in Patna Baba had asked me if I could manage if He were to come to Bhopal and I had already replied yes. Furthermore, Baba had promised to come. This was flashing back in my mind again and again. At the same time the regional secretary was adamant that Baba should first go to Jaipur and wanted me to say that I couldn’t manage. He was a higher authority in the organization and so he was trying to force his demand on me.
In the meantime, I saw a family Acharya who was very famous and respected in Ananda Marga. He was not from Bhopal but he was from the same province. His name was Acharya Narendra. We used to call him Narendra Dada or just Dada. He was also in Patna to attend the new year DMC. When I saw him I was very excited but I was sad too and he could read my face. When he asked me about it I told him about Baba wanting to come to Bhopal first but my RS is discouraging me from organizing it. I told him that Baba was satisfied when he got the assurance from me that I could manage His visit to Bhopal, but RS was forcing me to say that I couldn’t manage. I told him about the RS arguments that Baba must first visit Jaipur as the regional headquarters where there and that there were many important people in Jaipur and not a single one in Bhopal. Narendra Dada said, “I am with you. And whatever you need I will do for you.”
The RS happened to come where Acharya Narendra and I were. Narendra Dada looked at the RS and told him, “Hey RS, what do need from Bhopal?” When the RS saw Narendra he got scared. He started to make some excuses. Dada Narendra said, “I am in support of the DS of Bhopal. I’ll bring as many people as you want. So if Baba wants to come to Bhopal first you go and tell Baba that DS of Bhopal can manage His visit.”
I am always thankful for Dada’s support. Just a few months ago he passed away. He was a freedom fighter of India during the British time and when he became a family Acharya of Ananda Marga he gave up his job and created many monks and many thousands of members of Ananda Marga in his area.
## How We Got a Printing Press
In the end Baba visited Bhopal first. We had DMC in Bhopal. When Baba came there I had 2 schools, one children’s home (orphanage), our own land, our own building and our own small newsletter, but I didn’t have a printing press. So when Baba came to Bhopal he wouldn’t hold DMC unless we had a printing press. The margiis became very worried.
When Baba came to Bhopal many people came to attend this program from Bhopal itself. It was a surprise to me and the other members as before they had been afraid of the government, but when Baba came they all came out. A lot of people where ready to help with getting the printing press and gave it to Baba as a present, the DMC conducted in Bhopal.
After that incident Baba used to joke with me sometimes and tell me that He had go to Bhopal and hold a DMC so I could buy a printing press.
The most important thing for me was that Baba had promised to come to Bhopal and He did. He never went to Bhopal afterwards and two months after the DMC I was transferred from Bhopal. If I had missed that opportunity, I wouldn’t have another chance to bring him to Bhopal.
## Sacrificing Attending Baba’s Arrival to Bhopal
Although the government was changed after Indira Gandhi’s rule, the mentality of the bureaucrats did not change.
Everywhere there were delays with paperwork if the paperwork got done at all. Obstruction with our activities kept being an issue. They did not give recognition to my school so my students in the board examinations appeared as private students. That was not good for my school. Still we were always getting students, we had four to five hundred students.
I had two board exams, one in 1978 and one in 1979. In 1978 somehow it was a bit easier to appear at an exam as a private student, but in 1979 it became more difficult. I went several times to meet with the district education officials and the director of public instruction or DPI. But both officials ignored my requests. Finally, I went to my jail mate who was then the minister of education of the province. I complained about the situation, I told him that the government had changed but their activities hadn’t and that they were hindering me everywhere. He wrote a letter and sent it through his secretary. I went again to these officials and again they ignored me. The education minister sent letters three times and I was ignored every time.
The fourth time was very difficult for me because Baba was coming to Bhopal and my students were not allowed to appear in the exam. One side of me was running after government officials and the other side was preparing for Baba’s visit to Bhopal.
I got an appointment with the education minister the same day that Baba was arriving. I had no choice. This concerned the life lf many students, if they didn’t appear in the exams then one year would be wasted. I chose to go the minister’s house instead o receiving Baba at airport. But I sent all the students and many guardians who also wanted to see Baba, so they all went to welcome Him at the airport. I was sad because I was not there to receive Baba, sometimes in life we have to sacrifice everything for serving humanity.
I went to the minister’s house and told him about my problem. He was very upset when he came to know that the bureaucrats were behaving in such a manner with me. He took up the phone and called the district education officer and the DPI and asked them why I was being harassed. He told them, “What happened to the letters I sent you? I don’t want to send any more letters to you. You make arrangements for students to attend the board examinations. I don’t want to hear any other reasoning anymore.”
From there I went straight to the district education office. He was a bit shocked. He remarked that I had gone to the minister of education’s house. I told him, “I came to your place several times, so I had to do it”. He said that next time I should not do that and that they would do whatever was possible in their jurisdiction, he said, “Here is the paper for recommendation for your students.”
## Radheshyam’s Son
When I arrived in Bhopal in August or September in 1974 there were very few margiis there. We had two places for dharmachakra, one was our school in Security Lines, where one or two people, three at the most, would come for dharmachakra. But one person would always come, his name was Radheshyam. He was a factory worker and was a great devotee. Sometimes I did dharmachakra alone in the school.
I met Radheshyam’s son in 1995. Hi son used to study in my school. He became a very strong margii and wanted to become a monk of Ananda Marga, his father had no objection, but his mother was always objecting. He kept insisting that he wanted to be a monk, his mother totally refused, she wanted him to get married and get a job. But he didn’t want to do either.
One day he told his mother that he was going to look for a job. He went and bought some kind of poison instead. He drank the poison and killed himself. I was totally shocked. Ananda Marga is against suicide, but in extreme situations people do extreme things. This is a rather sad story, but I wanted to tell this to emphasize that parents should not object to their adult children if they are determined to do some service for society. In the end it was his family that suffered.
## A Senior Acharya
A problem with the school was that the Acharya working there would have no time left to do any other organizational or devotional work. There weren’t many people to help. The only person who was active at that time Dr. Vidiya Jain, she was the person who did all the job when Baba came to Bhopal.
There was also a Dada, my senior, who actually had a different posting but he used to stay all the time in Bhopal. He used to help a little bit and he had known all the guardians since he had lived at that place for many years. Many principals had come and gone but he had remained there, so he exerted a great influence on the margiis there and on some guardians. His behavior was sometimes very strange. He would unnecessarily interfere in my administration.
A family had one son and one daughter at our school. The daughter had already graduated from fifth standard and her father wanted to put her in a different school and wanted to keep his son in our school. In my absence, that senior Dada told that parent that if he took out his daughter, he should take his son out as well.
I came to know when I came back from town and the father of the kids complained to me. I was surprised. First of all, we did not want to have girls of a certain age in our school. Secondly there was nothing wrong with keeping his son in our school. I became really upset and gave a guarantee to the guardian that his son would stay and his daughter could go to whatever school he chose. This is one of the examples of his interference in my administration.
One day I came to know that Baba had sent this senior central worker to remove this Dada from Bhopal school although I did not make a formal complaint against him. In the end, after creating a lot of disturbances, he went to his posting.
There he was brought in to the education department and posted as the principal of a school in the northern part of India. He was very much confident that he could start and run a school, but he had never started a school in his life.
Some time later he went for DMC in Delhi. Baba asked him if he had started a school and he said he hadn’t. Baba told him to leave immediately, start a school and then come back. So he left and went back to establish a school. In the end he never succeeded in starting a school. Baba knew that he was much better at meddling in others school projects than in starting his own and proved it to him by giving him this responsibility.
## Sagar’s School
There was a place called Sagar, it was a very difficult place for Ananda Marga. Many workers were posted there to start a school without success. A couple of times I went with these workers to help them, but I couldn’t stay more than one or two days as I had other work. No one was able to start a school for many years. Due to hardships, many workers left that place, but Baba kept sending workers there.
Sagar workers always used to stay in Bhopal because they didn’t have a place to stay in Sagar. I didn’t mind it although I had pressures from my immediate senior authorities to send them out from Bhopal. Nevertheless, I didn’t feel it was the right thing to do.
Eventually a school was started there and it became the biggest school in India. Here I would like to point that Baba never gave up on any place even though He lost some workers.
## Why Did You Leave?
In Bhopal where I was posted there was also an Acharya who was supposed to be the principle of the school. Once, that worker got slightly sick. Although he could have come to my place and gotten medicine and rest instead he wrote later to his family for money. When the money came he changed his mind. First of all, he wasn’t seriously ill so he didn’t really need a lot of money to buy medicine, so he bought a railway ticket to go to Patna. But when he arrived in Patna he again changed his mind and went to his home. His family all are margiis.
When he went there of course he got some comfort from his family so he decided to just stay home. He thought the life of a monk was very difficult. If he had not made his first mistake, I think he would have survived as a monk today.
This story was narrated by him after many years when once I met him in an Ananda Marga function in India when I asked him why he left. He was a simple fellow and I had known him his since he was a margii, before we were monks.
I think when monks are in trouble they should not ask for help from their families but should ask for help from God. Because God can always provide help. That is my belief. Because God’s door is always open to those who dedicate their lives to God.
## Bihar DMC and Lemon Water
In the year 1979 I was posted in Purnea, Bihar, because Baba was going to give DMC there. It is very near to Jamalpur where Baba was born and lived. Most of the very early Margiis were from that area. I went there and visited several villages and met many margiis. Some of them were not very well educated and some of them were even illiterate but they were great devotees of Baba. It was quite different from Bhopal. I was able to initiate several hundred people in one month. After that, it was time for the DMC.
Baba called some of us Dadas in His room for reporting. During this reporting He asked me why was I so lean and thin. I told Baba I didn’t know. I told him this several times but Baba kept repeating, “You know why you are lean and thin”, I kept saying I didn’t. Then Baba asked me, “Do you feel very weak?” I said yes. “Do you feel very tired?” I said yes. “You cannot sleep well in the night?” I said that I couldn’t. He asked me if I had stomach pain and again I said yes. “Do you feel drowsy?” I said yes. He asked me a series of similar questions about my health. He said, “I was telling you that you know your problem and you are hiding it from me.”
I was scared. I was thinking that Baba was going to cure me by beating me with his stick, because sometimes he had done that. When he asked me to come very near to him, I got even more scared. Baba said, come closer, come closer. After that, Baba said, “I am going to cure you”. He touched all parts of my body where there were problems. He told me that from today onward, I had to drink lemon water with salt ever morning after getting up. Since then, I have drinking lemon water. Baba talked that day a lot about lemon water. Basically he said that the lemon can cure several illnesses, including mine as it was originating in the digestive system and the lemon water cleanses it. After fasting in Ananda Marga, the people drink lemon water to clean their bodies.
I didn’t like lemon water with salt from the very beginning so I always drank lemon water with sugar, I had to change this bad habit. Today lemon water still has great importance in my life.
Many people have discouraged me from taking lemon water every day but I say no to them, it is the instruction of my Guru to take it and perhaps I am still alive on this planet because I have been taking lemon water.
## Baba Loves All
It was also during this Bihar DMC that I went to see Baba and when I entered in His room, He asked me, “Angdesh ke ki samach acchai”, meaning, “What is the news about Angadesh.”
Angadesh was a kingdom in the Mahabharata age which used to be in what is now the state of Bihar. It was the kingdom of Karna a very famous king in the Mahabharata. Karna was a very good man but he supported the evil prince Duryodhana.
I replied to Baba's question, “This is a very good place and the Margiis in this area are great devotees. It seems people from all positions have great devotion in this part of the country and they have their own spiritual experiences.” Then Baba said, “Why is it that people of this part are so devoted.” I said, “Baba I don't know why people are such great devotees here in this place.” Then Baba said, “This is my birthplace.” Baba was born in the state of Bihar. I said to Baba that this couldn’t be the reason as people had a very strong feeling about Him in all the different places in India. If you go to Bhojpuri, you become Bhojpuri Baba, if you go the Magha, you become Maghi Baba, if you go to Bengal you become Bengali Baba. Wherever you go you become part of the people.
All of the sudden Baba said, “You have had some contact with non Indian Margiis and Acharyas.” I said, “Yes Baba I have had some contact.” Baba continued, “And what do they say?” I told Baba I had a very beautiful experience with early Margiis who came to India from other countries. In Ranchi I met several of them. Also I had training with non Indian Acharyas, people from the United States, Europe, Singapore, the Philipines, etc. I told Him I remembered the devotional singing of these people and I saw their great devotion. Then Baba said, “Baba Loves All.”
I remember one time a group of Italian Margiis came to see Baba in 1977. These people sang so beautifully. I had never seen people from outside of India doing such great devotional kiirtan. When I experienced that I felt like the whole humanity was one. Baba never belonged to a particular nation, or a particular state or group of people, or culture, he belongs to all.
## Balram Singh
Once Baba went to Varanasi after He was released from prison. Baba generally stayed in the Ananda Marga headquarters but in Varanasi we didn’t have a good place for Baba to stay. The margiis there arranged a room in a hotel.
When Baba came to the hotel he became furious. He said that he had been insulted by the margiis of Varanasi. He refused to stay in that hotel. And he would not stay in the house of any margii of Varanasi.
Now it was a big problem for the organizer of the DMC because they didn’t know where to take him to stay. So Baba asked if there was anyone outside of Varanasi. There was a high official, Deputy Director of Agriculture, his name was Balram Singh.
So Baba told them he wanted to go to the official’s house. When Baba arrived at this residence the door was closed. Somehow the door opened and Balram Singh was totally drunk. The official was lying on his bed. He woke up and saw baba. Baba just sat on the bed and the official jumped out of his bed and Baba laid down. He was very ashamed. After that he stopped drinking and became very active in Ananda Marga functions. The house became the center of all the activities for the DMC.
Balram Singh’s house was actually government property, so the CBI started to question Balram about Baba staying there and to put pressure to get Baba out of there. But Balram was adamant and he told the authorities, “He is my Guru and he will stay in my house, and you can do whatever you want, I don’t care about my job.” When the procession of Ananda Marga was taking place, Balram was dancing kiirtan in the streets.
The reason Baba didn’t stay in the hotel was because it was in a bad area of the town, respectable people didn’t go to that part of town. Secondly, he wanted to do something good for that official. I heard this story from that official himself when he came to visit Varanasi at our monastery.
## Kapalika Initiation and Why Hiding from Baba is Useless
It was summer when I left Bhopal to work on our summer program. After participating in some seminars I went to Kolkata to attend DMC.
Before the day of DMC Baba was giving Avadhuta Diiksa, that is, teaching Kapaliika Sadhana. My name was recommended every day but I was not able to get Kapaliika Sadhana from Baba. Baba had put certain conditions to get this such as having a school, or children’s home, medical center, printing press, land, building, etc. I had done all these things and everything required to receive Kapaliika Sadhana but I had handed over my charge to someone else, So at that time I didn't have any project under me.
I talked to my highest authority of the department whose name was Acharya Keshavananda Avadhuta. He told me that they had recommended my case although I didn’t have any current posting and I was soon going out of India. He told me, “You should go today to Baba's quarters and stay until you get Diiksa.”
I went there, my name was approved and I got my initiation on the same day of the DMC, June 17, 1979. It was one of the happiest days of my life. After the initiation we had to do Kapaliika Sadhana at midnight for thirty nights consecutively.
After the DMC was over I was instructed to stay in Kolkata, that time our camp headquarters were in Jodhpur Park. I was staying there with some other Dadas who were supposed to go outside of India as well. During those days I went to see Baba in private. Soon Baba left and we were instructed to stay in Jodhpur Park and complete our senior Acharya training. Basically we had to copy down some rules for Acharyas. After we finished we told to Acharya Keshavandanda Avadhuta who was ERAWS secretary central that we didn’t have anything to do. He said we should just stay there and relax as we would be leaving the country soon. He said jokingly, “Now you are the guests.”
There was a problem, Baba didn’t actually approve that we should be there without doing anything. Our superiors knew this and so they instructed us to hide from Baba if He were to come. When Baba came to the central office we hide ourselves in a room under the cot or in the bathroom. He had specific instructions not to see Baba face to face.
Baba started coming daily, so we were hiding every day. One day, Baba ordered that all the people in the building should be brought before him. So we were all rounded up and brought before Baba. Baba immediately asked me, “What is your duty?” I told him that, “I am education in charge of AMUPRESO, Ananda Marga Universal Permanent Relief Society and also I am posted in the Social Security Department in Hong Kong sector” Baba said, “But, you are not there right now, you are not in Hong Kong, you are in India, what is your duty?” I said, “I have no duty.” Baba became furious and told me, “How can you leave a single second on the planet without a duty?”
When Baba said that central workers were looking at each other and consulted among themselves and told Baba, “He is the ERAWS secretary of Delhi sector.” This sector would actually include all of India, Bangledesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Sikkam, a vast area. They told this to Baba so He would not punish them instead of me for not having given me a duty. Baba then asked me, “So how many schools did you open?” I said, “I didn't open any school.” So I was punished for not opening any school in the sector that I wasn't really in charge of. Baba was a tantric Guru and when he gave you a duty he expected you to give a positive response. I did not give a positive response so I was punished.
## Acharya in Sagar
Once there was an Acharya posted in Sagar which was a neighboring diocese of Bhopal. This particular worker couldn’t handle the hardships of being posted there and went back to his home with his family. He also met the Bhukti Pradhana, that is, the district secretary of Ananda Marga elected by local margiis of the district. When the Bhukti Pradhana met him, he came to know about what had happened and brought him to Kolkata to see Baba.
When baba came to know this situation he sent this Acharya right back to Sagar with a new name. Generally, if someone returned home, he had to go back to the training center, but in this case Baba wanted as punishment only to change the name of the worker. I came to know about this whole incident when I returned from the Purnea DMC.
I later met this worker again. I was leaving India soon so I gave many of my personal things to him, like my watch, blanket and clothes because and I told him that in the future I would help him if he had any difficulty there.
## Baba’s School
Once there was a worker who was posted in a place where there were many senior margiis. These margiis were initiated by Baba and some of them were family Acharyas. In short they were very important people in the organization.
Baba had given the duty to open the schools to the whole time workers, so this Acharya went to the field and talked to the senior margiis about opening a school. Most of them discouraged him for this or that reason. The worker was very new.
So when Baba asked him about the school he told him that the senior margiis didn't want to cooperate to open the school, he almost broke down and started to weep. Baba told him to go to back and work as if there were no margiis there. “Meet the common people and talk with them about opening the school in the area.”
He went back to the field and met with the common people and talked to them about the school. The people gave a positive response and soon the school was open.
When the margiis came to know that this whole time worker had succeeded they felt ashamed. After that everybody was ready to cooperate. The school was already open so they did whatever they could do to help him from then on. It was a great lesson for a missionary worker that without the support of margiis he could still start a school. Baba wanted to teach a lesson to both the missionary worker as well as the senior margiis.
## The “Missing” Money
After I was transferred from Bhopal I became a “floating” worker. Most of the people who were posted outside of India but for this or that reason, such as passport and visa issues, they had to stay in India, were called floating workers.
One day during my reporting Baba said that he had received a letter from Bhopal’s Buhkti Pradhana complaining about me. At that time Bhagawandin Ranjan was the Bhukti Pradhana of Bhopal. He was my jail mate and he respected me. So I was very surprised that he had complained of me. Central workers knew the real situation so they told Baba that the complaints didn’t have a solid basis. But Baba said, “They complained, it came from an elected representative of the margiis. So there should be an investigation.” Baba added, “How dare he make such an accusation.”
Baba made the first sectorial secretary of the Delhi sector, Acharya Satyatmakananda Avadhuta and myself go there to find out the truth about the complaint and report to the central office.
It was very strange for me to go to investigate a charge that was made against me. It was on Baba's instruction that we both went to Bhopal. We called the Bhukti Pradhana and the other margii involved and asked about the complaint. The Bhukti Pradhana started to weep and cry and the other margii also started to weep. I couldn't figure out what was happening. I asked why everyone was crying. They told me that my successor forced them to write a letter of complaint.
We went to ask my successor about it, he told us that he didn't see any money, so he was thinking I was responsible for the “missing” money and that is why he had complained about me. We told him there was a bank account book which had a record of all the money spent. I said, “You see I came here before the emergency and worked here after the Emergency as well. We had to purchase many new things so the money was spent and all the transactions were recorded.” He felt very guilty about it because he hadn't gone through the account book. We had nothing to investigate because everything was in black and white. Since that time whenever I saw this worker he felt ashamed about this.
## I Become a Prabuddha
I was very unhappy to leave India. When the time came to go I went to see Baba in private. When I met him I started to weep. I couldn't control myself. Baba said, “You are weeping because you ae going to miss me and because you will have to travel far away.” He consoled me, “We have a system of RDS and you will be able to come for RDS and able to see me then.” Baba wiped my tears with his lungi and I felt better. He also said, “The duty outside of India is different than it is here.”
After I left India I was only able to go back to attend RDS as it was a rule that a representative from each sector should attend it. So each year I had a chance to go to India and in that way I was able to meet Baba.
The relationship between Guru and disciple is very close. The Guru is father, mother, brother, sister and friend and everything else you can imagine. It was difficult for me to imagine that I would be missing Baba’s Dharmamahacakras. Ever since I first saw Baba in 1967 when he was visiting Daltonganj I never missed DMC, both the new year DMC and the Ananda Purnima DMC. In DMC I always felt very good.
Acharya Rameshananda Avadhuta was SS of Hong Kong sector. He came to India and gave me a ticket to go to Taiwan but my passport was not ready. Baba was going to visit the Hong Kong sector and so the SS wanted me to come as soon as possible but I couldn't do it.
Baba went to visit Hong Kong sector and only after he returned to India I was able to go there. My first stop was from Kolkata to Bangkok. I had talked with some American Dadas whom I knew from the training center at Vranasi. They told me that monks there could travel on buses for free, you had to sit in the very rear of the bus as those seats were reserved for monks. They also said, “You have to explain to them that you are a monk, because you are a foreigner and Buddhist monks have shaved heads in Thailand. Tell them you are a prabuddha.”
When I arrived in Bangkok I had only twenty-five dollars in my pocket and I still had to get my visa for Taiwan, so pretty much, I had no money for anything else. When I took a bus and the conductor came to charge for the bus ticket I told him that I was prabuddha and he went away. In this way I traveled all the time I was in Bangkok.
We had a small center in Thailand. There was a worker posted there who was from Norway. His name was Dada Paramahansa. He was a very nice person and he helped me get my visa. I had to borrow some money from him for the fees for my visa. After I got it I flew to Hong Kong.
## Taipei via Hong Kong
I wanted to visit our office there. They had told me that if immigration asked me about my financial situation and asked them to show them how much money I had, which was a question that they asked foreigners especially from Asian or poor countries, I should tell them that I had a friend in Hong Kong and he could bring money as much as I needed to show them. This came to happen and my friend actually came. Still the immigration official didn't accept that and they wanted to send me back to India. Nevertheless, I had a ticket to Taiwan as well as a visa, so after a lot of struggle I was allowed to continue on to Taiwan.
I arrived in Taipei airport and took a taxi to our center which was located on Roosevelt road section three. Our office was on the third floor. On the second floor we operated a vegetarian restaurant. It was mentioned in the tourist guide to Taiwan in those days. It had a funny name, Yoga Vegetarian Happiness Garden. Some people came to know of Ananda Marga through the restaurant. When I arrived to our center I met a Dada from Singapore. His name was Dada Vinita. The Dada that had given me the ticket was no longer there and we had a new SS who was in Japan at that time. Dada Vinita explained to me Taiwan’s situation. He told me to tour the entire country of Taiwan.
It is a small country and we had several Ananda Marga centers in the country. I was visiting every day one of the major cities. After Baba's visit, Taiwan was left with a Tantric wave. By this I mean that the people who were not ideological had left Ananda Marga. That is the reason Dada Vinita told me to travel to every unit of Ananda Marga, he wanted to revive the organization there.
He would stay in Taipei and I would travel. In Taipei there were several good margiis while in other places we had very very few people. Slowly we worked and rebuilt our organization in Taiwan. After two or three months a couple of Acharyas arrived from India and took some pressure off of me. Then I didn't have to travel so often and stayed in only one or two places.
## Baba Doesn’t do Magic, (kind of)
Some margiis had come to believe that when Baba arrived, He would perform magic and other things. This was a concern for some. There was a margii whose name was Yogendra. He was a hotel manager in Kausoung City. He told me his experience of Baba's visit. It was a fasting day and Baba was giving a talk and mentioned that someone was eating chocolate on the fasting day and was thinking that Baba wouldn’t know about it. It was Yogendra who had secretly taken chocolate that day.
## Baba’s Room
A margii from Italy was also in Taipei. His name was Omprakash. He was a hotel manager there. He had been a hotel manager in many big cities in different parts of the world. When Baba went to Taipei He had stayed at this margiis house. Sometimes we used to visit this house and he had kept Baba's room exactly as it was since His visit. We used to go and meditate in the room where Baba had stayed. Baba had left a very strong vibration in that room.
## Krsna Kumar
Many margiis used to take care of the different centers around the country. In Kausoung City Krsna Kumar was taking care of our center. He was an official in the customs department. He was a very lean and thin person but he had a very strong mind. He used to give yoga classes and talked very sweetly with others. He meditated for long periods and would always help every worker of the Marga he came in contact with.
Once he had saved some money so I could go to India. I went there and found out that he had given the money away. So I asked him why had he done that. He said that if he didn't help others, who would? Over a period of one week, six workers had come to him and he had helped everyone of them. He sweetly told me, “Dada, don't worry I have a friend, I will take you to my friend and he will give you the money.” And so it happened that he took me to his friend and his friend provided me with the travel money.
But a tantric Guru and God always takes a test of such kind of good people. It so happened that he lost his job. He had a couple of children who were in high school and middle school. To support them he started to work in a shop. Soon the shop was closed because it was robbed. To support his family he had to sell some kind of food item that was not sentient and that he would not eat himself. A margii consulted me about this and I told them that if there was no alternative, to support the family was the most important thing so I saw no problem with that. After that he got a job and things went back to normal.
## The Margii Buddhist Monk
Once I had initiated a Buddhist monk in Kausoung City. He was chief of one of the biggest temples there. He took the practice very seriously. I used to visit him from time to time.
Once one Didi wanted to open a school in Taiwan so she asked me how she could get children to come knowing that I had worked in a school. In India when you start a school we go to the public and and talk about our system of education. I thought that if I could take this monk to talk to the people on our behalf it would be easy to get enough students.
I called him and asked for his help. He said, “What kind of help?” I explained the situation and he accepted.
The day for the talk came. We were the Didi, Krsna Kumar, Yogendra, the Buddhist monk and myself. We went around different localities and when the Buddhist monk came out of his car sometimes the people recognized him and asked him what he wanted, he then explained them about the school and within a short period we had enough children to open it.
The next difficulty was, how to get a teacher since the Didi did not speak Chinese? We talked about it and put an ad in the newspaper and around thirty women came to her to apply for the job. And she not only got the teacher she wanted but some of the others became LFTs.
## Japan
After some time, I was posted in Japan. I flew to Osaka and from there went to Kyoto. The next day I went to Tokyo by hitchhiking. I hadn't done hitchhiking in my entire life. I was very lucky that I got information about how and where to hitchhike from the information center in Kyoto. I went to the highway entry ramp and saw some people standing there with their thumbs in the air. I went near and asked them what they were doing. They told me they were trying to get a ride. I did the same and soon a car stopped, they were an elderly couple who were going to Tokyo. I arrived in Tokyo around 2 a.m. They even dropped me at our center there.
At that time there was an Indian Dada, a German Dada and an American Dada posted there. Dada Ragatmananda was at that time an LFT. Me and Dada Ragatmananda organized a yoga class, we used to teach the class together.
After some time, I left Tokyo and went to Matsuyama, it is the capital of the island of Shikoku, Japan. The idea was to spread the ideology there. There was a high school student where I stayed. He immediately contacted newspapers and radio stations about me and I went to the radio and had an interview. Soon the newspaper also came and put my picture on the front page. Through that I made several contacts and many people were initiated.
After a few days I moved from the high school student's house and went to a family house for several weeks before I was running out of time on my visa. I went to the visa office but I was told they couldn't extend my visa. I left and went to Korea.
## Korea
Most of the time I stayed in a cheap place, an inn. It just cost a couple of dollars. It was the year 1980. Korea was in big political turmoil. The Korean President Park Chung Hee was murdered by his intelligence department. Somehow the army got involved and arrested the top official of the intelligence department and then the military took over the government.
Mr. Chung do Hwan took over the presidency. When I arrived in Korea there were police and army on every street corner in the main cities and there were protests nearly every day at the university against the military government. After midnight everything was closed until morning and there was actually a curfew, so no one was allowed to be outside. Any kind of meeting, debate or discussion was banned. The propagation of Ananda Marga was therefore considered illegal and it was difficult to do anything openly. I managed to go to universities, temples, and other social activity centers.
During that time, it was difficult to go out and do my midnight meditation in a lonely place which I had to do every month under the new moon. Once I went to a temple outside of the city. It was a walking distance of a couple of kilometers. I went with some friend who used to know the temple. I liked this temple very much because it was a very lonely place at the top of a mountain.
My friend who brought me left me there and returned next day. I stayed with the monks although it was very difficult because no one spoke English. Somehow one of the monks understood about my practices. Usually, in the temple, monks used to do morning and evening prayers, in that temple there was no meditation. They saw me doing meditation morning, noon, evening and midnight. After a few days a monk brought me back to the city and took me to a university.
This was one of the most famous universities in Seoul for the study of oriental philosophy. The name of the university is Dong Kuk. When I went there I saw many monks and nuns on the university campus. The monk who was accompanying me brought me to see a professor. When the professor saw me he was very happy. That very professor had studied in Varanasi, India at the Benares Hindu University. ???
When the winter came I did not know how to live in the cold climate. I was just running around without the proper clothes and without proper shoes. I had no experience about the winter of cold climates. I was staying wherever I could because there was no Jagrti. In the mean time I got frostbite. My legs were swelling up day by day. One day I went to the bathroom and I fell down and could not get up. There was one brother from England named Pavitram and he was teaching English in Korea at that time. I was living with him. I called him and he came and put me on the bed. I had severe pain in both legs. After this I got some treatment, but I did not get better.
Pavitram was a very good margii. He prepared my breakfast, lunch and dinner. He took care of me well.
## Baba’s Luminous Bodies
From Korea I went back to Japan and there I got some treatment but my pain never went away. Mostly I was just staying inside as I could not do much. I was staying in Ashagaya, Tokio, with two other Dadas, Dada Artapremananda and Dada Ravindranath from Mexico.
It was a very small room. We used to sleep, cook, eat, meditate and do all our work in the same room, somehow we managed. Outside there was a public toilet we used. I spent a lot of hours in that room.
One day the other two Dadas left to travel and I was left alone in that room. When the Dadas left I felt sad because there was nobody to look after me. I became very sad and lonely. That day I was just lying down trying to relax and covering my face with a shawl. At that moment, several times I heard a sound in the room. The sounds of pots, pans, glasses and dishes were disturbing me. I thought perhaps it was a mouse, but I could not figure it out.
I just uncovered my face so I could go drive away this mouse or rat or whatever it was. When I uncovered my face I was totally shocked and surprised and more. I saw Baba, just standing in the room. I said, “Baba, is that you?” Baba said, “Yes”. I asked Baba, “Baba, when did you come?” Baba said, “You know I was going to eat. I was in Kolkata and I felt your loneliness, so I left my food and I just came here to see you, but when I entered the room I found you lying on the floor. I was hungry so I was just looking for food in your room. I thought after I finished my food I would talk to you.” I felt so surprised. Baba said, “Why do you feel this loneliness, I am always with you?” I started to get up from the bed. Baba said, “Don’t move, don’t get up my son.” I was in a state of mind I cannot explain. I started to cry, since because of my loneliness Baba had to leave his food and come to see me. After a few minutes I closed my eyes and when I opened them Baba had disappeared. I could not understand what had happened. I had read about these occurrences in some magazines of Ananda Marga, but still after many years I could not really understand it.
One day I was talking with Dada Dasarath and Dada told me a very similar story. One day he woke up in the morning and found Baba in his room. While he was trying to get a chair for Baba, Baba vanished. After a few days he went to see Baba and asked Him, “Baba, did you come to visit me?” Baba said, “Yes, I came to see you.” Baba also asked, “What clothes was I wearing?” Dada replied, “You were wearing dhoti, chasma and kurta.” Baba said, “Yes, yes, yes, I came.” Then Dada asked, “Why did you leave?” Baba said, “Well, it was my luminous body, I did not come to eat mango in your house.” I realized then that it was Baba’s luminous body that came to visit me in Japan.
Baba further explained to Dada Dasarath that that same day he had visited 27 places during a half an hour time period. Baba can multiply himself in as many places as he likes. Baba is the most powerful entity, the Almighty, he can do whatever he likes on this planet. This was one of the greatest experiences of my life, that Baba came to visit me in my time of loneliness.
## Dharma Samiiksa, A Spiritual Overhaul
When Dharma Samiiksa started I was traveling in South Korea, it was 1981. Baba’s devotees were flocking from all over the world to India. Dharma Samiiksa was a way of screening devotees and helping them in their spiritual process. Baba was doing a great thing in the history of mankind.
He would see his devotees and tell them their mistakes and how to cure various conditions. Baba was prescribing asanas, diet, and various lifestyle changes for each person who came to see him. He addressed all kinds of physical, mental and spiritual problems and at the same time He gave his spiritual blessing to whoever came for Dharma Samiiksa.
Through Dharma Samiiksa Baba took away samskaras to help one’s spiritual progress. It was a very rare thing in the history of human kind. According to Baba, Dharma Samiiksa was the most epoch making event in the last fifteen thousands years.
When I came to know about the Dharma Samiiksa, I left Korea and went to Japan. I did not have much money to travel. Actually I didn’t have any money, so when I arrived in Japan the only way to get money was to go from door to door begging. Door to door begging is known as Takahatsu in Japan and has been part of Japanese culture for a very long time.
We used to go from house to house explaining to people that we needed money to travel because we were spiritual teachers. The Japanese people always gave us money so we were very fortunate. Luckily I was able to do most of my collections away from the city as I never liked to beg in the city.
I collected some money to travel but in the end I had to take care of the office there because the Dada in charge was gone. Many bills were piling up so instead of proceeding with my travels I had to pay the bills with the money I had collected. Again I went to collect money for my travels but at that same time some people came from India and needed my help. They were not in very good condition and did not have any money so I spent much of the money I had just collected for their food and amenities. I kept begging and begging and I seemed to always run out of the money before I could make buy my ticket to India.
After some time, I found out that Baba was going to stop Dharma Samiiksa. When I heard this I broke down. I thought to myself, “I have collected all this money by begging and now there will be no more Dharma Samiiksa.” Later I received a message that Dharma Samiiksa had officially stopped. At this point it did not matter to me. I had to go and see my Guru whether I would receive Dharma Samiiksa or not. Whatever little money I did have I used to buy a ticket to Thailand. Some devotees from Taiwan sent me some money for the ticket from Thailand to India.
During this time, Baba was requesting that his devotees should bring plants to India. The sectorial secretary of Hong Kong and the sectorial secretary of Manila had told me they would send certain plants to Baba with me when I arrived in Bangkok. In Bangkok one Didi came and gave me a lot of seedlings that the margiis and workers had collected. She gave them to me in plastic bags with all the various plant names and details and told me to take them to Baba.
I brought the plants with me and when I arrived I gave them to Dada Pranavananda. He was very happy because he had now received all of the different plants that Baba had requested. After some time, I came to know that Baba was very upset about the plants I had brought and I did not know why. At first everyone was very happy about the plants I had brought. When everyone found out that Baba was unhappy no one wanted to take responsibility so I was the one who took the blame. Once things got sour everyone changed their tone.
Soon after I sent a message that these plants were given by the Didi from the Manila sector and that they were not my plants. When Baba heard this He announced that he would not accept any more service from the Hong Kong or Manila sector. When I found out about this I felt horrible. I started to cry. I felt I had done no wrong, yet Baba was no longer accepting service from our sector. I immediately called Hong Kong sector and told them that Baba was no longer accepting service from the Hong Kong or Manila sectors. I told them that we needed to try and make Baba happy and that by acquiring the proper plants Baba would become happy again.
I was very unhappy because all of this meant I would not be able to see Baba. This was the rule. I could not even attend RDS without bringing plants. I felt so bad that I had come all the way to India and I had to return without seeing Baba. Every day I was crying because I felt my life had become meaningless. “Baba is everything for me.” I thought. For us sannyasins the only thing in our life is Baba and if Baba rejects us it means we have nothing left, we only live on our Guru. For me it was very painful.
When people came to know of what had happened they sympathized with me but really could not do anything to help me. There was one margii there in the Jodhpur park office who was seeing every day how unhappy I was. One day he finally came to me and said, “Dada, you are very unhappy, you know, I want to remove your unhappiness.” He wanted to take me on a ride to do some sight seeing but I refused. I said, “I don’t want to go a do sight seeing in Kolkata, I came to see Baba.” He said, “No, you are very unhappy, crying all the time, I am not going to leave you.” I said, “No, no, I am not going to go with you.” He was very strong and big so he just picked me up and put me on his motor bike and drove me away. I could not resist or do anything about it because I was a very little and thin guy. I was 110 pounds at the time. He took me here and there and said I would be happy. I went to some of the most important places in Kolkata with him until finally we arrived at Baba’s quarters.
When I arrived at the quarters there was a lot of shouting going on and they were looking for me. Baba had sent some people to look for me because he wanted to see me but they could not find me at Jodhpur Park where I was staying. They were very disturbed because they did not know what had happened to me. They were running around everywhere in town looking for me and reporting to Baba that I was not here or there. Baba was becoming angry. He said, “I want to see my boy.” When I arrived they said “Come, come, Baba is waiting for you.”
I ran as fast as I could to see Baba. You can imagine how I might have felt. I entered Baba’s room and did sastam pranam and then Baba started to talk to me. He said “I know you did not bring these plants and that the German Didi gave you these plants to bring, the German Didi doesn’t know whether a potato grows on a tree or under the ground but you are the son of a farmer. You should know this. Your father is such a good farmer and you don’t see the difference between a plant and a branch. Why did you give me these branches in the place of plants?” I kept mum. To keep silent is the best thing when you do not know how to answer.
I became very happy while Baba was talking with me but there was one other Dada in the room. I didn’t like anybody there in Baba’s room when I was meeting with Baba. In one way I was very happy even though I did not receive Dharma Samiiksa because at least I got to see my Guru. Inside though I wanted something more, so I requested for field contact with Baba.
The next day they took me for a ride and walk with Baba. When I was walking with Baba He said¨ “So tell me, now you are happy? Is now everything OK?” I said, “Yes Baba¨ now I am happy with all things.”
Several days later some devotees arrived with the plants from the Hong Kong and Manila sectors. Baba was very pleased so he removed the service ban, all things were happy once again.
Sometimes the Guru does things which we do not understand. Whenever I was going to Kolkata, Baba always used to tease me. He used to talk to Dada Asiimananda who was in charge of Baba’s plants, “Watch out of Dada Ik, he gave me the branches of a tree one time instead of plants. You see, his father is such a good farmer, yet he doesn’t know anything about plants. He should know what a plant is and what a branch is.” Whenever I would see him he would say this to me.
In the organization I was given the nickname Ik, which stands for Isvarakrsna. I didn’t really like this, but everyone started calling me by this nickname. Eventually, even Baba started calling me Ik! I didn’t like it, but there was nothing I could do.
I felt really bad when Baba said that I didn’t know anything about plants even though I came from a family of farmers and I wondered why Baba kept reminding me of my father even though I was a sannyassin. Honestly speaking, it is true that I had very little interest in plants although I was born in a farmer’s family. I had felt that farming and these things were not for educated people. My elder brother had always said that farming was not for us. He said that farming was for the older generation and that servants were supposed to do these things. Baba wanted to put it clearly in my mind that farming, planting, and agriculture were also important things in life. This is probably the reason he used to tell me about my father. Baba had given many teachings at that time which emphasized the importance of plants, agriculture and master units and had encouraged us to grow our own food. In the end it seemed that Baba actually did my Dharma Samiiksa through creating all these situations so I could be close to Him.
## Plant Story
When I was working in Hong Kong sector I was traveling mostly in Japan, Taiwan and Korea. One time, I was flying from Japan for RDS in India. I was carrying over 1000 plants with me for Baba. Baba had implemented plant quotas at that time. Every worker had to bring 300 plants for departmental quotas and 40 plants for personal quotas to India. There was a departmental quota and personal quota. I was carrying 3 quotas worth of plants, this was to cover my quota and also that of some others.
I came to the airport with these 1050 plants or so and when I arrived at customs they asked me if I had a quarantine certificate. I said, “What is that?” He explained to me I had to get a certificate for the plants. I said, “Ok give it to me.” He said, “You have so many plants, we can't give you a quarantine certificate right away, it will take quite a bit of time.” It came in my mind that if I were to wait for the certificate I would miss the flight. That would have meant not reaching India in time for RDS, so I would miss RDS. If I took the flight, I would have to leave my plants. It was so difficult because if Baba found out I had left 1000 plants in an airport I would have been in big trouble. Baba was so serious about plants that even if one plant died, people would get a serious punishment. I became very, very worried. I couldn't see any solution. If I took the flight, I would not have plants and if I waited for the certificate, I would miss RDS; either way I was going to be in big trouble.
Shortly after I remembered there was another Dada leaving on the next day for RDS. I called my office in Tokyo and told them I was unable to take the plants and asked if Dada Vimalananda could bring them. They agreed and everything was arranged.
From Tokyo I flew to Bangkok, I was a bit worried during the flight. I waited for Dada on Bangkok as he also had a layover there. He did not arrive to Bangkok on that day or the next day. Even the following day after he did not come. I became extremely worried and began to panic. The entire two days I was in Bangkok I was very much disturbed.
I went to town to buy three or four plants, so that when I arrived at RDS I could offer at least these to Baba and tell him that the other plants were coming. The next day I went as usually to the Bangkok airport to look for Dada Vimalananda, soon after I saw him coming and I was so happy. I was so relieved; it was like life came back. I thought to myself, “I am going to bring all these plants, and Baba is going to be very happy.” I approached Dada and asked him about the plants. Dada said that he did not get my plants at the Tokyo Airport. I almost died when he said this. It is not possible to explain how I felt when he said he did not get my plants. It was like murdering 1000 people. For me it was devastating. How was I going to face Baba? It would be very serious if Baba came to know about the mishandling of 1000 plants.
With a heavy heart I took the flight to India, but the whole time my mind was completely vacant. I felt so bad. When I arrived in Kolkata I went to our central office in Jodhpur Park. People were coming and going. Some workers asked me, “Hey, you came from the Hong Kong sector, how many plants did you bring?” I told them that I had only four plants. “Only four plants!”, they said. They were infuriated. They said, “You know Baba has stopped people who did not bring the plants from seeing Him.” I tried to get some plants in Kolkata, but I did not have enough money. I asked some people who had more plants to give me some, but nobody would help, nobody gave me plants. I was very sad and unhappy. I had come all the way from Japan after one year of not seeing Baba and now I could not see him.
At this point, I lost my balance of mind. I was crying inside all the time. I would keep telling myself, “I came all the way from Tokyo and I cannot see Baba. He is everything to me, my mother, father, Guru, teacher, friend and I cannot see him.” It was very painful.
After a few of days I just took my plants and went to Baba's quarters. By chance the door was open and there was no guard. This was unheard of as usually Baba’s house was very well guarded. I opened the gate and walked in. When I went there, Baba's Personal Secretary was also gone so I walked into the room where Baba used to have reporting sessions, just outside of His bedroom. I just put the plants outside of Baba's bedroom door. I then went close to where Baba's coat was lying. I sat near his coat. Baba used to sit on that coat during reporting. I became relaxed. I thought, “Well I did not see Baba, but minimum I at least came to Baba's house.” So I felt quite happy.
In the meantime, Dada Ramananda, who was Baba's Personal Assistant, came in. “How are you?” He asked. I told him I was fine. “How many plants did you bring?” I became very nervous. I said, “Dada I lost all my plants, and I have only brought four plants.” “Four plants! Get out immediately from here!” He was out of himself. He said, “How did you enter? You can't enter this campus without your quota of 300 plants. Who let you in?” I said, “Dada there was nobody guarding so I just entered.” He again said, “Get out immediately from here. If you don't I am going to call the guards and they will remove you from here.” I said, “Dada, please. I came all this way.” He said, “No, no, If Baba finds out about this I will lose my job, so get out!”
Just then Baba opened the door and came out of His room. He saw the four plants. He started to praise the plants saying, “Such beautiful plants, such wonderful plants.” Meanwhile, Dada Ramananda kicked me a little bit and told me to go and hide under Baba's coat. Baba asked Ramananda, “Who brought these plants? how beautiful are these plants, no one has given me such plants until now.” Dada Ramananda was very nervous. Baba became very mad, and said, “I want to know right now, who brought me these plants.” Dada said, “Hong Kong Sector.” Baba then asked, “Who in Hong Kong sector?” Dada said, “It was Iishvarakrshnananda who brought these plants Baba” Baba said, “Do you see how much he cared, he brought the most beautiful plants, nobody has brought plants like these. You just go and find him wherever he is and call him immediately. No body has brought such plants; send him in my room right now!” Baba went into his room and shut the door. Dada Ramananda called me, “Come out, come out, you go in Baba's room, Baba wants to see you now!” You can imagine my situation, I had come from a complete hell, my mental condition was such. Then I was in Heaven.
I went in to Baba’s room where He was sitting, waiting for me. He asked me a question, “How many times have I helped you directly and indirectly?” I started to think about it. After some time, I came up with a reply and said, “Four times.” Baba then asked me to tell Him which four incidents so I told him. He said it was true, but then added, “But what about today?” I said, “Baba you know you did not help me today.” Baba said, “I did help you today.” I kept telling Him He hadn’t helped me and He kept saying He had. Baba finally said, “If I had not helped you, you would not be in my room right now. There are all these senior Dada's waiting to see me who have brought many more plants than you and still they are not able to enter here. Not only were you able to enter here, you entered the campus and then the quarters, but now you are in my room with me.” I dropped dead then. I said, “Not four times, You have helped me thousands of times, these four times were times when you saved my life.” After that time, I felt that Baba knew everything, my pain, my difficulties and all of my efforts.
Add One Good Person to Society, It Will Have A Great Effect On The Universe
I was posted in the southern city of Taiwan called Koashung City. One day I was not feeling well so I did not go out at all. I had a lot of pain in my leg, this was from the frostbite I had gotten in Korea, the pain remained for many years.
In my seclusion I started to feel upset not only because I had pain in my leg but also because I was distraught as to how we could establish Ananda Marga. At the time there were only a small number of people in Ananda Marga, so many times this question would come in my mind. Each time I told myself I would try very hard to establish Ananda Marga for Baba although only a few people had come to join us so far. At the same time, I started to fear that I would somehow get lost in tamas as there were so many tamasic people in the world. I felt very concerned about this because I knew many good people that had been lost in this way. They were not able to maintain their spirituality in a tamasic society. Many people go to propagate spiritual ideals but get lost. In the old days the tradition was that the monks, sadhus and sages would go to the mountains and the caves and meditate. Modern sadhus, especially Baba’s sannyasins, were not allowed to go to reclusion. Baba not only asked that we seek our own liberation, but that we also serve society. For this reason, we have always been involved with the different elements of society. Thinking about all these things my mind became very negative.
I tried to distract myself by reading some inspiring books so as to have close inner contact with Baba. I began reading an Ananda Marga magazine with some very interesting stories. One story was about a great saint in India named Kabir.
Kabir had this habit of feeding people even though he did not have much money. If anyone would come to him, he would always feed them. He went out of his way to feed the people. While I was reading this story I fell asleep with some sadness but with something deep to think about. During my sleep I saw Baba in a dream.
When Baba appeared I told Him that when I was growing up I had some neighbors whom my parents would not allow us to meet. My parents forbid us from visiting these neighbors or from playing with their children. The reason was very simple, these people were always quarrelling, fighting, arguing, and drinking. Even the small children used to say bad words. My family believed that if I were to be in contact with these people I would also get these bad habits. Despite my family’s wishes I would sometimes escape and play with the neighbors’ children. This was deeply ingrained in my mind so in my dream I began to tell Baba what my family had told me in my childhood. In other words, that I should not come in contact with tamasic people because I would become contaminated by their association. Baba at this moment said, “Yes that is true but you are a good person and it is also true that if the people come in your contact they will become good persons.”
Baba had said that even to inspire one person to be good was great because one good person could inspire many more good persons. Baba said, “If even you add one good person to the universe it will have a great effect on the society.” When Baba told me these things I realized how important it was to cultivate the good in people. After sometime I accepted Baba’s teaching. Baba reminded me how helpful it is to add even one good person to this planet and that this would have a powerful effect on the universe. I felt after this that even if not everyone was coming to Ananda Marga simply by helping to create one good person would be of great help to society.
## Bali to Berlin
I was posted for six months in Bali, Indonesia, from June to November 1987. Eventually I had to leave the country to renew my visa. It was a beautiful place to work at but it was also difficult since Ananda Marga was not registered there. I had to go there as a tourist. Indonesian Government was very tough that time. They did not like any kind of new organization in the country. I had gone there as a trainer for LFTs. There were few margiis and with their help I gave some LFT training to some people there.
In December 1987, I was informed about my new posting in Berlin sector and at the end of the month, I went to India. When I met Baba in private, Baba asked me if liked better Hong Kong sector or Berlin sector. I was posted in Berlin Sector but I had never been there. I had very wrong opinion in my mind about Europe, which is what the Berlin Sector comprises.
I had met and lived with a family in Seoul Korea who used to live in Europe. They were a very good couple. Before marriage the husband was involved with social service activities and his wife was an activist. One day after marriage, they attended a social service meeting. Some European activist friends were very much annoyed with this couple. They used to bother her telling her that how could she have married with someone involved in social service since it was the opposite from activism. Since then I got very wrong opinion about the people in Europe. I always prayed to God and told in my prayer that I never wanted to go to Europe. But My Guru had something else in store for me.
When Baba asked me whether I liked Hong Kong or Berlin Berlin I told him I likes both. Baba said, “What difference does it make for my Ik whether he goes to Hong Kong or Berlin?” Baba was very happy with my answer. I did not want to displease my Guru. I knew, He had decided my posting and He knew what was best for me.
When I went to Europe, my opinion about it changed. I met many nice people. Sometimes we humans form an opinion about something without knowing the complete picture about a place or person.
## Baba Knows
It was the month of March, 1988. I was still waiting in India for my visa for Europe. Dada Shambhushivanada was the sectorial secretary of Berlin Sector. He Asked me to present myself on his behalf for the RDS, (reporting session), because he had been staying in India working in the Ananda Marga world wide education project called Gurukul. He had not been in Europe for some months. I didn’t have any idea about the Berlin Sector. He gave me a report, some herbs and herbal oils that had been produced in the Master Unit of Finland.
When reporting came the first thing Baba said was, “What product have you brought from the Finland Master Unit? I said to Baba that I had brought the herbs and herbal oil from there. Baba told me that that was not actually from the Master Unit. He said, “This is the product of the private farm of a Margi. A Master Unit is an organizational property. Private property and organizational property should be kept separately.” I told Baba I was sorry.
After reporting I told whole thing to Dada Shambhushivananda. He said there were some margiis from Finland then visiting India. I went to meet Lalitesha and Sumitra from Finland who had brought the herb products from Finland. Lalitesha and Sumitra had a farm called Frantsilla Yurtitilla in Finland. I narrated whole story to Lalitesha and Sumitra. I requested both of them if they could donate 6 acres of land to create master unit. They agreed to donate and made a contract in paper. Unfortunately, the land was never given to the organization because it belonged to Sumitra's parents and they refused to donate it.
## Europe
When I went to Europe for the first time, it was April 1988. First I visited Germany, then Switzerland and then Italy. My Italian visa ended and from there I went to Denmark, Sweden and Finland. I stayed in Finland from around August, 1988 to January, 1990 and I actually became a legal resident of this country.
## Communist Russia
Once I went to St. Petersburg, still called Leningrad then. The communist rule was still there. I stayed there for about 10 days during which I met a lot of people. Every day between 20 and 30 people came to visit me in a small room during the day and evening. In the night I used to stay in a Hotel. People were very curious about meditation there. I was surprised to know that communist had failed to kill the human spirit. I had to be very careful from the KGB.
Once I asked how were they all able to visit me during working hours. They said it did not matter whether they worked 5 hours or 50 hours, they were going to get same amount of money. Most people were escaping from work. They had a way to set an agreement with their superiors if they wanted to get away for some time. Life was hard there. I went to see the shops and only very few things were available for the common people.
A brother that was accompanying me took some pictures of our travels there which I later sent to our sectorial office. Dada Giridevananda published the pictures in a newsletter which was sent to India. In the end it was given to Baba. When baba saw my pictures in the newsletter with Russian people, He said that all the newsletters that had been printed had to be destroyed. Baba said, “Ik has been risking his life all this time. Now you all have put him in more danger.” My Guru loved me so much.
He wanted to raise awareness about protecting people who were doing work in Communist countries.
## Back in the USSR
At the end of 1988, there was earthquake in Armenia. I was in Copenhagen at the time for treatment. Dada Tadbhavananda had arranged treatment for me with a homeopathic doctor. I was coughing very badly and had pain. I had lost lot of weight. I was barely 70 pounds. I had been suffering from this for two years already. I was in very bad shape.
I got a phone call and was informed that Baba was taking report every three hours about the earthquake in Armenia. He wanted to know whether AMURT had arrived or Not to help people. I was the AMURT secretary of Berlin Sector. I wanted to go to Russia, but Dada Tadbhavananda, seeing my health situation, objected. In the end I persuaded him and went to Moscow. It was winter. It was a very dangerous game to play. I had to meet with a Russian margii in a subway. We had some kind of sign and code words to meet each other. I met him in the subway and went to his house and instructed him about the relief work that could be done in Armenia. I could not do the work directly as Ananda Marga was being persecuted by Communist Russia.
I went to India and reported to Baba. He was very pleased to know that I completed my mission. Baba wanted us to go immediately to help people wherever there was a man made or natural calamity.
## Norwegian Birds
I was in Norway and staying in Vasudeva's house for few days before I went to India. We had a margii neighbor, Dayal and his children had some birds. The kids used to have fun with birds.
One day I was talking with children and I told them that usually, when I would go t India, I would take a gift for Baba. The children said they too would like to send a gift to Baba but they had nothing to give. I thought about the birds and when I mentioned this to the kids they were very happy to send them as a present for Baba. Baba loves plants, birds, animals. I promised them them that I would take all precaution with the birds. I brought birds from Norway to India for Baba.
## Baba Acknowledges my Small Gift
When I went to Russia in 1989, I wanted to bring some gift from Russia for Baba. I wanted to find something which is very useful for my Guru. I went to a shop and bought a Russian walking stick. I thought it would be very useful for Him. I brought that walking stick from Russia and gave it to Baba.
One day I went to see Baba in private. He said, “Look! I am universal Man.” He started saying that He was wearing a shirt given by some margii from another country and then said His shoes were also from another country and then he said, “And a walking stick from Russia.” He was letting me know Baba had not forgotten my small gift.
## Badly Sick
In the month of November 1990, I received a letter from Helsinki. At the time I was stating in Yurtitilla Lalitesha and Sumitra’s farm in Hamenkyoro, Finland. My doctor had found some something in the results from my tests and I was asked to visit the hospital immediately. I went to hospital and had X-rays of my Lungs taken. When the doctor saw my lungs, he was shocked. He told me that if I had delayed for a week more, there would have been no cure for me. Death would have been certain. There were many holes in my lungs, they had been badly damaged by tuberculosis bacteria. He told me that if I followed his instructions I would survive.
For the past two years I had been coughing badly, I had fever, pain and I was losing weight. I was barely 70 pounds. I had many X-rays taken before in different countries and I had visited many doctors but no one found tuberculosis. They kept giving me medication for coughing.
After following the doctor’s instructions, I started to recover rapidly. As part of my treatment I was not allow to travel.
One day I got a call from India, I was told that Baba had stopped taking reports since I was not there. Baba wanted to know why I was not present in RDS. Everybody was keeping mum and He started punishing people when no answers came. The third day baba became furious, He wanted to know why I was not there. He was told by the central administration that I was badly sick. Baba then stopped the punishments. He was showing me that he cared about me.
## Baba Leaves
In the month of January 1990, I got Call from Dada Shambhushivananda. He wanted me to assist him with some work in Germany so I went there. He was still working fulltime in Gurukul, the Ananda Marga education project, so he was spending most of his time in the central office in India although he was officially the sectorial secretary of the Berlin Sector.
I was trying to solve some of the serious legal problems in Germany and doing some other work for the sector. On top of that Baba gave me additional duty of sevadal, which put me in charge of the LFT trainings of the Berlin Sector. I had triple duty.
I was still recovering from my health problem and was very busy. Baba had made it clear that He wanted me to go to India to attend RDS. I had no idea that he would leave His physical body that same year. I had so much work in Europe that I did not want to go to India for RDS. But then general secretary, Sarvatmananda, persuaded me to go to India. He told me that Baba wanted me to be there in Kolkata.
That year I ended up going 3 or 4 times to India. It was important thing of me. To a disciple, it is a great thing to be with the Guru in last phase of the Guru’s life. I was one of the persons who took an oath along with other dadas in the Sevadal reporting in the month of October 1990. Baba talked about many things; Dharmacakra, the invention of the wheel, etc. During this talk Baba asked us if He could depend on us for materializing His mission. We took the oath to accomplish this.
## Life in Varanasi
In 1993 I left Germany due to my health problems and went to Varanasi. was staying in the same center where I had been trained as a monk. At that time there were two other persons also staying at the center trying to improve their health. One of them was Dada Dasarath, he was my examiner when I was trying to become a monk. He was one of the very close disciples of our Guru. He came in contact with the Marga in its very early stage. He was a very strict spiritual practitioner. Although he was over eighty years old he used to get up in the morning at three o'clock and finish his meditation around six in the morning. Then he would still do his evening meditation.
He had a lot of spiritual experiences. Moreover, in the early days of the Marga Baba had given him many occult powers and had given public demonstrations using him as a medium. He could see any event in the Universe at any given time, be it past present or future. He would screen people before they went to see Baba for personal contact to see if they were ready for it. I think that is also why Baba made him our examiner, he could look into us and know weather or not we were ready to become monks.
## I Gave it Only to You
He narrated a very interesting story to me. Once some people came who had a high position in society. When that person went to see him before seeing Baba for personal contact, Dada Dasaratha found out that he was not a suitable candidate. Some of the high officials of the organization intervened and wanted him to recommend this person to be allowed to see Baba. Dada Dasarath refused. He was unsure what to do in this situation because he was being put under pressure by high officials in the organization. In the end he went to see our Guru and told him the problem. Baba was furious. He said that He had given it only to him the duty to see who was fit for personal contact. Baba refused to give a personal interview to this person. Baba had very high principles in life. When he made a rule everybody had to follow it, no one was every above the law.
## A Trip with Baba
Once Baba asked Dada Dasarath to travel with Him. Dada was teaching at a government school and didn’t have time to inform the school he left and accompanied Baba. He even did not tell his own family where he was going or when he would return. He went with Baba and was given some work to do by Him. He was successful with his work but was thinking he could have done it from anywhere and couldn't understand why Baba specifically wanted him to go with Him.
After one year, Baba asked him if he remembered what day was it. He said he didn’t. Baba said, “This is the day we left on that journey together last year. Do you know why I took you with me?” He said, “No Baba.” Baba told him, “I took you to save your life. You were going to die of food poisoning on that very same day and that's why I took you with me.”
When he told me this story he became very emotional. Baba had saved his life several times and this was one of them.
## The Story of the 3 Taraka Brahmas
When Dada Dasarath came to see me in the early morning at six o'clock after his early meditation, almost every day he would tell me that he didn't wish to live anymore. He had a high stress hernia so he used to feel a great deal of pain, as if he were suffocating. I used to listen to him complain about his suffering.
One day, he started again talking in this manner and I told him, “Dada, there is only one person on the planet who I know saw the three Taraka Brahmas, Lord Shiva, Lord Krishna and Shrii Shrii Anandamurti. When I told him this he completely forgot about his sickness and began narrating his experience about how Baba had taken his mind back 7000 years and showed him Lord Shiva meditating. Then Baba took his mand 3500 into the past from today and Baba showed him Lord Krishna when he was King of Mathura. And he used to get lost explaining these stories. He had many experiences with Baba.
## Different Kinds of Sweetness
Once, Dada Dasarath went for a walk with Baba. He was planning to go later that day to a festivity where a lot of sweets were going to be served.
After the walk Dada Dasarath and Baba went to Baba’s house. Baba laid down on the bed and Dada started to serve his Guru by massaging His feet. When he was massaging he went into bliss and completely lost track of time, he stayed like that for 4 hours. He was in Dhyan, one of the highest meditative states and he had no idea of how much time had passed. Around eleven pm Baba reminded Dada Dasarath about his appointment. He had actually forgotten. He was in so much bliss that he didn't want to go but Baba forced him to go.
He felt great pain at that time to leave his Guru. He used to love sweets and this particular celebration was given on the occasion of a sweet maker’s son. Most of the people had already arrived by the time he went there. He received a large amount of sweets called Rasgula. He ate some but confessed to me that although he loved sweets the sweetness of the Dhyana was a thousand times sweeter than any food.
## I See My Nephew
After I became a monk I never saw another member of my family again except once. It was in 1991 when my nephew came to visit me in Ananda Nagar. He came to see me because his father, who was my eldest brother, had been murdered. When my father came to know about the death of his eldest son, he also collapsed and died. Later that day, my second eldest brother also died. So on the same day my father and two elder brothers passed away.
A margii saw this on the newspapers and went to my village. Margiis had never visited my village so my family members knew nothing about them. They explained that they were my friends and wanted to help them in any way they could. My family refused to accept help, they had enough of everything. They had a hundred acres of land, cows, water buffaloes and all my nephews had jobs. The one thing they needed was guardianship, so my eldest nephew wanted to meet me and request this from me. The margiis told him that I was not in India but in Europe at that time but that maybe I would go to Ananda Nagar at the end of that December. It was not very long till then, just a few weeks.
So my nephew went to see me when the time came. I arrived in December to Ananda Nagar and someone told me what had happened with my family and that my nephew was there looking for me. First of all, when I heard the story I thought it was some kind of hoax to bring me back home. Secondly, my Guru had left his physical body from this earth the year before, so I had to complete his work. I was not interested in meeting my nephew. Nevertheless, pressure was built up from the margiis and some Acharyas. I met with my nephew.
The last time I had seen him was in 1971, when he was eight years old. I was meeting him after twenty years so it was very difficult to recognize him because there were many young boys surrounding him. Finally, I called his name looking at his face and he nodded his head.
He told me, “Uncle come back home. I lost both my father and grandfather, we have no one to guide us.” The second thing he told me was that due to these three deaths in the family my mother would also die soon. He said that she was always calling my name and wanted to see me somehow. I told him, “I am a monk. I am not supposed to go back home. Moreover, my Guru left his physical body and I have accepted the whole world as a global family. So there are millions of people suffering in the world and I have to remove their suffering as much as I can.”
He had one more problem and for this I recommended a lawyer whom I knew and who was also a margii.
Sometimes we have to make small sacrifices for the greater good. I was not heartless, but I knew that if I went home, my family would never allow me to leave again. Moreover, this would have been a violation of the rule of avadhutas, which prohibits to have any kind of relation with one’s own family.
